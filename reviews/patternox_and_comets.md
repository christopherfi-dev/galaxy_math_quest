## A8. Errors found in the drafts

I recomputed these in Python (standard library). The script filled each puzzle with a step of 5 and compared it with the key. [Source for the text; the checks are mine]

1. **Task 4, puzzle 2:** "__, 10, __, 25" has 2 blanks. The key gives 3 numbers, "5, 15, 20". And 5, 10, 15, 25 does not count by 5s. No step-of-5 sequence fits the puzzle as written.
2. **Task 4, puzzle 7:** "__, __, __, 30" has 3 blanks. The key gives 5 numbers, "5, 10, 15, 20, 25". The fitting answer is 15, 20, 25.
3. The other 8 puzzles match their keys. Puzzle 10 needs 0 as an answer ("__, 5, 10, __, 20").
4. **Patterns - Quest 2, boss example:** "You have 1000 stardust. You need to divide it equally among 5 space pirates. After giving each pirate their share, you multiply the remaining stardust by 2. Subtract 200 from this total." 1000 ÷ 5 = 200 each. Nothing remains. 0 × 2 − 200 = −200. The problem is broken, and a negative answer is above Grade 3.
5. **Patterns - Quest 1, Plan B boss:** "Sequence B: 5, 10, _, _, 50". Two blanks cannot take a steady count from 10 to 50. The puzzle is unclear.
6. **Patterns - Quest 1, Plan A boss, the printed maze:** the text maze starts "2-4-6-8-X-10-12-14". As pasted it is not a usable maze.
7. **Plan A, code example:** (4 × 8) × 25 = 800 is right. But "every second number" and "every fifth number" are not explained well enough for a child to reach it.

Above typical Grade 3, for whoever takes these further: a Fibonacci sequence (Plan A, Sea Monster C); "Create a formula" (Task 3, question 5); fractions and decimals (Quest 4 and the Advanced quests, which the doc itself labels Grade 4).
