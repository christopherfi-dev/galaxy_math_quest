role="main" tabindex="0">2-1 - Fuel Calculation
Objective:
Attention, Space Cadets! We're flying far, but our fuel is limited. Plan a smart route and decide where to refuel. Use your add/subtract skills for fuel math.
Click here for your ----- > Introduction Video
Mission Steps:
Read Map: We've got paths on a map, each cut into 5 legs max. Check the map here.
Starting Fuel: You start with 100 liters. Different legs use different amounts.
Pick Stop: Choose a smart place to refuel. Factor in future legs' fuel needs.
Do Math: Add and subtract fuel for each leg. Calculate fuel needs after stops.
What You'll Need:
Map with paths and fuel use numbers.
Spreadsheet for your answers. (Go find it in Google Classroom).
What You'll Learn:
Practical addition and subtraction.
Smart route planning.
Performance Assessment:
We look at your fuel math and route choice. Better plans mean less fuel waste.
Mission Conclusion:
Great job, Space Cadets! Your smart fuel math gets us further. Share your work with your teacher for a new card.
