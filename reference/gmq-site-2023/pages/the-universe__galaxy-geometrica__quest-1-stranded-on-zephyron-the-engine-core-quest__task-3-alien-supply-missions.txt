role="main" tabindex="0">Task 3:
The Supply Run Gauntlet
Greetings, Commanders -
We're getting closer to acquiring our much-needed Weapo, but the aliens have one more task for us. They've asked us to make a supply run to three different outposts in their territory. There's a catch: we must avoid mountains and forests, which are home to deadly creatures and carnivorous plants.
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Objective:
Your mission is to chart a safe path to each of the three outposts from our starting supply zone. You must use coordinate points to guide us and ensure we steer clear of the mountains and forests.
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
Grade 5 -
LL2 - Objects on a coordinate plane
LL3 - Graph points on a coordinate plane
Materials Needed:
Alien Territory Map with Coordinate Grid: A map displaying our starting point, the locations of the outposts, mountains, and forests.
Marker or Highlighter: To trace out and mark your planned routes on the map.
A sheet of paper: You need to write down the directions. (For examples - go to (4,1). Then go to (3.5). Etc.)
Mission Steps:
Identify Obstacles: Locate the mountains and forests on the map and note their coordinate ranges.
Plot the Outposts: Identify the coordinate points for each of the three outposts.
Chart the Paths: Use straight lines and coordinate points to chart paths from our starting location to each outpost. Remember, the paths must avoid crossing mountains and forests.
Record Your Paths: Write down the coordinate points that make up each path to guide us on our journey.
Submit your evidence to the aliens: Take photos of what you did. Upload it to Google Drive. Make a video reflecting on what you did and how you figured out where to go. Upload that, too.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Use coordinate grids to plan routes and navigate real-world spaces.
Apply logical reasoning to avoid obstacles in path planning.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Navigate using coordinate grids accurately.
Make strategic choices to avoid obstacles and hazards.
Mission Conclusion:
Fantastic work, Tactical Planners of Zephyron! Your planning ensures that we can make these critical supply runs while avoiding all hazards. This is a crucial step in not only securing the Engine Core but also in building trust with our alien hosts. Your skills in coordinate-based navigation and strategic planning are becoming invaluable to this mission. GO UPGRADE YOUR ENGINE CORE!
