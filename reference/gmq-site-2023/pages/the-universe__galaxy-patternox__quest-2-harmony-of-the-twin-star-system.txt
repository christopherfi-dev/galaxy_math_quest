Quest #2 - "Harmony of the Twin Star System"
Attention, Galactic Mathematicians! You're now entering the "Twin Star System" of Multiplica and Dividia. Their stability depends on a delicate balance between multiplication and division. You'll have to use all your maths powers to keep them in perfect harmony. This isn't just math - it's a quest to save a system!
