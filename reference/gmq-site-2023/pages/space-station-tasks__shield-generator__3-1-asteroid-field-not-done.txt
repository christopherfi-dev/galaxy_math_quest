role="main" tabindex="0">3-1 - Asteroid Field
Objective:
Space Navigators, we need to fly through a field full of asteroids! Your job is to find which path has fewer asteroids. Count the rocks on each path and pick the one with the least.
Mission Steps:
Look at the Grid: It shows a field of space rocks.
Count Rocks: Rows x Columns = Total rocks.
Find Paths: Three ways through the rocks. Which has fewer rocks?
Pick Path: Use your counts to pick the safest way.
Reflect: Write why you picked this way. Take a picture. Put it in your Portfolio.
Learning Objectives:
Learn to multiply and divide.
Think ahead to solve problems.
Materials Needed:
Go get this sheet with the asteroid field.
Pen and paper.
Performance Assessment:
Did you count right?
Did you use math to pick a safe way?
Did you think and write about it?
Mission Conclusion:
Wow, Space Navigators! You used math to find a safe way through rocks. It’s not just about counting, but how to use it.
Share your work with your teacher and get a new card (or choose your ship part if you have completed all of the Shield Generator tasks).
