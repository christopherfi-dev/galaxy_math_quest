role="main" tabindex="0">Task 2: Unravel the Temporal Rift Riddle
Greetings, Commanders -
You have successfully fixed the Grand Clock Tower, but our challenges are far from over. Time Quakes are distorting our timelines, wreaking havoc, and jeopardizing our future. We have data on six past quakes. Your mission: Use this data to predict and prepare for the devastating seventh Time Quake, which will destroy time itself!
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Objective:
Use your time-calculation skills to decipher the pattern of Time Quakes and predict the seventh Time Quake, which holds the key to stabilizing time itself.
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
IXL Grade 3 - Practice: RR1, RR2, RR3, RR4
Materials Needed:
Time Quake Data Analysis Worksheet
Pencil and Eraser: For making and correcting your calculations.
Restoration Report A report filled out by you at the end of the task to assess your performance.
Mission Steps:
Analyze Past Data: Use your Time Quake Data Analysis Worksheet to review past Time Quakes' start and end times. Calculate the duration of each and record your findings.
Discern the Pattern: Examine the duration data closely. Can you detect a pattern? Your insight is critical for predicting the seventh Time Quake.
Predict the Seventh Quake: Based on your analysis, predict the start and end times of the seventh Time Quake. We must know when it will occur to deploy our Time Stabilizers effectively.
Strategize: Write down your strategy for handling the seventh Time Quake. Your tactical approach could save Chronosia from collapsing timelines.
Unlock the Next Quest: Submit your worksheet and strategy to advance to your next mission. Capture photos of each and upload them to Google Drive.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Calculate the duration between two given times.
Utilize pattern recognition in a practical, real-world scenario.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
The accuracy of your duration calculations.
The validity of your pattern recognition and subsequent predictions.
Mission Conclusion:
Excellent work, Timekeepers! Your mastery over time calculations has given us the necessary information to anticipate and neutralize the seventh Time Quake. Chronosia’s timelines are stabilizing, and a time calamity has been averted. Prepare yourselves; new adventures await!
