role="main" tabindex="0">Level 1 - Food Shopping Lists
Welcome back, commanders! As we prepare for our next mission, we need to stock up our spacecraft with the right food supplies. We have a long list of options, but we need to make sure we are choosing the right mix to keep us healthy and make the food last. Your task is to categorize these foods in a meaningful way.
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Objective:
Galactic Commanders, you have an important job! We need to sort food for our year-long space trip. Your work will help us pick the right foods for our journey.
Materials Needed:
Food Supplies Data Sheet: A paper that tells you about 20-25 foods, how long they last, and how good they are for you.
Categorization Worksheet: A paper where you'll sort the foods into different groups.
Mission Steps:
Round 1: Sorting by How Long Food Lasts
Read the Supplies: Go through the Food Supplies Data Sheet.
Rearrange: On a new section of your Categorization Worksheet, re-sort the foods based on their shelf life (e.g., short-term, medium-term, long-term).
Analyze: Observe which types of food have longer shelf lives and note down your observations.
Round 2: Sorting by How Good the Food is for You
Study Supplies: Go back to the Food Supplies Data Sheet one more time.
Prioritize: On another section of your Categorization Worksheet, classify the foods based on their nutritional value (e.g., high protein, low fat, high fiber).
Connect: Identify any foods that are consistently in the same categories across all three rounds.
Round 3 has two options. A guided one, and a more challenging, creative one.
Challenging, creative, independent choice -
Round 3a: Sort by Student Choice
Study Supplies: Review the Food Supplies Data Sheet one more time.
Innovate: Choose your own categorization scheme. Maybe it's by texture, color,  type of food, or the planet where the food originates.
Connect: Identify any foods that you would rate high in the same categories across all three rounds.
Guided choice -
Round 3b: Sort by Food Type
Read the Supplies: Go through the Food Supplies Data Sheet.
Categorize: On your Categorization Worksheet, sort the foods into columns based on their type (e.g., protein, fruits, vegetables, etc.).
Reflect: Note down any insights or patterns you see, such as the most frequent type of food.
Final Task:
Based on your categorization, which foods should we definitely take with us?
Write them down.
Make a video explaining why these are the best foods. Refer to your charts to guide your words.
Upload the video to your Google Drive.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Understand how categorization can affect resource allocation.
Analyze the food supplies based on multiple attributes: type, shelf life, and nutritional value.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Correctly categorize all food items in each round.
Make logical connections between the different categorization methods.
Mission Conclusion:
Well done, Commanders! Your skills in resource categorization ensure that the mission to Planet Nourish will be a success. Submit your completed Categorization Worksheets to your teacher and await further instructions for your next mission.
