role="main" tabindex="0">Task 1 - Architectural Origami
Greetings, Space Architects -
🚀 Prepare to embark on an exciting journey to Xylophora, a newly discovered planet. Here, your task is to design and calculate the area of crucial structures for our new colony. Ready to put your mathematical skills to the test?
Objective:
Design and calculate the area of four types of buildings (Home, Hospital, Food Store, Solar Factory) using geometric blocks, and explain your architectural choices.
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
IXL Grade 3 - Practice: MM8.
Materials Needed:
Magnetic geometric blocks (squares, triangles).
The side of each square = 5 meters
The height of the tallest triangles = 10 meters.
The height of the smallest triangle = 4 meters.
Two of the middle-sized triangles = one square
Calculation sheet for area computation.
Design explanation sheet.
Mission Steps:
Design Buildings: Use geometric blocks to design each building creatively. You need to create the blueprint for 4 types of buildings that our colony will need to survive - a house, a hospital, a food store, and a solar factory.
Explain Your Design:  On a separate sheet, describe why you chose each design.
Calculate Areas: Determine the surface area of each building using your mathematical skills. Calculate the area of each shape that you used....and then add them all together.
Record Your Work: Show all your calculations and designs clearly. AND TAKE A PHOTO AND SHARE IT WITH MS. NUI
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Understand and apply the concept of area in designing structures.
Creatively use geometric shapes in architecture.
Articulate the reasoning behind your design choices.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Accuracy in area calculations.
Creativity and functionality in building design.
Clarity and thoughtfulness in explaining your designs.
Mission Conclusion:
Congratulations, Space Architects! Your innovative designs and precise calculations are paving the way for our new colony on Xylophora. Your skills in geometry and creativity are truly out of this world. Get ready for your next architectural adventure! 🚀🌟
