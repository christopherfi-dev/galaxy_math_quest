role="main" tabindex="0">2-2 - Coordinate Plotting
Objective: Space Cadets, our Navigation System's key part is lost. Retrieve it through a star map with math challenges. Get your add/subtract skills ready.
Mission Steps:
Examine Map: Look at the star map. It's a grid with different space locations.
Decode Points: Each grid point is an add/subtract problem. For example, instead of (5,3), you'll see (2+3, 5-2).
Solve Math: Find real coordinates by solving. For example, translate (2+3, 5-2) to (5,3).
Draw Path: Follow instructions like "Go to (3+1, 4+2)." Plot a safe course through stars.
Navigate: Use your path to find the lost part. Click HERE to guide the ship.
What You'll Learn:
Spatial addition and subtraction.
Math decoding for strategic planning.
Performance Assessment:
Your ability to decipher coordinates, solve addition and subtraction problems, and plot a safe path will be assessed.
Accuracy and efficiency in guiding the ship are key to the mission's success.
Mission Conclusion:
Great job! You found the lost part using math and strategy. Share your map with the teacher and prep for the next mission.
