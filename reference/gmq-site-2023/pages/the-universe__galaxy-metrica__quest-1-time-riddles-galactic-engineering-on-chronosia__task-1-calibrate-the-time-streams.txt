role="main" tabindex="0">Task 1 -
Calibrate the Time Streams
Greetings, Commanders -
The grand clock tower in our beloved Chronosia is causing chaos by malfunctioning! The day-night cycle is disrupted, and our very sense of time is at risk. This is a mission only the most capable Timekeepers can handle. Will you restore order to our days and nights?
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Objective:
Fix the Chronosian clock tower by solving various time puzzles to restore the day-night cycle.
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
Clock Flashcards: Practice until mastery.
IXL Grade 3 - Practice: QQ1 - QQ3.
Materials Needed:
Chronosia Clock Tower Checklist: A sheet filled with malfunctioning clocks showing both plausible and impossible times.
Pencil and Eraser: For making and correcting your calculations.
Restoration Report:  A report filled out by you at the end of the task to assess your performance.
The Broken Clocks: This is where you go to see the broken clocks.
Mission Steps:
Identify the Problem: Print or view the "Chronosia Clock Tower" checklist. Notice that the times shown are distorted—some are even impossible times like 14:75.
Fix the Clocks: Use your prerequisite skills in time-telling to correct the times shown on the worksheet.
Check the Gears: For each corrected time, mark a checkbox on the worksheet to signify unlocking a gear in the clock tower.
Restoration Report: Complete the "Clock Tower Fixed!" form, summarizing your actions and reasoning in solving this crisis.
Unlock the Next Quest: Submit your completed worksheet and restoration report to advance to your next mission. Take photos of each and upload to Google Drive.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Accurately read and correct times on both analog and digital clocks.
Understand the 24-hour and 12-hour time systems and convert between them.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Your accuracy in reading and correcting times.
Your completion and explanation in the "Clock Tower Fixed!" form.
Mission Conclusion:
Well done, Timekeepers of Chronosia! Your skill and diligence have restored order to our days and nights. The clock tower is functioning normally, and all is well in Chronosia—thanks to you. Brace yourselves; another mission is on the horizon!
