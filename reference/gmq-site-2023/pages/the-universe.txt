role="main" tabindex="0">The Universe
Welcome, brave space explorers, to the exciting world of "The Universe." Dive into a math-filled cosmos where every galaxy promises thrilling adventures and puzzles. Each galaxy is like a treasure chest, packed with mysteries and fun facts, waiting for you to discover.
In the precise lands of Galaxy Metrica, stars and planets sit on a giant space grid. Zip your spaceship through this galaxy, guessing and measuring distances. Every space inch and light-year will make you think about size, weight, and even how hot or cold things are. Soon, you'll be a super space captain, quickly changing units and doing smart math to lead the way.
Galaxy Geometrica  is the shiny center of our Universe. It's filled with awesome shapes everywhere you look! Fly from flat shape worlds to cool 3D places, seeing how space and shapes work together. Your job? Learn about shapes and play with them to see how they fit together.
Over in Galaxy Databa, it's like a huge puzzle of space info. Be a space detective, turning loads of facts into easy-to-read charts and pictures. Spotting patterns and clues, you'll figure out the galaxy's big picture, finding cool secrets hidden in the data.
Next up, Galaxy Numerix is a place where number riddles are everywhere! Travel around, solving math mysteries using adding, taking away, times tables, and sharing. You'll see the cool tricks numbers can do, and find out their magic powers.
Get ready for Galaxy Patternox a world where patterns come to life! Spot the patterns and figure out how they work. Your smart thinking will help you solve tricky puzzles and see the special beat of this number dance.
URGENT UPDATE - A series of Cosmic Coding Comets have been detected entering the learning galaxy.! These comets have blocks of super old code wisdom from the start of the universe. Young explorers, like you, need to crack these codes and use their power. This comet energy will fix the Heart of the Learning Station, giving us more tools and fun for our space adventure.
Every galaxy is a special math journey, just for you. Set your spaceship's map, get ready, and jump into the fun, space explorers!
