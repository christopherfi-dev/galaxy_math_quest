role="main" tabindex="0">Task 2:  Efficient Use of Alien Resources
Greetings, Resource Managers of Xylophora!
🌌 Welcome to your next challenge, where things get a bit wild and 'out of this world'. Get ready to make the most of a bizarre opportunity! We've stumbled upon a colossal, alien creature—a gentle giant of Xylophora, now resting in eternal slumber. Its enormous, leathery skin stretches across fields, a mix of vibrant colors and strange, rubbery textures.
Objective:
It's oddly fascinating, and incredibly, it's the perfect building material for our new colony! Your task: creatively use this alien skin to construct vital buildings for our community.
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
IXL Grade 3 - Practice: MM12.
Materials Needed:
Resource Map: A detailed layout of the giant alien creature's skin, indicating available resources.
Calculation Sheets: For determining how much area each resource covers.
Building Plan Sheets: To outline how many of each building type you can construct with the available resources.
Mission Steps:
Study the Resource Map: Understand the total area available from the alien skin.
Calculate Resource Utilization: Determine how many square meters each resource covers.
Plan Your Buildings: Decide how many Homes, Hospitals, Food Stores, and Solar Factories you can build using all the resources.
Record and Explain: Show your calculations and explain how you maximized resource use. Show all your calculations and designs clearly. AND TAKE A PHOTO AND SHARE IT WITH MS. NUI
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Precision in calculating the total area of available resources.
Effectiveness in resource allocation for building construction.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Accuracy in area calculations.
Creativity and functionality in building design.
Clarity and thoughtfulness in explaining your designs.
Mission Conclusion:
Well done! You've shown exceptional skill in managing Xylophora's unique resources. Your strategic planning has set the foundation for a thriving colony. Prepare for your next task in building our new world! 🚀🌟
