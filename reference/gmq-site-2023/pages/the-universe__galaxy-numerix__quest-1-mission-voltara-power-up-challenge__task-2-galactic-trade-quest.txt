role="main" tabindex="0">Task 2 - Galactic Trade Quest
Mission Briefing:
Hey, Space Commanders! 🚀 Gather 'round, we've got an urgent message from the Galactic Council. We've just left Planet Voltara, and they have sent out a galaxy-wide SOS! They're low on essential supplies, and guess what, they're willing to trade us an UPGRADE POWER MODULE for it.
Objective:
Your mission is to set up trade routes with different planets and make the most fuel tokens. But, you'll have to round numbers for your fuel and profits!
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
Grade 3 -
C3 - Round to the nearest ten or hundred in a table
C4 - Rounding puzzles
Materials Needed:
Trade Data Sheet: Lists what you can trade at each planet for fuel tokens.
Fuel Tokens: You start with 50 fuel tokens. Each token lets you travel 1 light-year.
Profit Sheet: Keep track of how many fuel tokens you spend and how much profit you make.
A Deck of Scenario Cards - For rounds 2-4, these will help you see how random events can ruin the best-planned routes.
Mission Steps:
Stellar Trade Data: Look at your Trade Data Sheet. How many Galactic Gems can you earn at each planet?
Plan Your Fuel: Use the Trade Data Sheet to see where you can go. Try to use as many of your fuel tokens as possible.
Execute Trades: Pick the planets you’ll visit. Use your Profit Sheet to figure out  your profits in Galactic Gems.
Profit Tracking: Write down how much money you made. You'll use this for your next trip, if you dare to take one.
Final Boss Challenge -
After your first trades are finished, plan your next trip.
You can buy 1 Fuel Token for 20 Galactic Gems. So if you have 800 Galactic Gems, you can buy 40 fuel tokens.
After you have planned out your trip, DRAW A SCENARIO CARD.
Adjust your plans accordingly.
Go make Galactic Gems!
Record your new profits and repeat. You can do a total of 3 trips.
Take a photo of your work. Upload it to Google Drive.
Make a video reflecting on your trades. What scenarios did you face? How were your trades? What choices did you make?
The student with the most Galactic Gems wins a 'Lucky Draw' ship part!
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Understand the concept of rounding and its real-world applications in resource management.
Develop strategic planning skills by optimizing trade routes for maximum profit.
Grasp the impact of external factors (scenario cards) on economic activities and plans.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Accurately round numbers for fuel tokens and profits, adhering to the rules set in the quest.
Make efficient trade routes by balancing fuel token costs and profits in Galactic Gems.
Adapt to external changes by effectively using scenario cards to alter strategies without compromising profits.
Mission Conclusion:
Congratulations, Space Commanders! 🚀 By successfully navigating through trade routes and unforeseen scenarios, you've not only boosted Planet Voltara's supplies but also mastered important mathematical and strategic skills. Your Galactic Gems will go a long way in procuring advanced ship parts, and who knows, maybe one day you'll sit on the Galactic Council. Till then, keep your rockets ready and your minds sharper!
