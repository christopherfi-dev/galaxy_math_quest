Earth Needs Your Help!
It is the year 2024, Earth has a big problem. Our cities are too crowded, the oceans are getting dirty, and it's hard to grow food. We need a new place to live!
People all over the world started wishing for a new home among the stars. They began "Project Second Home" to build spaceships and explore far-away galaxies.
The most important place for this project is the Kepler International Space Station (KIS Station). Lots of smart astronauts and scientists work there. But guess what? Kids like you are the real heroes! Young minds are full of great ideas that adults sometimes don't even think of.
When you arrive at KIS Station, you see a mess! The spaceships are broken. They need fixing and creative ideas to fly through space. Hidden around the station are special parts to build them back up.
If you can fix the ships, you'll get to explore amazing galaxies:
Galaxy Metrica: Where numbers dance and play!
Galaxy Geometrica: A universe of wild shapes!
Galaxy Databa: A maze bursting with cosmic facts!
Galaxy Numerix: Dive into number riddles!
Galaxy Patternox: Navigate the ever-changing patterns!
Your mission is to use your awesome ideas to complete challenges, find parts, fix spaceships, explore five galaxies, defeat aliens, study comets, and find the perfect new home for Earth.
This is a huge space adventure, and brave heroes like you are needed! Earth is counting on you.
Are you ready for this special mission? Let's blast off!
