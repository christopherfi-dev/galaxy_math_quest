role="main" tabindex="0">Task 1 - Mission Voltara: Power-Up Challenge
Welcome back, commanders! We have been asked to join Voltara's Energy Council!  As Voltara's Energy Council members, we have a big job today. Our planet needs energy to keep the lights on and machines running. We have to give energy to homes, stores, and factories. But remember, we only have so much energy to share!
Objective:
Your job is to be an energy helper. You must decide how much energy goes to homes, stores, and factories on Planet Voltara.
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
Grade 3 -
C1 - Round to the nearest ten or hundred using a number line
C2 - Round to the nearest ten or hundred
Materials Needed:
City List: This paper tells you what each part of our planet needs for energy.
Energy Count Sheet: This paper shows you how much energy we have to give away. It tells you in 1s, 10s, 100s, and 1000s.
City Map: This is a map that shows you where everything is located.
City Map Key: This tells you what the symbols on the map mean.
Energy Resource: This is how much energy you have available
Available Blocks:
150 of '1s' = 150
70 of '10s' = 700
8 of '100s' = 300
Total: 1650 energy units available
Mission Steps:
Round 1: Make a Plan
Look at the List: What do the homes, stores, and factories need?
Think: Write down on your Energy Count Sheet how you will give out the energy.
Round 2: Do the Plan
Go to the Map: Put the energy where you planned on the computer map.
Check: Make sure you didn’t give away too much energy.
Round 3: What If Something Changes?
Pick a Scenario card that says what changes.
Change your map in 5 minutes based on what the card says.
Pick two more Scenario cards that say what changes need to happen.
Decide which change is more important and fix your map.
Pick a final Scenario card that says what changes and make those changes, too.
Final Task:
Take a photo of everything.
Upload it to Google Drive.
Make a video reflecting on the choices you made and why you made them. Is you city a great place to live by the end of the task?
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Understand place values in resource allocation.
Evaluate importance and prioritize sectors based on energy demands.
Adapt to unforeseen scenarios effectively.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Distribute energy with accurate understanding of place values.
Balance energy allocation efficiently across all sectors.
Rapidly adapt to changing scenarios without wasting resources.
Mission Conclusion:
Well done, Energy Allocators of Voltara! Through careful planning and quick adaptations, you've successfully navigated through the maze of resource allocation. Your well-thought-out decisions ensure that Voltara thrives, and you’ve honed valuable skills in resource management and adaptability. Keep those wits sharp; the council may call upon you again!
