role="main" tabindex="0">Task 4: Timekeeper Cadet's Challenge: Plan the Perfect Day
Welcome, Timekeeper Cadets-
You aced the Temporal Coordinator exam! As a newly minted Trainee, you have a critical task: scheduling a flawless day for Council Members Sara, Tim, Alex, and Zara. Are you ready for this time-bending challenge?
Watch this video for instructions - LINK.
Objective:
Your role is to ensure each Council Member attends events that align with their interests and constraints. Can you achieve temporal harmony?
Before you Begin -
Read through the Events Description Brochure
Do the Timetable quiz to make sure you are ready for this kind of event
Materials Needed:
Council of Timekeepers: Annual Timetable
Events Description Brochure
Character Profiles Sheet
Pencil and Eraser: For making and correcting your calculations.
Mission Steps:
Make sure you have finished the quiz and understand the events.
If you miss any questions, read the brochure carefully and re-do the quiz until you get them all right.
Interests & Constraints: Quickly absorb the character profiles. Take note of their preferences and limitations.
Draft Schedules: Use the timetable to allocate events for Sara, Tim, Alex, and Zara. Keep feasibility in mind.
Optimization: Check your draft carefully. Identify and fix any possible problems.
Special Conditions: Now read the following stipulations that apply to each character. Adapt your schedules accordingly:
Sara needs to stay in Chronosia for an important meeting.
Tim can't travel to other dimensions because he's still a trainee.
Alex requires a chaperone for any risky missions. We have no chaperones.
Zara can't do any events longer than 45 minutes due to her sleeping sickness. She must sleep every 46 minutes.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Calculate the duration between two given times.
Create your own timetable.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Calculate the duration and time between events.
Mission Conclusion:
Great job, young Timekeepers! If you pass this test, you are one step closer to becoming a full-fledged Temporal Coordinator. New adventures and challenges await you!
