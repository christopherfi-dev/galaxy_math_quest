role="main" tabindex="0">Quest #1 - Stranded on Zephyron: The Engine Core Quest
Mission Briefing:
Attention, Space Commanders! Our journey took an unexpected turn. We were visiting Zephyron for an interstellar meetup, but we've crash-landed. The good news? This planet is unlike anything you've seen. The bad news? Our spaceship needs a new Engine Core for repairs.
🚀 Where are we going?
We're off to Planet Zephyron! This place is unlike any other. Imagine walking through fields where the grass sings as you pass by, or encountering mystical creatures like the Levitiger, a feline that floats in the air. 🌾🐯
And get this: Zephyron has a liquid that tastes like different types of smoothies as you drink it. They call it "MoodQuench," and it can go from tasting like mango to a blueberry surprise! 🥭🍇
So prep those spaceship engines, Commanders! We have an Engine Core to acquire and aliens to aid! 🌟
Task 1 - Journey to the Right City
Objective: Identify the city on Zephyron where we can find the advanced Engine Core needed for our ship repairs.
Use the map's scale to locate the city precisely 200 km away from our crash site. This is where our intel suggests the Engine Core can be found.
Task 2 - Find the Engine Core in the City
Objective: Once in the city, locate the facility where the Engine Core is stored.
Use the city map and coordinates to pinpoint the exact location of this vital piece of technology.
Task 3 - Alien Supply Missions
Objective: Win the trust of the local Zephyronians to get access to the Engine Core.
Complete supply missions to earn their trust. Utilize the map to find what the Zephyronians need.
We have no time to lose. Let's secure that Engine Core and make our way back home!
