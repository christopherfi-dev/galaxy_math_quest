role="main" tabindex="0">Task 3: Exam to Become a Time Coordinator for the Council of Timekeepers
Greetings, Commanders -
Our Council of Timekeepers is facing scheduling challenges of cosmic proportions. Important events scattered across dimensions are at risk of colliding, and we need your analytical mind to avert temporal catastrophe! Your task is to prove that you can read and understand our annual timetable, as you'll be playing a crucial role in planning the universe's most urgent events. Brace yourselves; the Council is counting on you!
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Objective:
Show that you can read and understand our annual timetable well. You'll be helping plan future schedules for very important people and events.
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
IXL Grade 4 - Practice: CC7
Materials Needed:
Council of Timekeepers: Annual Timetable
Pencil and Eraser: For making and correcting your calculations.
Mission Steps:
Calculate Event Times: Study the timetable and fill out the last column. How long does each event last (in minutes)
Take your exam: Answer the following questions at the bottom of your timetable.
What is the shortest event on the timetable?
What is the longest event on the timetable?
Which two events are clashing on Oct 4, 267?
On which date are the most events scheduled?
How much time is there between the end of "Save the Dinosaurs" and the start of "Prevent the Robot Uprising"?
Unlock the Next Task: Submit your worksheet and strategy to advance to your next mission. Capture photos of each and upload them to Google Drive.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this quest, you should be able to:
Calculate the duration between two given times.
Utilize pattern recognition in a practical, real-world scenario.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Calculate the duration and time between events.
Mission Conclusion:
Great job, young Timekeepers! If you pass this test, you are one step closer to becoming a full-fledged Temporal Coordinator. New adventures and challenges await you!
