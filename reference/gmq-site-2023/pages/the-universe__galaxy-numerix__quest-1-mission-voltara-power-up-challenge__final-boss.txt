role="main" tabindex="0">Final Boss - The Ultimate Colony Commander
Voltara is running out of space and stuff we need to live! We're going to find new planets and make them our new homes. You'll be in charge of this big job. We're counting on you to use our energy tokens smartly. You start with 1600 of these valuable tokens. Use them to build colonies and fuel your spaceships.
Objective:
Build strong colonies on new planets and set up trade routes with them. It's not just about making quick money; it's about thinking long-term!
CLICK HERE FOR DIRECTIONS
Materials Needed:
Distance Data Sheet: Info on different planets and what they offer.
Colony Needs Sheet: The different things you can build for each colony.
Place value blocks (1s, 10s, 100s) - Totaling 1600
Profit/Loss Sheet: To check if you're gaining or losing tokens.
Scenario Cards: You'll draw three of these to find out what good or bad stuff happens to your colonies.
Pencil and Eraser: For any changes in your plans.
Mission Steps:
Step 1 -
Acquire the Distance Data Sheet and Colony Needs Sheet.
Understand the Data: You have a Distance Data Sheet in front of you, listing the distances and potential profits for trading with various colonies.
Round the distances to the nearest 100. Round the profits to the nearest 100. (see the examples for Alpha and Beta)
Look at the Colony Needs Sheet. Round each Energy Units needed to the nearest 10.
Step 2 -
Decide how you'll use your 1600 tokens!
You only have 1600 tokens. For your first trade, you must trade with either Alpha, Beta, or Gamma. The other tokens must be used to start building your colony. Your colonists need buildings!
But first, draw 2 Scenario cards. These will tell you what will happen to you by the end of this mission. You can use these cards to guide your decisions...or ignore them and deal with the consequences.
Use the Profit/Loss sheet to plan out how you'll spend your money.
Show your Commander (your teacher) and have your plans approved. And then go on your first trade mission!
Draw 1 more Scenario card and then apply the 3 cards to your colony. These can be positive or negative consequences.
Use the Scenario Cards to figure out what has happened to your colony during your first mission.
Calculate how many energy tokens you now have on the Profit/Loss sheet. Decide whether to buy more buildings or trade with more planets.
That is the end of Round 1.
For Rounds 2 and 3, do the same thing again, except this time, you draw your 2 Scenario Cards after you have done your trades. Then you will adjust accordingly.
After you have done 3 rounds of this, you calculate your final profits (including the value of the buildings you have bought).
Metacognitive Reflection:
At the end of the quest, submit a brief reflection on your decision-making process. Upload this to Google Drive.
Learning Objectives:
By the end of this task, you should be able to:
Understand the concept of rounding to the nearest 10 or 100.
Apply place value knowledge to allocate energy units.
Make informed decisions based on fuel costs and potential profits.
Reflect on your decision-making process to understand your strategic strengths and areas for improvement.
Performance Assessment:
Your performance will be assessed based on your ability to:
Correctly round distances and calculate fuel costs.
Optimize trade routes for maximum profitability.
Make strategic investments in your colonies.
Mission Conclusion:
Well done, Commander! You've successfully established sustainable and profitable colonies. Your judicious use of energy units and fuel has made you a true asset to Voltara. Share your Profit/Loss Sheet and Colony Needs Cards with your teacher and await further instructions for your next mission.
