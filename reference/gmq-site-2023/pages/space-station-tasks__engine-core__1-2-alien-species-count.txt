role="main" tabindex="0">1-2- Alien Species Count
Objective:
Hi Space Cadets! We found new aliens on a far-away planet. Your job is to look at a picture, find different aliens, count them, and tell us what you see.
Introduction Video
Mission Steps:
Get Ready: Grab the Alien Picture, blank paper, and something to draw with.
Look at Aliens: Check out the Alien Picture. See the different types of aliens? Tell them apart.
Count Aliens: Now, count each kind of alien. You can use lines on another paper to help you count.
Make a Graph: Time to show what you found! Draw two lines like a "plus" sign on your blank paper.
a. Name the bottom line with the alien types.
b. Put numbers on the side line.
c. Draw boxes above each alien name. The box size shows how many you found.
d. Give your graph a name!
Check: Make sure your counts and graph are right.
Show Us: Once you're confident with your work, take a photo of your work and put it in your Portfolio.
Remember, this job isn’t just about counting. It's about being careful and showing what you know.
What You'll Learn:
How to spot and count different things.
How to show your counts on a graph.
How We Check:
We will look at how well you counted and made your graph. Being right matters for our space trip!
Finish Line:
Good job, Space Cadet! Your counts help us know more about aliens. Share your work to get a new card.
