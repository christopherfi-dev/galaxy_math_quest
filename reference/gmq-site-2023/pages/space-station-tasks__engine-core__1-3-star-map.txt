role="main" tabindex="0">1-3 - Star Mapping
Objective: Attention, Space Cadets! We're flying through a galaxy with five kinds of stars. You'll make a star map, find a path, and count stars to get a special code to start our ship.
Introduction Video
Mission Steps:
Draw Stars: Color your map with five star types (like Red, Blue, White, Yellow, Orange).
Know Stars: Learn what each star type means. Each has a number for math later.
Find the Path: Your teacher  will draw a line through stars on your map. Start and end where it shows.
Count Stars: Note how many of each star you see along your path.
Get Code: Multiply each star's count by its number. Add them up for your code.
Unlock the Engine Core:
Enter your calculated code. If it's correct, our Engine Core will turn on!
Learning Objectives:
Telling different stars apart.
Making your own star map.
Simple math to get a code.
Performance Assessment:
We see how well you made your map, counted stars, and got your code. Accuracy is key for our journey!
Mission Conclusion: Congratulations, Navigator! Your star map and code help us go further in space. Share your work with your teacher and get YOUR ENGINE CORE CARD.
