role="main" tabindex="0">Level 4 - Food for Thought in the Cosmic Kitchen 🌌🍏
Mission Briefing:
Hey Commanders! 🚀 We're at the halfway point in our mission, and we need to take a close look at what we've been eating. Our food supplies are changing, and we need to plan ahead. Ready to dive into some tasty data?
Watch this video for instructions - LINK
Before you Begin - IXL Practice:
Grade 3 -
UU-2 - Use bar graphs to solve problems
Objective:
Create a bar graph and use it to help analyze the data.
Materials Needed:
Graph Paper
Colored Pencils
Pre-Collected Data Sheet on kinds of food eaten and how much
Your Quest:
Part 1: Be a Data Chef! 🍳📊
Create Your Own Bar Graph: You've got data on the kinds of food we ate during the first three months of our journey. Grab some colored pencils and graph paper to make a bar graph.
You can choose to make 2 different bar graphs, or put the information on one graph so you can more easily compare the data.
👀 Data For You:
First 3 Months :
Fruits: 300 units
Vegetables: 250 units
Protein: 400 units
Grains: 350 units
Dairy: 400 units
Snacks: 150 units
Last 3 Months:
Protein: 100
Dairy: 120
Grains: 400
Canned Goods: 350
Freeze Dried: 250
Snacks: 50
Part 2: Put on Your Thinking Caps 🤔🧢
Thinking Question 1: Spot any changes in our eating habits? What types of food have we eaten more or less of?
Thinking Question 2: Got any theories on why our food choices have shifted?
📝 Write or Discuss: Share your thoughts in a few short sentences. Think about how these munching trends might affect our next food supply mission.
After writing down your ideas, create a video where you talk to the Supply Chief, you tell him what you have discovered, and how we should plan to pack food for our next long voyage. Upload it to Google Drive.
Mission Complete! 🌌
You did it, Commanders! Knowing more about our food habits will help us better prepare for our upcoming cosmic trips. Hand in your work and gear up for the next mission!
🏆 How You Did: Your teacher will review how well you understood the food data and your thoughtful answers to the questions.
