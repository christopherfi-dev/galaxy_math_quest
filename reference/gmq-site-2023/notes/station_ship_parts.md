### 1.2 The order of repair, in Marc's words

All **[Source: space-station-tasks]**.

1. **Engine Core** (counting). "The Engine Core of your ship is locked away and the only way to recover it is by calculating the correct codes. Counting space debris, identifying alien species in a grid, and mapping stars to find the right sequence of numbers will test your counting skills and help you recover your Engine Core."
2. **Navigation System** (addition and subtraction). "basic addition and subtraction problems, the backbone of your Navigation System recalibration. Figuring out fuel calculations for your journey, plotting the right coordinates, and determining the maximum weight your ship can carry".
3. **Shield Generator** (multiplication and division). "your Ship Defense: the Shield Generator. To protect yourself from threats, you'll need to master multiplication and division concepts. By calculating energy requirements, determining safe passage through asteroid fields, and calibrating your shield's strength, you will ensure your ship can withstand any assault."
4. **Weapon System** (odd and even). "upgrading your Offensive Systems: the Weapon System. Understanding the properties of odd and even numbers is crucial here. From coding your weapons to balancing your ammunition, to practicing targeting".
5. **Power Module** (place value). "Powering Up your ship's Power Module. You will need to understand the concept of place value to effectively distribute power across your spaceship. Connecting circuits in the right order, partitioning your ship's total power, and regrouping power distribution in an emergency scenario".

### 3.4 Rules of the world

- **Codes open things.** The Engine Core and the Weapon System both start when the right number code is entered. [Source: 1-1, 1-3, 4-1]
- **The weapon code needs all-odd sequences.** "The criteria for activating our weapon system is finding sequences that are all odd numbers." [Source: 4-1]
- **Ammunition must be even.** "To maintain balance in our spaceship, we need to ensure an even count of each type of ammunition." "It's crucial for the ship's balance that we maintain even numbers of each ammunition type." [Source: 4-2]
- **Cargo has two limits at once.** "We have 11 options, 30-ton limit, but only 7 items fit." [Source: 2-3]
- **The Engine Core and Navigation System come first in an emergency.** "The Engine Core and Navigation System are crucial for our survival." [Source: 5-3]
- **Power is shared between systems.** The Power Module feeds the Engine Core, Navigation System, Shield Generator and Weapon System. [Source: 5-2]
- **Coordinates are hidden as sums.** "instead of (5,3), you'll see (2+3, 5-2)." [Source: 2-2]
- **Card battle rules mentioned in passing** (recorded only, see section 2): starting hand size; "attack cards"; "once per game" abilities; discarding cards to heal; a "total shield"; a hit "lands" somewhere; "1/6 chance of dodging"; "two consecutive turns".
