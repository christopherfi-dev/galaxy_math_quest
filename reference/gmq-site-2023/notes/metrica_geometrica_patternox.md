- "Our primary mission is twofold: First, we need to restore the Chronosian time streams that have gone haywire. Second, we're on a quest to secure an advanced Engine Core for our spaceship. However, the Chronosians insist we help them first." [Source: CHR]

**What is wrong**

- "Chronosia is a universe-bending location, but its delicate time-balance is off-kilter, and we're the wrench to fix it." [Source: CHR]

**The prize**

- Version 1: "an advanced Engine Core for our spaceship". [Source: CHR, first paragraph]
- Version 2: "We have a timeline to restore and an upgraded Weapons System to acquire!" [Source: CHR, last line of the briefing]

**The four-step story, in order** [Source: CHR, CHR-T1 to T4]

1. Fix the clock tower. The day-night cycle comes back.
2. Study six Time Quakes. Predict the seventh.
3. Sit the exam to become a Temporal Coordinator for the Council.
4. As a new Trainee, plan a day for four Council Members.

## B3. The tasks

**Task 1 - Calibrate the Time Streams** [Source: CHR-T1, DOC-Clocks, DOC-Report]

- Look at eight clocks. Some show impossible times. Write the corrected time. Tick "Gear Unlocked".
- Fill in the Restoration Report. Photograph both and upload.
- Maths: reading time; valid and invalid times; 12-hour and 24-hour clocks.
- Practice first: clock flashcards, IXL Grade 3 QQ1 to QQ3.

**Task 2 - Unravel the Temporal Rift Riddle** [Source: CHR-T2, DOC-Quakes]

- Work out how long each of six quakes lasted. Find the pattern. Predict the start and end of the seventh.
- Write a strategy for handling the seventh quake.
- Maths: elapsed time; number patterns.
- Practice first: IXL Grade 3 RR1 to RR4.

**Task 3 - Exam to Become a Time Coordinator** [Source: CHR-T3, DOC-Timetable]

- Fill in the "Duration (Minutes)" column for eight events. Answer five questions.
- Maths: elapsed time; reading a timetable; spotting overlaps.
- Practice first: IXL Grade 4 CC7 (transportation schedules).

**Task 4 - Plan the Perfect Day** [Source: CHR-T4, DOC-Profiles, DOC-Quiz]

- Read the brochure. Pass the ten-question quiz.
- Give events to Sara, Tim, Alex and Zara that fit their interests.
- Then read the four special conditions and change the plan.
- Maths: elapsed time; building a timetable; logic with limits.

## B6. Errors and recomputed numbers

**Clocks** [Check, from DOC-Clocks]

- Four are impossible: 3:67, 14:75, 25:00, 7:99.
- Four are possible: 12:15 PM, 9:42 AM, 5:30 PM, 0:00.
- If "correct" means carry the extra minutes: 3:67 is 4:07, 14:75 is 15:15, 7:99 is 8:39, 25:00 is 1:00 the next day. [Guess: Marc does not say this is the rule]
- 0:00 is a real 24-hour time (midnight). A child may think it is broken. Decide which answer the game accepts.
- The page promises analog clocks ("both analog and digital clocks"). The checklist has only digital times.

**Time Quakes** [Check, from DOC-Quakes]

| Quake | Start | End | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12:49 | 12:54 | 5 min |
| 2 | 1:24 | 1:34 | 10 min |
| 3 | 2:04 | 2:19 | 15 min |
| 4 | 2:49 | 3:09 | 20 min |
| 5 | 3:39 | 4:04 | 25 min |
| 6 | 4:34 | 5:04 | 30 min |

- Lengths go up by 5. The quiet gap between quakes is always 30 minutes.
- So quake 7 starts at 5:34, lasts 35 minutes and ends at 6:09.
- The data is clean. I found no error.
- One thing to check: quake 1 is at 12:49 and quake 2 at 1:24. That only works on a 12-hour clock. In 24-hour time it would be 13:24.

That is hard for Grade 3. It also assumes both "local" clocks are the same clock, and the two events are in different dimensions.
