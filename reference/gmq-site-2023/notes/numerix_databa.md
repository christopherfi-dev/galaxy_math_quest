For each part pick foods that will still be good, multiply energy score by number of units, and reach 360. Maths: multiplication (score × units), adding to a target of 360, comparing numbers (shelf life against month), decimals in passing (0.5 month). [Source: FOOD-L2, 12 Month Food Plan doc]
