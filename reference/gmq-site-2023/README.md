# Marc's 2023–24 student site, saved as text

**Saved:** 20 November 2026. **From:** https://sites.google.com/view/g3c-2023-space-math-quest (public).

[Source: Marc, 2026-11-20] "Explore that website. Follow the links. It's where I was building the worlds. But those are just skeletons." And: "I am offering you a base floor."

So this folder is the **floor**: what Marc's own pages say. New things in the game should start here and go further.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `pages/` | The words of all 63 pages. Each file starts with the page address, its links out and its count of images. |
| `linked_docs/` | The words of the Google Docs the pages link to (27 files). All opened without a login. |
| `notes/` | Three notes written by helper agents: every place, being, object and rule, in Marc's own words, with conflicts and errors. |

**The three notes**

- `notes/numerix_databa.md`: the home page, the universe, Voltara, the trade quest, the Final Boss, Operation Stardust, the Coding Comets.
- `notes/metrica_geometrica_patternox.md`: Chronosia, Xylophora, Zephyron, Patternox.
- `notes/station_ship_parts.md`: the 15 station tasks and the five ship parts.

**What was not read**

- Pictures. No page's pictures could be read, and most pages report none in the saved copy.
- About 17 Loom videos. They may hold spoken story and rules that are written nowhere.
- Google Forms and one Google Sheet. Not opened, on purpose: they may hold student answers.
- A Google Classroom link, and about nine Google Drive files (maps, the broken-clocks picture, the Events Description Brochure). Marc would have to open these by hand.
- IXL links.

**Privacy**

- No student names, passwords or results were met.
- One adult's name stood on three public Xylophora pages. It is replaced here by "[a teacher]", because this repository is public.

**Care**

- The notes were written by AI helpers. Their quotes were copied from the saved pages, but check a quote against `pages/` before relying on it.
