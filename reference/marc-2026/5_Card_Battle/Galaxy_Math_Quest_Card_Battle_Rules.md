# Galaxy Math Quest Card Battle: Rules Rebuilt from Your Threads

This brings together everything about the card battle in four threads from July 2023 to October 2024. Where two threads disagree, the newer one wins, and the older version is listed under "Conflicts between threads." Each rule is tagged:

- **[Stated]**: you said or wrote it in a thread.
- **[Inferred]**: the threads point to it but never spell it out.
- **[Open]**: the threads never settle it. These are listed together under "Rules still to decide."

## 1. What each player needs

- **5 ship parts**, one of each type: Engine Core, Navigation System, Shield Generator, Weapon System and Power Module. [Stated]
- **A deck of booster cards** that the student has earned. [Stated]
- **A way to track HP.** Whiteboards or counters were the first plan. In August 2023 you said whiteboards were too hard to manage, so you started a Glide app built on a Google Sheet. Each student's row holds the Ship Shield HP plus the HP and AP of each part. [Stated]

## 2. Earning cards and ship parts

- Students start at the space station. The space station tasks cover the basic skills they need before the galaxy quests, and they earn booster cards and Level 1 ship parts. [Stated, July 2023]
- **Cards:** students get cards for completing quests. [Stated, Oct 2024] In July 2023 the rule was one new card per completed task.
- **Ship parts:** students earn a randomly drawn ship part after completing five quests in a section. [Stated, Oct 2024] In July 2023, students chose a ship part once they had finished all the tasks tied to that part.
- **Upgrades:** students level up their ship parts by doing quests across the galaxies. The galaxy map holds the Grade 3 and Grade 4 scope and sequence, so leveling every part is meant to take the whole year. Many quests require quests in other galaxies first. [Stated]

## 5. Booster cards

- **Rarities:** Common (C), Advanced (A) and Epic (E). Rarer cards are more powerful. [Stated]
- **Target mix for new card sets:** 5 : 3.5 : 1.5, which is 50% Common, 35% Advanced and 15% Epic. [Stated]
- **Card design** [Stated]:
  - Same size as a Pokémon card (63 × 88 mm), with no images.
  - Card name in bold at the top, and the effect in the middle.
  - C, A or E in the bottom-left corner, and the card number (about 1 to 200) in the bottom-right corner.
  - Background color by rarity: Common #FFFFFF, Advanced #DEFADE, Epic #D8BFD8 (see Conflicts about the Epic color).
  - Made in NanDeck from a CSV file with the columns Title, Description, Type, ID, BackgroundColor.
- **The current card pool:** the latest CSV (July 25, 2023) has 134 numbered cards. Cards #62–79 repeat #32–49 exactly. Without that repeat there are **116 different cards**: 60 Common, 33 Advanced and 23 Epic. That works out to about 52% / 28% / 20%, which is more Epic and fewer Advanced than your target.
- **What the cards do:** direct damage (mostly 2 to 20, plus Time Bomb at 100), damage to two parts, three parts or every part, damage to the Ship Shield, repairs and healing, damage reduction, blocks and dodges, disabling a part or skipping the opponent's turn, extra turns, and math-themed modifiers (add 10, double, triple, halve, round up to the nearest 10).

## 6. How a battle works

**Setup** [Stated]
1. Each player lays their 5 ship parts face up on the table.
2. Each player sets their Ship Shield to 100 HP, or higher if their Shield Generator is upgraded.
3. Each player shuffles their booster deck and draws 5 cards, or 6, 7 or 8 with an upgraded Engine Core.

**Taking turns** [Stated]
1. It is a 1-on-1 game. Players take turns going first.
2. On your turn you play one card from your hand (two attack cards with Weapons Level 3).
3. When the first player plays an attack card, the second player can play a card in response before the attack hits.
4. Each player keeps 5 cards in hand at a time.
5. There is no board and no movement. Effects that move a ship or teleport it don't fit the game.

**Targeting** [Inferred]
- The attacker chooses which part of the opponent's ship to damage, and the amount of damage varies from attack to attack. This is how you described it while building the Glide app.
- Attacks seem to hit the Ship Shield before the parts. The Weapons Level 6 and 10 abilities let an attack "bypass a ship's shield," which only makes sense if attacks normally hit the shield first.

**Winning** [Stated]
- You win by bringing your opponent's ship parts down to 0 HP.

## 8. Rules still to decide [Open]

The threads never answer these questions. Players will run into most of them in their first game.

1. **What does AP do?** Every part has AP, and cards talk about "your attack" ("add 10 to your attack") and a part being "unable to attack." That suggests parts attack using their AP, but no thread says how. For example: does each part attack once per turn, does the player pick one part, or is it something else?
2. **Ship Shield vs. parts.** Does every attack hit the shield first? Once the shield is gone, who picks which part takes damage? The cards are mixed: Photon Torpedo hits "the ship shield," Energy Beam deals damage *and* lowers the shield, and most cards just say "inflict X HP damage."
3. **Destroyed parts.** When a part reaches 0 HP, does it stop attacking and lose its upgrade abilities? Do you need all 5 parts at 0 to win?
4. **Do upgrade bonuses add up?** It's unclear whether "+8 HP" at Level 3 means 8 more on top of Level 2, or 8 in total. If the bonuses add up, a Level 10 part gains +180 HP and +171 AP. That is far more than any starting HP (5 to 40) or the strongest attack cards.
5. **Responses.** Which cards can be played in response to an attack? Does a response use up the defender's card for their own turn? When do players draw back up to 5 cards?
6. **Deck rules.** How many cards are in a deck, and is there a limit on Advanced and Epic cards? (Limiting Advanced and Epic cards was one of the fixes considered when you felt the boosters were too strong.)
7. **Navigation, Weapons and Power Module abilities.** Keep what's on the website, or replace them? The last set Claude suggested (July 2023) was:
   - Level 3 (as strong as Advanced cards): Navigation, immune to damage for 1 turn. Weapons, 15 damage to the target part and nearby parts. Power, a 50 HP shield for 2 turns.
   - Level 6 (as strong as Epic cards): Navigation, take two turns in a row. Weapons, 40 damage that skips past shields. Power, fully restore all parts.
   - Level 10 ("god-like"): Navigation, teleport anywhere (this doesn't fit, since there's no board). Weapons, 100 damage to every enemy part. Power, immune to all damage for 2 turns.
8. **Random draws.** When a student earns a card, how is its rarity decided? What are the odds of drawing a Lucky Draw ship part?
9. **Patterns galaxy cards.** On July 24, 2023 Claude drafted 15 Patterns galaxy cards (Fact Families, Prime Protector, Fibonacci Firepower and others). You never confirmed them, and they aren't in the 134-card CSV. Some of them refer to "× or ÷ cards" and "+ and − cards," which don't exist in the current card pool. The draft also split 5/5/5 instead of your 5 : 3.5 : 1.5 mix.
