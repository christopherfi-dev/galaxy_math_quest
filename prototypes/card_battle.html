How to play

The cards

The rim of a card is its kind: green is common, purple is rare, gold is epic. A shield card of a lower kind still stops the other ship's own attack. It cannot stop the part that a higher attack card adds.

A round

  1. A card is an order to your ship. One player goes first and gives an order. The other sees it, then answers. You take turns to go first.
  2. A Spy acts first. It gets past the shield. A Station Guard stops it.
  3. Hearts repair.
  4. Both ships attack. Your attack is the AP of all your working parts, plus your card. The page adds it up.
  5. Both attacks land together.
  6. An attack hits the Main Shield first. If the attack is bigger than the shield, the shield breaks and soaks up all of it.
  7. A shield that a card puts up has the colour of that card: green, purple or gold. It always stops the other ship's own attack. But if the other player's attack card is of a higher kind, the part that card adds goes through to your parts. If that higher card doubles the attack, or rounds it up, the whole attack goes through. A block card works the same way: it takes its number off the ship's own attack, whatever card the other player used. The shield you start with stops everything.
  8. Dodge cards make the other ship's attack miss you. You still fire. Against Wormhole Zip and Triple Illusion, a higher attack card can still hit with the part it adds. Nothing gets past Vanishing Act, except a Spy.
  9. A broken shield can come back. Play a shield card: a new shield goes up, as strong as the number on the card. Even a small one soaks up one whole attack. In your hand, such a card is marked "shield back".
  10. With no shield, you choose which of your parts take the hit. The view moves in on your ship. The hit comes as blocks of ten, five and one. Tap a part to send it the lit block: its HP goes down as you tap. Tap another block to light it. "Break a block" makes the lit block into smaller ones. If a tap would destroy a part, the page asks first: tap again to give that part up.

A lost part

  • Weapons: you cannot attack.
  • Shield Generator: you cannot defend: shield cards do nothing.
  • Power Module: you cannot heal: heart cards do nothing.
  • Engine or Navigation: you lose 1 card.
