How to play

The cards

The rim of a card is its kind: green is common, purple is rare, gold is gold. A common card cannot block a rare attack card. A common or a rare card cannot block a gold one.

A round

  1. A card is an order to your ship. One player goes first and gives an order. The other sees it, then answers. You take turns to go first.
  2. A Spy acts first. It gets past the shield. A policeman stops it.
  3. Hearts repair.
  4. Both ships attack. Your attack is the AP of all your working parts, plus your card. The page adds it up.
  5. An attack hits the Main Shield first. If the attack is bigger than the shield, the shield breaks and soaks up all of it.
  6. With no shield, you choose which of your parts take the hit. The hit comes as blocks of ten, five and one. Tap a part to send it the lit block. Tap another block to light it. "Break" makes the lit block into smaller ones.

A lost part

  • Weapons: you cannot attack.
  • Shield Generator: you cannot defend: shield cards do nothing.
  • Power Module: you cannot heal: heart cards do nothing.
  • Engine or Navigation: you lose 1 card.
