How a round goes

HP is how much damage a part can take. AP is how hard it hits.

  1. You and the enemy each put down one card, at the same time.
  2. A Spy acts first. It gets past the shield.
  3. Repair cards work next.
  4. Both ships attack. Your attack is the AP of all your working parts added up, plus your card.
  5. An attack hits the Main Shield first. If the attack is bigger than the shield, the shield breaks and soaks up all of it.
  6. With no shield, you choose which of your parts take the hit.

An example, with another ship

A ship's parts have AP 15, 10, 5, 20 and 10. That is 60. It plays a card that adds 7. Its attack is 67.

The other ship blocks 12. So 55 gets through. Its shield has 80. Now it has 25.

A lost part

  • Weapons: you cannot attack.
  • Shield Generator: your defence cards do nothing.
  • Power Module: your repair cards do nothing.
  • Engine or Navigation: you lose a card.
