Play the practice battle with these cards

1. One rule for the numbers

A child can say it: Common: one small number. Rare: about double, or two things at once. Epic: it does something no other card does.

An attack card and a block card of the same size cancel each other. Every number is one a Grade 3 child can add or take away in their head, or on paper.

Words the cards use (working guesses, yours to change)

  • A working part has HP left. A lost part is at 0.
  • Block comes off the attack first, before it reaches your shield or your parts.
  • Your rule, 22 November: a block card stops an attack card only of its own kind, or a lower one. A common card cannot block a rare card. A common or a rare card cannot block an epic card.
  • A repair is for a working part, and never lifts it above the HP it started with. Only Stellar Revival and Comeback Crew bring a lost part back.
  • The Main Shield never passes 100. Your rule, 22 November: a shield card played on a broken shield puts it back up, as strong as the number on the card.
  • Two things on one card are rare. Each of the two is no bigger than a common card.

2. The ship parts: five parts, four kinds

The colour of the border is the kind. Each kind means the same thing on every part.

A ship of five Standard parts has HP and AP. The names and the Standard numbers are yours. 13 of the 15 Standard, Guardian and Striker numbers are the ones in the table from your 2023 chats. The two moved onto the rule: Neutron Wall (33 and 2 there) and Quantum Deflector (28 and 7 there). The Lucky Draw numbers are new, except the Cosmic Navigator.

3. The battle cards: to choose from

The big symbol is what the card does: a laser gun attacks, a shield blocks, a heart repairs, a spy sneaks past the shield, a policeman stops a spy, a ship slipping aside dodges. The thick rim is the kind: green for common, purple for rare, gold for epic. Those are the colours of your 2023 cards. The Guard is your Policeman under a new name; change it back if you prefer.

In a battle each deck has one Spy card, drawn at random from the here, and at least as many Guards. [Marc, 22 November: one Spy card each, and at least as many Policemen.]

4. The starter pack

Your rule, 23 November: every child earns 15 cards on the KIS Station, always with 1 Spy, 1 Policeman, 1 epic and 1 rare. The other 11 are my proposal: 4 common attack cards, 4 common shield cards and 3 common repair cards, so the first deck can fight, bring a shield back and mend. Each child's pack is drawn from their own seed, so packs differ, and a child always gets the same one.

5. Level 2 upgrades

The numbers are yours: HP +4 and AP +3. In 2023 an upgrade was earned on quests. In the practice battle it is one of the cards in each deck: playing it makes that part Level 2 for that battle. That is a guess, and a switch.
