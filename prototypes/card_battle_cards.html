Play the practice battle with these cards

1. One rule for the numbers

A child can say it: Common: one small number. Rare: about double, or two things at once. Epic: it does something no other card does.

An attack card and a block card of the same size cancel each other. Every number is one a Grade 3 child can add or take away in their head, or on paper.

Words the cards use (working guesses, yours to change)

  • A working part has HP left. A lost part is at 0.
  • Block comes off the attack first, before it reaches your shield or your parts.
  • A repair is for a working part, and never lifts it above the HP it started with. Only Stellar Revival brings a lost part back.
  • The Main Shield never passes 100. Once it is broken, only Shield Reborn brings it back.

2. The ship parts: five parts, four kinds

The colour of the border is the kind. Each kind means the same thing on every part.

A ship of five Standard parts has HP and AP. The names and the Standard numbers are yours. 13 of the 15 Standard, Guardian and Striker numbers are the ones in the table from your 2023 chats. The two moved onto the rule: Neutron Wall (33 and 2 there) and Quantum Deflector (28 and 7 there). The Lucky Draw numbers are new, except the Cosmic Navigator.

3. The battle cards: to choose from

The colour of the card is what it does. The gems and the frame are the kind: one bronze gem for common, two silver for rare, three gold for epic. The Guard is your Policeman under a new name; change it back if you prefer.

4. Level 2 upgrades

These are not played in a battle. They change a part for good. The numbers are yours: HP +4 and AP +3.

5. Ideas that need a rule from you first

These are not in the set. Each needs a rule your rules sheet does not have yet. If you make the rule, the card can join.
