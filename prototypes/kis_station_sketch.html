For the teacher: what this sketch shows

Galaxy Math Quest. This is a sketch, not the real build.

  • One station you walk around in. Rooms you have not entered stay dark. Signs by each door say where it leads.
  • Every command is two to four words: a verb, a thing, and sometimes a second thing. Type it, or tap a verb and then tap the thing.
  • The astronaut walks to the thing before acting, so every command shows movement.
  • There are 12 secrets across five station rooms, and 3 more on Voltara. Things change and stay changed.
  • Maths is staged in the world. Asteroids drift in rows outside the Shield Bay windows. Crates stack on the cargo scale. The locker code is the number of stacked crates, and each player gets a different stack.
  • After launch you fly across a galaxy map and see other cadets’ ships. Fly to Galaxy Numerix and land on Voltara.
  • Voltara is drawn from your own words: rainbow skies, cotton-candy clouds, glowing trees, a sparkling river, a Gumblefish, FlavorBursts. You can dig holes, and they stay.
  • The city shows the Energy Council problem. As a child types a plan, each district lights up. The numbers are 570, 920 and 450 against 1,650, from your drafts.
  • The robot is the skills trainer. Talk to it and it asks the next skill you have not shown yet. A wrong answer gets one more try. A second miss gets small steps, then three in a row to prove it. Each skill is a star on the star chart, and the teacher view shows the status of each one.
  • Story problems come from a bank of 120, set in your world. Code picks the numbers.
  • This sketch knows about 40 words. On your site, your constrained AI would answer any other sentence.
  • The robot is a new character. It is a proposal. Card names are samples from your files. Odds are 70, 25, 5.
