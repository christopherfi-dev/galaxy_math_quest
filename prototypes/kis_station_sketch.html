KIS Station

For Marc: what this sketch shows
  • The world is data, like Hollow House. Each room is a title, a few short lines, a picture and a list of things.
  • The map fills in as a child explores. One room is secret. Only typing finds it.
  • Tap does the expected thing. Typing does the unexpected thing. On your site, the typed line goes to your constrained AI. Here it only knows a few words.
  • The Shield Bay has a mini battle that interrupts. The Navigation Bay has a mini quest you find. Code makes the numbers and marks them.
  • Cargo Load is the 2-3 project, with your own item list. The code checks the two conditions. In class it stays a real task with item cards.
  • Cards use sample odds: 70 common, 25 rare, 5 gold. A project always gives gold. The card names are samples from your set.
  • Progress is saved on this device only. Your server would keep it for each student.
