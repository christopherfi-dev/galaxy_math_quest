KIS Station

For Marc: what this sketch shows
  • One station you walk around in. Rooms you have not entered stay dark. The small map in the corner fills in.
  • Every command is two to four words: a verb, a thing, and sometimes a second thing. Type it, or tap a verb and then tap the thing.
  • The astronaut walks to the thing before acting, so every command shows movement.
  • After launch you fly across a galaxy map and see other cadets’ ships. Fly to Galaxy Numerix and land on Voltara.
  • Voltara is drawn from your own words: rainbow skies, cotton-candy clouds, glowing trees, a sparkling river, a Gumblefish, FlavorBursts. You can dig holes, and they stay.
  • The city lights show the Energy Council problem. As a child types a plan, the lights come on. The numbers are 570, 920 and 450 against 1,650, from your drafts.
  • There are 12 secrets across five station rooms, and 3 more on Voltara. Things change and stay changed: a crate moves, a vent opens, a robot wakes and follows you.
  • Maths lives in the world. The locker code is the number of stacked crates, and each player gets a different stack.
  • The Shield Bay battle, the fuel quest and the Cargo Load project are the same as before. Code makes the numbers and marks them.
  • This sketch knows about 40 words. On your site, your constrained AI would answer any other sentence.
  • The robot is a new character. It is a proposal. Card names are samples from your files. Odds are 70, 25, 5.
