## 3. What to deliver: one note, `proposals/GMQ_Stardust_Story_v1_2026-11-28.md`

1. **The story,** in Marc's sense (handoff rule 5): on arrival a child knows where they are, what happened there, and what they are doing and why. Short readings: two or three arrival screens, speech a sentence or two at a time.
2. **The room plan:** 16 rooms on a 4 × 4 board. For each room:
   - its job on the station, and why it is there;
   - 2 to 4 things to look at or use;
   - a task or a clue (or neither: say so);
   - a secret;
   - its doorways, labelled, and what closes any of them (with a story reason, never a number lock).
   - **Each room is one screen, wider than tall:** design for about 16 squares across and 10 down, on an iPad held sideways. The main thread confirms the exact size, because it is changing the screen's layout now (the menu button).
   - **No room is black until entered.** A map shows the rooms seen.
3. **The tasks.** Keep the temperature chart and the food task, made clearer. Say what confused children in the food task (ask Marc). Every task passes the story test (the master plan, section 5b): **need, meaning, consequence, picture.** No maths as a password. A finished example first, about a different quantity. Nothing that gives the answer away.
4. **How it joins the rest of the game.** The crew taken to a planet is a thread that crosses worlds. Lay out options for Marc: which planet, when the rescue comes, and whether this is the first piece of the story that joins the places ("Project Second Home", `proposals/GMQ_Story_and_Size_Options_v1_2026-11-20.md`, still open since 20 November). The radio's ending 3 (the map in the beeps) was decided on 22 November: keep it, or say why not.
5. **Cards.** Where a card is earned, and why (the children love them). No card for nothing.
6. **Decisions for Marc,** shortest first, with your pick for each.
7. **What could prove this wrong.**

**Numbers:** give the story, not the numbers. The builder writes and checks every number rule on thousands of seeds.
