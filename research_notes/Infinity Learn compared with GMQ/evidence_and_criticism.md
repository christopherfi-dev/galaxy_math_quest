# Infinity Learn by Sri Chaitanya: evidence and criticism (as of 17 November 2026)

How to read these notes:

- **Company claim** means the number comes from Infinity Learn or Sri Chaitanya, usually through a paid press release.
- **Independent** means a journalist, regulator, store listing or reviewer who is not the company.
- **Snippet only** means I saw the text in a search result but could not open the page itself.
- Reviewer names are left out on purpose. They are private people.
- "Infinity Learn" is the edtech company. Its legal name is Rankguru Technology Solutions Private Limited. "Sri Chaitanya" is the parent group of schools and coaching colleges. They are kept apart below.

## 1. What results does the company claim, and is there independent evidence behind them?

### Takeaway
Every outcome number I found comes from the company itself, mostly through paid press-release wires. I found no randomised trial, no third-party evaluation, no EdTech Tulna review and no published efficacy data for any Infinity Learn product.

### Cited Findings

**Learner numbers (all company claims)**

- September 2025: "close to 1 million paid learners across 10,000+ schools." The page is labelled an advertorial supplied by BusinessWire India. No date or method is given for the figure. — [Tribune India, 10 Sep 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)
- 2024: "more than 7 million users, with over 750K actively subscribing for premium content." Snippet only. I could not confirm which press release page carries it. The likely page is this wire copy. — [The Week / NewsVoir wire, 27 Apr 2024](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2024/04/27/dcm5--infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya.html)
- September 2024: the company said its goal was "over 50 million learners on our platform by 2025." I found no later report saying whether this was met. — [Outlook Business, Sep 2024](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/sector/ed-tech/ex-ceo-of-fiitjee-eschool-and-ex-business-head-of-upgrad-joins-infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-news-10314)
- **[Conflict]** The three learner figures do not line up: 7 million users (2024), a 50 million goal (for 2025), and "close to 1 million paid learners" (Sep 2025). They may count different things (registered users, paying users, school students). The company does not define them.

**Rank and exam results (all company claims)**

- Infinity Learn's own pages claim "22094+ students qualified in JEE Main 2025", "4212+ students qualified in JEE Advanced 2025", and "1 out of 5 IIT admissions in 2025 came from Sri Chaitanya." Snippet only. Note the last claim is about the parent group, not the app. — [infinitylearn.com/jee](https://infinitylearn.com/jee); [infinitylearn.com JEE class 13 course page](https://infinitylearn.com/jee-class-13-online-course)
- 2026: a press release claims Infinity Learn is "the only institution in India to produce two All India Rank 1 holders in the same year" in JEE Main 2026. Snippet only. — [Devdiscourse, 2026](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/3883019-infinity-learn-students-achieve-top-ranks-in-jee-main-2026); [ThePrint / ANI press release, 2026](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/infinity-learns-hybrid-advantage-drives-strong-performance-in-jee-advanced-2026/2947263/)
- 2024: a press release calls Infinity Learn an "outcomes-based edtech" that "sets a new industry benchmark in JEE Main 2024." It is an ANI press release, not reporting. — [ThePrint / ANI press release, 2024](https://theprint.in/ani-press-releases/outcomes-based-edtech-infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-sets-a-new-industry-benchmark-in-jee-main-2024-session-1/1965963)
- Parent group, 2025: Sri Chaitanya said it won five all-India top-10 ranks in NEET UG 2025. The headline itself says "says Sri Chaitanya". — [Deccan Chronicle, 2025](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/got-top-5-ranks-in-neet-ug-says-sri-chaitanya-1885427)

**"AI-powered" outcome claims (all company claims)**

- AINA (voice AI mentor, launched 10 Sep 2025): the CEO says it has "96.7% response accuracy" (97% for NEET 2025, 95% for JEE Mains 2025), resolves "2 lakh+ doubts" a month, and answers in about 5 seconds. The press release does not say how accuracy was measured or who checked it. The "India's first" headline claim is also unsupported in the text. — [Tribune India, 10 Sep 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)
- VISTA (launched Oct 2023): described as a "$1M AI-powered solution" using several large language models. Coverage is launch announcements with no outcome data. — [YourStory, Oct 2023](https://yourstory.com/2023/10/infinity-learn-ai-solution-vista-learning-challenges)

**Money claims (company claims)**

- The company said revenue passed Rs 100 crore in FY23, up from Rs 2.3 crore in FY22. — [Outlook Business, Sep 2024](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/sector/ed-tech/ex-ceo-of-fiitjee-eschool-and-ex-business-head-of-upgrad-joins-infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-news-10314)
- The company said it reached break-even at profit-after-tax level in FY23, after a Rs 37 crore loss the year before. — [CXOToday, media-coverage reprint](https://cxotoday.com/media-coverage/infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-reports-strong-revenue-growth-along-with-profit-in-its-latest-financial-report/)
- September 2023: the CEO set a target of Rs 500 crore revenue by 2025. I found no report of whether it was met. — [YourStory, Sep 2023](https://yourstory.com/2023/09/india-edtech-infinity-learn-shri-chaitanya-ujjwal-singh-techsparks)

**Independent evaluation**

- EdTech Tulna is the main Indian product-evaluation index for edtech. Its published reviews include Khan Academy and Matific. I found no Tulna review of Infinity Learn. — [Central Square Foundation on EdTech Tulna](https://www.centralsquarefoundation.org/articles/how-edtech-tulna-aims-to-build-evidence-around-the-efficacy-of-edtech-solutions)
- Indian regulators have repeatedly found coaching rank advertisements misleading. By February 2025 the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) had fined 24 coaching centres a total of Rs 77.6 lakh. — [Business Standard, 14 Feb 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/iitpk-fined-misleading-iit-jee-results-ads-125021400896_1.html); [Taxmann summary](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/consumer-protection-authority-fines-24-coaching-centres-₹77-6-lakh-for-misleading-ads-urges-compliance-with-guidelines/)
- The CCPA fined Narayana, Sri Chaitanya's main rival, Rs 8 lakh over JEE Advanced 2024 rank ads. The regulator said the ads showed names and ranks but hid which course each student took. This is about Narayana, not Sri Chaitanya. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/misleading-jee-advanced-ads-ccpa-imposes-8-lakh-penalty-on-narayana-educational-institutions-539031); [Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/litigation/ccpa-fines-narayana-educational-institutions-8-lakh-for-misleading-jee-advanced-ads)
- I found no CCPA order, ASCI ruling or court finding against Infinity Learn or Sri Chaitanya over rank claims.

### Inferences
- The rank claims cannot show that the app works. Sri Chaitanya runs selective, full-time residential coaching. A top ranker who studied in a Sri Chaitanya classroom and also had an Infinity Learn login is not evidence about the app. The press releases do not separate online-only learners from classroom students.
- The "96.7% accuracy" figure for AINA is a marketing number until someone outside the company says what was tested and how. Read the other way, it concedes that about 1 answer in 30 is wrong.
- The step from "Rs 500 crore by 2025" and "50 million learners by 2025" to silence in 2026 is worth noting. It is not proof of failure. It is an unfinished story.
- What would prove these inferences wrong: an audited filing showing FY25 revenue, a published AINA test set with third-party scoring, or a results list that names each student's course and mode of study.

### Gaps
- I could not find audited FY24 or FY25 financials. I did not access Ministry of Corporate Affairs filings. Entrackr and Inc42 trackers returned nothing for Infinity Learn or Rankguru Technology Solutions.
- The Infinity Learn home page is built with scripts and I could not read its claims directly. The on-site claims above are from search snippets.
- A search snippet said Careers360 found several coaching institutes claiming the same NEET 2025 toppers, and listed the AIR 1 student under a different institute than Sri Chaitanya. I opened the page and could not find that text in the first 100,000 characters. Treat it as unconfirmed. — [Careers360 page](https://medicine.careers360.com/articles/neet-2025-exam-which-are-the-best-coaching-institutes)
- I could not open the Lokmat Times JEE Main 2026 press release (403) or the YourStory AINA feature (403).

## 2. What do ratings and reviews say?

### Takeaway
Ratings are split. Students who rate their own teacher are often happy. Parents who write about sales, timetables and refunds are often angry. The Trustpilot score is "Poor" (2.4 of 5, 60% one-star) and the Google Play score is middling (3.6 of 5).

### Cited Findings

**Scores (independent listings, read 17 Nov 2026)**

- Google Play, "Infinity Learn-JEE, NEET, CBSE": 3.6 stars, 6.46K reviews, 10 lakh+ (1 million+) downloads, updated 14 Nov 2026, rated 3+, has in-app purchases. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)
- Trustpilot: TrustScore 2.4 of 5 ("Poor"), 94 reviews, 92 of them in the last 12 months. Split: 60% one-star, 3% two-star, 6% three-star, 8% four-star, 23% five-star. The company claimed the profile in August 2025. Trustpilot notes it "hasn't replied to negative reviews." — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)
- ConsumerComplaints.in: 3 complaints listed. None shows a company reply or a resolution. — [ConsumerComplaints.in company page](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/bycompany/infinity-learn-a661721.html)
- Google reviews of physical centres (through an aggregator): the Sri Chaitanya (Infinity Learn) Madhapur location shows 2.7 from 177 reviews. Another listing shows 3.8 from 220. Snippet only. **[Conflict]** between listings. — [Collegedunia, Madhapur](https://s3.collegedunia.com/institute/8885-sri-chaitanya-infinity-learn-hyderabad/google-reviews); [Collegedunia, second listing](https://s4.collegedunia.com/institute/8023-infinity-learn-hyderabad/google-reviews)
- Third-party install tracker: 100,000 installs reached in 2023, 1,000,000 passed by April 2026. — [AndroidRank](https://www.androidrank.org/application/infinity_learn_jee_neet_cbse/com.infinitylearn.learn)

**Recurring praise (Trustpilot, mostly 4 to 12 Aug 2026)**

- Even the positive reviews carry small complaints: maths pace too fast, internet drops, a repeated "RM taken error" in live class, microphone faults. — [Trustpilot, page 2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com?page=2)
- One 4-star review (8 Aug 2026) says the app "sometimes marks correct answers as wrong." This is a single report. — [Trustpilot, page 2](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com?page=2)

**Recurring complaints (several independent reports each)**

Examples: 17 Nov 2026 (representative went silent after payment); 18 Oct 2026 (Rs 2,000 seat-blocking fee, only "explanations about internal procedures"); 20 Aug 2026 (about Rs 30,000 lost); 6 Aug 2026 (refund not paid inside the stated window). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)

- No teacher or no response after payment. Examples: 14 Oct 2026 (no teacher for about 15 days); 5 Oct 2026 ("once fees paid then no response"); 9 Oct 2026 (classes suspended after a refund request). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)
- Timetable clashes with school hours, then no refund (20 Aug 2026, and an earlier Class 12 case). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)
- Technical faults and low video quality (24 Oct 2026; 10 Aug 2026). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)
- Google Play's three featured reviews on 17 Nov 2026 were all one-star. They are dated 26 Oct, 27 Oct and 4 Nov 2026. Between 5 and 20 people marked each as helpful. They allege broken sales promises, no working support line, and a stalled refund of a seat-blocking fee. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)
- The same seat-blocking refund complaint appears on Trustpilot (18 Oct 2026) and Google Play (27 Oct 2026) in almost the same words. It is probably one family posting twice, so count it once. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com); [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)

**Complaints that involve young children (closest to Grade 3)**

- 2 May 2025: a parent says two sales representatives came to the home on 19 April 2025 to sell a "HOTS Program – Grade 2." The parent paid Rs 20,000 by credit card, the child never attended a class, and the refund was refused. Single complaint, no company reply. — [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/infinity-learn-non-refund-and-coercive-practices-by-infinity-learn-rs-20-000-loss-c3528403)
- 3 Oct 2026: a parent enrolled a Grade 4 child in a two-year digital programme, cancelled by email within days, and says the refund was not processed. Single complaint. — [ConsumerComplaints.in company page](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/bycompany/infinity-learn-a661721.html)
- 20 Jan 2026: a parent asks to cancel a subscription paid through March 2027 and to stop a standing instruction on a credit card. Single complaint. — [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/infinity-learn-no-response-from-infinity-learn-sri-chaitanya-c3538894)

### Inferences
- The praise is about human teachers in live classes. The complaints are about the selling and the contract. Neither tells us much about whether the app's own content or AI features teach well.
- The August 2026 run of short, happy reviews that name a teacher looks like students being asked to leave a review. That does not make them false. It does mean the 23% five-star share is not a random sample.
- Angry customers post more than content ones. The 60% one-star share overstates how many families are unhappy. But the complaints are specific, dated, and repeat the same pattern across three sites, which makes the pattern credible even if its size is unknown.
- What would prove this wrong: a company reply showing refunds were paid, or a store rating that rises once the featured complaints are answered.

### Gaps
- Apple App Store: I could not open the listing (404 on the app ID I had) and the iTunes lookup returned nothing. No App Store rating is reported here.
- MouthShut, Quora and Reddit: searches returned no Infinity Learn threads. I am not saying none exist. I could not find them.
- National Consumer Helpline: I found no public report that names Infinity Learn.
- I read 47 of the 94 Trustpilot reviews (pages 1 and 2). I could not load the raw Trustpilot page with a script (403), so dates come through a page reader. I cross-checked the year 2026 against a matching Google Play review.
- Google Play shows only three featured reviews without signing in. I did not see the full spread of the 6.46K reviews.

## 3. Are there documented concerns about sales, refunds, advertising, children's data, or staff treatment?

### Takeaway
Sales and refund complaints are well documented by users but not by any regulator or journalist. The company's own privacy policy and Google Play data declaration contradict each other. I found no layoff reports and no unpaid-salary reports for Infinity Learn. The parent group has serious, separately documented issues.

### Cited Findings

**Sales practices and refunds (Infinity Learn)**

- The official refund policy gives 7 calendar days only. For the Pro package the 7 days run from purchase. For the Ultimate package they run from the first class. The Pro+ package and anything sold at Rs 999 or less is non-refundable. A refund request counts only if sent by email from the registered address. — [Infinity Learn refund policy](https://infinitylearn.com/refund-policy)
- The policy says a user who paid by EMI, Buy Now Pay Later or a loan is "solely liable" for the lender's charges, including charges on refund. — [Infinity Learn refund policy](https://infinitylearn.com/refund-policy)
- A reviewer (1 May 2026) says "payment options are in loan form only either by credit card or bajaj finance loan." Single report. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)
- A Google Play reviewer (26 Oct 2026) alleges the company "secretly got a loan processed under my name without proper consent," promised a batch of 6 and delivered a class of over 200, and promised English but taught in Hindi. Single, unverified report. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)
- Pressure selling: the Grade 2 home-visit complaint says the parent was told a delay would lose the discount, was put straight onto a call with a "finance officer," and was sent a payment link repeatedly. Single complaint (2 May 2025). — [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/infinity-learn-non-refund-and-coercive-practices-by-infinity-learn-rs-20-000-loss-c3528403)
- Unwanted sales calls: one reviewer (19 Oct 2026) says agents "keep calling me even after several escalations." Another review site carries a similar "pushy" sales-call complaint (snippet only). — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com); [RealReviews.io](https://realreviews.io/reviews/infinitylearn.com)
- Refund terms not disclosed at sale: one reviewer (28 Aug 2026) says refund rules were revealed only when asked. — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.infinitylearn.com)

**Data privacy, including children (Infinity Learn)**

- The Google Play data safety section says: "The developer says that this app doesn't collect or share any user data." This is the developer's own declaration. — [Google Play data safety page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/datasafety?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)
- **[Conflict]** The company's privacy policy says it collects name, email, password, address PIN code, contact number, location from IP address and phone number, voice samples and audio recordings, and device location. It says "all video, audio and telecalls" with its representatives are recorded. — [Infinity Learn privacy policy](https://infinitylearn.com/privacy)
- The privacy policy sets the minimum age at 18. Under-18s "must use the Platform under the supervision of your parent or legal guardian." The product is sold for school children, including primary grades. — [Infinity Learn privacy policy](https://infinitylearn.com/privacy)
- The policy contains a consent to marketing calls, SMS and IVR that says it "will override any registration for Do Not Call (DNC) or National Do Not Call (NDNC) registry." — [Infinity Learn privacy policy](https://infinitylearn.com/privacy)
- The policy says personal information may go to group companies, to "third party service providers and marketing partners," and that third-party advertisers may use information about visits to show ads. — [Infinity Learn privacy policy](https://infinitylearn.com/privacy)
- The policy cites India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and a UAE data law. It does not mention India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or verifiable parental consent. I searched the page text for those terms and found none. — [Infinity Learn privacy policy](https://infinitylearn.com/privacy)
- The policy does give a deletion route by email to a privacy address. — [Infinity Learn privacy policy](https://infinitylearn.com/privacy)
- I found no reported data breach and no regulator action on privacy for Infinity Learn.

**Staff treatment (Infinity Learn)**

- Layoffs: a search found no report of layoffs at Infinity Learn. A startup database lists about 1,803 employees with a small rise over 90 days. Snippet only. — [Inc42 company profile](https://inc42.com/company/infinity-learn/people/)

**Advertising (Infinity Learn)**

- Results announcements are distributed as paid press releases through ANI, BusinessWire and NewsVoir and then appear on news sites. I found no final order or charge. — [A2Z Taxcorp reprint](https://a2ztaxcorp.net/income-tax-officials-estimate-over-rs-230-crore-tax-evasion-by-sri-chaitanya-group-of-colleges/)

### Inferences
- The Google Play "no data collected" declaration and the privacy policy cannot both be right. The app needs a login with a phone number. This is my own comparison of two primary sources. No journalist or regulator has reported it.
- A minimum age of 18 in the policy, for a product sold to children, shifts all responsibility to the parent on paper. It is a common pattern in Indian edtech. It is not a child-specific data safeguard.
- The pattern in the complaints matches the government's 2021 warning list point for point: loans, pressure, refunds, and promises made by sales staff (see section 5).
- Long hours and sales targets are reported by a few staff. I found nothing that shows unpaid wages or mass layoffs. The staff picture is ordinary for Indian edtech sales roles, on thin evidence.
- The parent group's tax and student-welfare issues are about its physical colleges. They bear on trust in the brand name. They are not evidence about the app.

### Gaps
- AmbitionBox and Glassdoor both blocked direct reading (403). All staff findings are from search snippets and may be out of date.
- No independent press investigation of Infinity Learn's sales practices was found in The Ken, The Morning Context, Entrackr, Inc42, Mint, Economic Times, MoneyControl, Rest of World or The News Minute. Absence of coverage is not a clean bill of health. It may only mean the company is too small to have been looked at.
- I did not find the outcome of the March 2025 tax searches.
- I did not check whether the company's separate apps (for example the school-facing "Infinity Meta" apps) have different data declarations.

## 4. How is the AI tutor or assistant described by independent reviewers?

### Takeaway
It is not. I found no independent review, test or audit of AINA, VISTA or any Infinity Learn AI feature. Everything known about accuracy and safety comes from the company.

### Cited Findings

- AINA was announced on 10 September 2025 as a "voice-first AI-powered mentor." The company claims 96.7% response accuracy, 5-second answers, nine Indian languages plus English, and a "closed-loop system" with "zero distractions." The source is a BusinessWire press release marked as advertorial. No measurement method is given. — [Tribune India, 10 Sep 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)
- The same release says AINA draws on "years of curated curriculum and student-performance data" and mentions work with Google Cloud and IIIT-Hyderabad. No details are given. — [Tribune India, 10 Sep 2025](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)
- A YourStory feature on AINA (Sep 2025) was described in search results as company-sponsored. It repeats the "nearly a million learners across 10,000+ schools" figure. ## 5. What is the wider Indian edtech context?

### Takeaway
Infinity Learn launched in 2021 at the peak of the boom. Since then funding has collapsed, the market leader has gone into insolvency, and both the education ministry and the consumer regulator have warned families about exactly the practices that Infinity Learn's reviewers describe.

### Cited Findings

- 23 December 2021: India's Ministry of Education issued an advisory to parents on edtech companies. It warned of companies "tempting and trapping parents in the name of free services." It told parents to avoid auto-debit, not to sign up for loans they do not understand, not to trust advertisements blindly, to check reviews, and to read the ministry's PRAGYATA child-safety guidelines. — [Careers360, 23 Dec 2021](https://news.careers360.com/be-careful-of-free-services-government-warns-parents-students-about-ed-tech-companies); [CBSE copy of the PIB advisory](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/documents//Press_Information_Bureau_Advisory_EDTECH.pdf)
- The advisory followed complaints on consumer forums about misrepresentation, broken terms and delayed refunds, and noted that edtech firms must follow the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. Snippet from the advisory text. — [CBSE copy of the PIB advisory](https://www.cbse.gov.in/cbsenew/documents//Press_Information_Bureau_Advisory_EDTECH.pdf)
- Byju's, once valued at about $22 billion, entered insolvency proceedings in July 2024 after a petition by the cricket board over Rs 158 crore unpaid. Its FY22 loss was Rs 8,245 crore. By early 2024 one investor valued it at about $1 billion. In August 2024 over 120 tuition centres were locked by landlords. — [Inc42, 16 Dec 2024](https://inc42.com/features/byjus-in-2024-how-the-22-bn-company-crumbled/)
- Funding fell sharply after 2021. One report gives $4.1 billion in 2021 and $2.6 billion in 2022. Another gives $2.9 billion in 2022 and about $712 million in 2023 (Tracxn data). Snippets only. **[Conflict]** on the 2022 figure. — [YourStory, Dec 2023](https://yourstory.com/2023/12/edtech-2023-review-byjus-unacademy-physicswallah-funding-layoffs); [Policy Circle](https://www.policycircle.org/economy/industry/edtech-post-byjus-fiasco/)
- Layoffs across the sector: Byju's cut 2,500 jobs in October 2022. Unacademy cut another 250 in July 2024. — [TechCrunch, 12 Oct 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/12/indian-edtech-giant-byjus-cuts-2500-jobs); [TechCrunch, 2 Jul 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/02/indian-edtech-unacademy-cuts-another-250-jobs)
- The consumer regulator has moved against misleading coaching advertisements. By February 2025 it had fined 24 coaching centres Rs 77.6 lakh in total. Its 2024 guidelines require ads to state the student's name, rank, course type and whether the course was paid. — [Business Standard, 14 Feb 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/india-news/iitpk-fined-misleading-iit-jee-results-ads-125021400896_1.html); [Taxmann summary](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/consumer-protection-authority-fines-24-coaching-centres-₹77-6-lakh-for-misleading-ads-urges-compliance-with-guidelines/)
- Infinity Learn's own positioning: launched June 2021 with $50 million from Sri Chaitanya. It leans on a "captive base" of learners inside Sri Chaitanya schools, which other edtech firms lack. — [YourStory, Jun 2021](https://yourstory.com/2021/06/sri-chaitanya-enters-edtech-space-with-infinity-learn); [YourStory, Oct 2023](https://yourstory.com/2023/10/sri-chaitanya-groups-growth-strategy-for-education-infinity-learn)

### Inferences
- Infinity Learn has survived where venture-funded rivals shrank because its parent funds it and supplies students. That is a business strength. It is not evidence of learning gains.
- The 2021 ministry advisory is a ready-made checklist. Against it, user reports about Infinity Learn raise flags on loans, auto-debit and standing instructions, sales promises, and refunds.
- The sector's history shows that large user numbers and rank lists were used by Byju's too, right up to its collapse. Those numbers alone should carry little weight for any company.
