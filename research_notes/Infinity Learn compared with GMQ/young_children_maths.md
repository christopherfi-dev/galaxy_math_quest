# Infinity Learn and its acquired products: maths for young children (about Grade 3, age 8)

Research date: 17 November 2026. All pages were read on that date unless a date is given.

How to read these notes:

- Every fact has a link. Quotes are copied from the linked page.
- "Company claim" means the company said it about itself. Nobody independent checked it.
- I could not log in to any app. Everything about what a child sees comes from store listings, company pages and reviews. I did not see a single real Grade 3 maths screen.
- Reviewer names are left out on purpose.

Short answer for the report writer:

- Infinity Learn's main business is JEE, NEET and Class 6 to 12 coaching.
- It does have real products for Grades 1 to 5. They are school products, not a consumer maths game.
  - **Infinity Meta Jr.** (K-5 app used by Sri Chaitanya Techno School pupils, 500,000+ Android downloads).
  - **Infinity Headstart** (a near-copy of the same app, linked to printed "Headstart" coursebooks for Classes 1 to 5).
  - **Free Class 3 maths worksheet PDFs** on the website.
- Wizklub still exists in 2026 as "WizKlub Futurz, an Infinity Learn by Sri Chaitanya initiative". It is now mainly a school coding, robotics and AI programme for Grades 1 to 9. Its HOTS programme is still on sale. Its "Head Start" course for Grades 1 to 3 is still reachable but hidden from the menu.
- Don't Memorise starts at Class 6. Its YouTube address now opens a NEET coaching channel.
- I found no narrative, no world, no exploration and no consequences for choices in any of these products. I found no points, badges, streaks, leaderboards or avatars described for the primary products. The game language used is only "games, quizzes, videos".

Yes, but it is a school companion app (Infinity Meta Jr., with a twin called Infinity Headstart) plus free worksheet PDFs, not a stand-alone Grade 3 maths course. ### Inferences

- Infinity Meta Jr. is the real Grade 1 to 5 product. It is an extension of school, not a home product. The evidence is the "SCTS" reviews, the school-set exams, and the fact that the listing never names a price or a subscription.
- Infinity Headstart is probably the same app re-badged for schools outside the Sri Chaitanya chain that buy the Headstart coursebooks. "Let's Scan" probably scans the printed book to open matching videos or activities. The evidence is the identical description, the 2024 release dates close to the coursebook dates, and the snippet that mentions a "Headstart App". This is my inference. No source states it.
- A Grade 3 maths "session" most likely looks like this: pick a subject and topic, watch a short video, do a quiz or simple game, and sit a timed multiple-choice test when the school sets one. This is pieced together from the listing and reviews. I did not see it.
- The US "Grades 3–8" text looks like search-engine copy for a tutoring service that is new or not yet built. It should not be treated as a product until a real page for it is found.

### Gaps

## 2. What did Wizklub offer (HOTS and SmartTech), how were sessions run for 8-year-olds, and is it still offered under Infinity Learn?

### Takeaway

Wizklub sold two things: HOTS (thinking-skill classes with app "workouts") and SmartTech (children build and code small devices). Both ran as small live online classes, twice a week, with app practice between classes. In 2026 it still trades as "WizKlub Futurz", published under Infinity Learn's developer account, but the weight has moved to an in-school coding, robotics and AI programme for Grades 1 to 9.

- Infinity Learn announced the Wizklub deal on 25 May 2022. Its own press release describes Wizklub as building skills "for school children of age 6-14 years through its HOTS (Higher Order Thinking Skills) and SmartTech programs". It launched a new arm called "Infinity Futurz". — [Infinity Learn press release](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-acquires-wizklub-for-10-million/)
- The press release says: "Over the next 24 months, Infinity Learn is looking at over 1 million early years students joining for a multi-year journey with Infinity Futurz." This was a forecast, not a result. — [Infinity Learn press release](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-acquires-wizklub-for-10-million/)
- **[Conflict]** The price is reported two ways. Infinity Learn's release says "$10 million". Other outlets say $7.5 million for a 75% stake. — [Infinity Learn press release](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/infinity-learn-by-sri-chaitanya-acquires-wizklub-for-10-million/); contradicted by [The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/business/infinity-learn-buys-majority-stake-in-wizklub-for-75-million-744974)

- The forecast of "over 1 million early years students" in 24 months was not met on the company's own numbers. The 2025 brochure claims 100,000+ learners.

## 3. What does Don't Memorise offer for primary maths, and at what grades does its content start?

### Takeaway

Don't Memorise is a library of short concept videos that starts at Class 6. It has no Grade 1 to 5 curriculum. Its website looks frozen since 2020, and its YouTube address now opens Sri Chaitanya's NEET coaching channel.

### Cited Findings

- The maths menu is "Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, High School Math". — [Don't Memorise website](https://dontmemorise.com/)
- The site also lists side material that a younger child could use: "Abacus" (Levels 1 and 2), "Warp Math" (Vedic mental maths: squaring, multiplication, division, roots), "Riddles", "Kyu Box" curiosity episodes and "One Minute Bites". — [Don't Memorise website](https://dontmemorise.com/)
- The formats listed are videos. The page does not mention quizzes or practice exercises. — [Don't Memorise website](https://dontmemorise.com/)
- The site says "Sign Up. It's Free". Its footer reads "© Copyright 2013 – 2020 Zouka Solutions Private Limited". It does not mention Infinity Learn. — [Don't Memorise website](https://dontmemorise.com/)
- Infinity Learn acquired Don't Memorise in March 2022 for an undisclosed price.
