# Infinity Learn by Sri Chaitanya: company and products (fact file, checked 17 November 2026)

How to read these notes:

- **(Company claim)** means the company, its press release or its own site says it. Nobody independent checked it.
- **(Independent)** means a news wire, a database or an app store reported it.
- **(Opened)** means I read the page myself. **(Search summary only)** means the page would not open and I only saw a search engine's summary of it. Treat those with more care.
- **[Conflict]** means two sources disagree. Both are listed.
- Anything dated before 2024 may be out of date. Edtech products change fast.
- Every list of "what I could not open" is in the Gaps part of each section, and again in full at the very end.

## 1. Who founded and runs Infinity Learn, how does it relate to Sri Chaitanya, and when did it launch?

### Takeaway
Infinity Learn is the online arm of the Sri Chaitanya school and coaching group of Hyderabad. It started in June 2021 with a stated USD 50 million from the group, is run by founding CEO Ujjwal Singh, and trades through a company called Rankguru Technology Solutions Private Limited.

### Cited Findings
- (Company claim, opened, FAQ page undated, read Nov 2026) "Infinity Learn is the Ed-Tech arm of Sri Chaitanya Educational Institutions." It launched in 2021. Listed founders: Sushma Boppana, Sridhar Yalamanchili and Seema Boppana. CEO: Ujjwal Singh. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Company claim, opened) Rankguru Technology Solutions Private Limited holds the "Infinity Learn by Sri Chaitanya" trademark and operates under it. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Independent, opened) Apple's App Store names the seller of an Infinity Learn app as "Rankguru Technology Solutions Private Limited". This confirms the legal entity. — [App Store: Infinity Headstart](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6476543527)
- (Independent, PTI wire, 21 Sept 2023, opened; older than 2024) Infinity Learn "commenced operations in June 2021". — [PTI via Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/2602677-infinity-learn-turns-profitable-targets-rs-500-cr-biz-by-2025)
- (Independent press on a company announcement, June 2021, search summary only; older than 2024) Sri Chaitanya announced Infinity Learn with USD 50 million (about Rs 370 crore) earmarked. — [YourStory, June 2021](https://yourstory.com/2021/06/sri-chaitanya-enters-edtech-space-with-infinity-learn); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/atom/sri-chaitanya-group-enters-edtech-space-infinity-learn-151242)
- (Company press release, 10 Sept 2025, opened) Titles used in 2025: Sushma Boppana is "CEO and Director, Sri Chaitanya Educational Institutions". Ujjwal Singh is "Founding CEO, Infinity Learn". — [Tribune India, advertorial via BusinessWire India and ANI](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)
- (Independent, Wikipedia, opened; use as a pointer only) Sri Chaitanya was founded in 1986 in Vijayawada by Dr Boppana Satyanarayana Rao and Dr Jhansi Lakshmi Bai Boppana. Rao died on 13 July 2023. The group lists over 321 junior colleges, 322 Techno Schools, 107 CBSE schools and "over 8.5 lakh students" (850,000). — [Wikipedia: Sri Chaitanya Educational Institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Chaitanya_Educational_Institutions)
- (Independent, Wikipedia, opened) The Infinity Learn app launched in 2022 with content for classes 6 to 12 and coaching for IIT-JEE, NEET and CBSE boards. Cricketer Rohit Sharma led the marketing. In December 2024 Infinity Learn partnered with Google Cloud India on AI. — [Wikipedia: Sri Chaitanya Educational Institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Chaitanya_Educational_Institutions)
- (Company claim, opened) Brand ambassadors are Rohit Sharma and chess player Gukesh Dommaraju. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Company claim, opened) Head office is in Hyderabad. Corporate office is in Bengaluru. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Independent, opened) Inc42 lists the company as founded 2021, Hyderabad, private, "Bootstrapped", with founders "Seema Boppana and Sridhar". — [Inc42 company page](https://inc42.com/company/infinity-learn/acquisitions/)
- (Search summary only) Ujjwal Singh previously worked at Pearson and Shine.com. — [FirmSuggest review, no citations, "pricing as of August 2025"](https://www.firmsuggest.com/blog/infinity-learn-cutting-through-the-hype-in-indias-edtech-rush/)
- **[Conflict]** Who counts as "founder". The company FAQ lists three Sri Chaitanya family and group figures as founders. Tracxn (search summary only) lists "Sushma Boppana and Ujjwal Singh" as co-founders and CEOs. Press calls Ujjwal Singh "Founding CEO". — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs); [Tracxn profile, not opened](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/infinity-learn/__QIik1vibTddABlwGid67nr7u95KVhxXTJ1bEk5srt-A)
- **[Conflict]** Ownership wording. One FAQ answer says Sri Chaitanya owns Infinity Learn. Another says Rankguru holds the trademark. The page does not explain how the two fit. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)

### Inferences
- Infinity Learn is a group-funded subsidiary business, not a venture-backed startup. Inc42's "Bootstrapped" label and the absence of any named outside investor in every source both point that way.
- The owning family (Boppana) holds the founder titles. Ujjwal Singh is the hired operating head who has run it since the start.
- Sri Chaitanya's own schools and colleges give Infinity Learn a ready-made base of students. YourStory's October 2023 feature said this directly (search summary only): it "benefits from a captive base of learners within Sri Chaitanya schools". — [YourStory, Oct 2023, not opened](https://yourstory.com/2023/10/sri-chaitanya-groups-growth-strategy-for-education-infinity-learn)

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the exact shareholding of Rankguru Technology Solutions. No filing was readable.
- I found no Wikipedia page for Infinity Learn itself (the URL returned 404). Facts come from the parent group's page.
- I could not confirm whether Ujjwal Singh is still CEO after September 2025. The latest source naming him is the 10 Sept 2025 press release.
- LinkedIn was not checked. It needs a login.

## 2. Which grades and exams does it serve? Is the centre of gravity JEE / NEET / CBSE grades 6 to 12, or does it truly serve grades 1 to 5?

### Takeaway
The centre of gravity is clearly classes 6 to 12 plus "droppers" (repeaters), aimed at JEE, NEET and CBSE boards. Primary grades appear only at the edges: a thinly documented K-5 app called Infinity Headstart, a scholarship test open from grade 3, and maths help pages for grades 3 to 8.

### Cited Findings
- (Company claim, opened) Programmes cover "Classes 6 to 12" with JEE, NEET and CBSE board preparation. One answer also mentions CUET. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Company claim, opened, Nov 2026) The courses page heading reads "Affordable Microcourses for Class 6 to 12+: CBSE, Foundation, JEE & NEET". — [Infinity Learn courses page](https://infinitylearn.com/courses)
- (Independent, opened, Nov 2026) The Google Play app is titled "Infinity Learn-JEE, NEET, CBSE". — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)
- (Independent, PTI, June 2024, search summary only) Headline: "Infinity Learn focuses on tapping 6th-12th grade users". The story ties the focus to government limits on physical coaching for children under 16. — [PTI via LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/latest-news-infinity-learn-focuses-on-tapping-6th-12th-grade-users-aims-to-double-revenue-to-rs-400-cr-this-fiscal-6042239.html); [Business Standard, 16 June 2024](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/infinity-learn-targets-6th12th-graders-aims-to-double-revenue-in-fy25-124061600476_1.html)
- (Company claim, 10 Sept 2025, opened) AINA stands for "AI NEET/JEE Assistant". The flagship AI feature is built for the two entrance exams. — [Tribune India advertorial](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation); [CollegeDekho](https://www.collegedekho.com/news/aina-india-voice-first-ai-mentor-infinity-learn-71197/)
- (Search summary only) The Google Play description is reported to say it covers "From Grade 1 all the way to 12 and repeaters". I could not open the description to check the wording. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?gl=GB&hl=en-GB&id=com.infinitylearn.learn)
- (Independent, opened) Infinity Headstart is a free app, tagline "Early learning for K-5 kids!", seller Rankguru Technology Solutions. Apple labels it "Made for Ages 6–8". The whole description is one line: it has "3 major learning sections for kids for overall development" named Let's Explore, Let's Scan and Let's Learn. First release 23 Feb 2024. Latest version 1.0.8 on 25 June 2025. It has too few ratings for Apple to show a score. — [App Store: Infinity Headstart](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6476543527)
- (Independent, opened) The same developer account lists five apps: WizKlub: AI, Robotics, Coding; Infinity Headstart; Infinity Meta Teacher; Infinity Meta Jr. ("Early learning for K-5 kids!"); and Infinity Meta. — [App Store developer page](https://apps.apple.com/in/developer/infinity-learn-learning-app/id1642492196)
- (Company claim, opened) SCORE is a free online scholarship exam for students in grades 3 to 12. The FAQ cites scholarships "worth up to ₹1 crore" and a start date of 9 October 2024. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Company claim, opened, Nov 2026, seen from a US internet address with a "US" country selector) The home page offers "online math help" for Grades 3–8: multiplication, fractions, decimals, word problems, geometry, ratios, percentages, equations and pre-algebra. The page title still names K-12 test prep, IIT-JEE and NEET. — [infinitylearn.com home page](https://infinitylearn.com/)
- (Independent, PTI, 21 Sept 2023, opened; older than 2024) The company planned to "scale up" online maths and science tuition for classes 4 to 8. No numbers were given. — [PTI via Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/education/2602677-infinity-learn-turns-profitable-targets-rs-500-cr-biz-by-2025)
- (Independent press, May 2022, search summary only; older than 2024) WizKlub, bought in 2022, offers HOTS (Higher Order Thinking Skills) and SmartTech programmes for children aged 6 to 14. — [Education21](https://education21.in/infinity-learn-acquires-wizklub-for-10-million-launches-a-new-vertical-infinity-futurz/)
- (Independent press, March 2022, search summary only; older than 2024) Don't Memorise, bought in 2022, makes short maths and science videos, "specifically grades 6 to 10". — [EdTechReview](https://edtechreview.in/?p=44748)

### Inferences
- Infinity Learn's paying business is exam coaching for teenagers. Every priced product I found is a JEE or NEET course for classes 11, 12 or repeaters.
- Grade 3 children can touch the company in only a few ways: the SCORE scholarship test, WizKlub's thinking-skills and coding programmes, the Headstart app, and maths help pages. None of these is the main product.
- Infinity Headstart and Infinity Meta Jr. look like companion apps for Sri Chaitanya's own schools, not open consumer products. The evidence: a section called "Let's Scan", update notes about login and OTP (one-time password) flows, a sibling "Infinity Meta Teacher" app, and almost no public ratings. This is my inference. No source states it.
- The grades 3 to 8 maths pages may be a separate push at overseas (US) families. PTI in June 2024 mentioned overseas expansion. I found no press report confirming a US launch.

### Gaps
- I could not read the Google Play description, so the "Grade 1 to 12" wording is unconfirmed.
- I found no description of what a child actually does inside Infinity Headstart or Infinity Meta Jr. No screenshots were readable and no review exists.
- I could not open the free study material pages (404), so I cannot say whether free worksheets or NCERT solutions exist for classes 1 to 5.
- I do not know whether Indian visitors see the same grades 3 to 8 maths text on the home page. My view came from a US address.

## 3. What exactly are its products and named features today, and how does each work for a learner?

### Takeaway
The core product is a paid live-class course for JEE or NEET, delivered through the "Infinity Learn" app and website, wrapped with recorded videos, printed books, test series, 24×7 doubt solving and, since September 2025, a voice AI mentor called AINA. Around this sit free study material, a scholarship test, a few offline centres, a schools programme, and the WizKlub brand.

### Cited Findings

**Main app and website**
- (Independent, opened) Android app name: "Infinity Learn-JEE, NEET, CBSE", package `com.infinitylearn.learn`. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.infinitylearn.learn&hl=en_IN)
- (Search summary only) Store listings describe "Live Classes — Attend live classes with expert faculty", recorded classes, and "Self-Study Videos — Thousands of concept-wise videos mapped to your syllabus". Another listing adds learning materials, assessment modules, test series, NCERT solutions and instant doubt resolution. — [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?gl=GB&hl=en-GB&id=com.infinitylearn.learn); [Microsoft Store listing](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9p5ttx9504dk?hl=en-US&gl=US)
- (Company claim, opened) The Learn App is on Google Play and the App Store. It gives study materials, doubt resolution and tests. It is free to download. Full access for longer periods needs a purchase. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)
- (Company claim, opened, Nov 2026) Site menu items: courses, study material, score (or "results"), more, sign in. A sales phone line is shown on every page. — [infinitylearn.com home page](https://infinitylearn.com/)

**Live courses (the paid core)**
- (Company claim, opened, Nov 2026) Example: "NEET Grade 11 & 12 Live Full Course - DC Pandey (2026-28)". Two years. Valid to 31 May 2028. The table lists "Live classes + books, Infinity Learn Test Series, mock tests". — [NEET class 11 course page](https://infinitylearn.com/neet-class-11-online-course)
- (Company claim, opened) The same page promises two-way live sessions, live doubt-clearing classes, "unlimited recorded video access", printed books, e-notes, question banks, concept videos, DPPs (daily practice problems), one-shot revision and flashcards. — [NEET class 11 course page](https://infinitylearn.com/neet-class-11-online-course)

**Microcourses**

- (Company claim, opened) Mock tests, time-bound quizzes, practice tests, rank predictors and past-year papers. Regular tests copy exam conditions. — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)

**Doubt solving**
- (Company claim, opened) Live doubt solving comes with all offerings. A "doubts app offers live chat 24x7". — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)

**AINA, the AI mentor (launched 10 September 2025)**
- (Company press release, opened) AINA is a "voice-first, AI-powered mentor" for NEET and JEE preparation. The name means "AI NEET/JEE Assistant". — [Tribune India advertorial](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation); [CollegeDekho](https://www.collegedekho.com/news/aina-india-voice-first-ai-mentor-infinity-learn-71197/)

The release gives it an "elder-sister" persona. — [Tribune India advertorial](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)

Weaknesses listed: little beyond science and maths; "basic tech compared to AI-driven rivals such as Embibe"; the mobile site sometimes logs users out. — [FirmSuggest review](https://www.firmsuggest.com/blog/infinity-learn-cutting-through-the-hype-in-indias-edtech-rush/)

### Inferences
- The product is a digital copy of a coaching institute: timetable of live classes, books, daily practice sheets, weekly tests, rank predictions, parent meetings. The learner's path is set by the exam syllabus, not by play or exploration.
- AINA is the only clearly new product idea since 2023. It is a tutor and coach for exam students, not a world, game or story. Its "closed system" design (answers tied to the company's own content, for safety and focus) is the one idea that sits close to a constrained-AI approach.
- Motivation in this product comes from exam stakes, ranks and scholarships. I saw nothing aimed at making practice feel like play for a young child.
- The VISTA name seems to have been dropped or folded into AINA. No source says so; it simply stops appearing.

### Gaps
- I could not read the app store descriptions in full (Google Play pages came back cut short; the Apple page for the main app returned 404; AppBrain and Apptopia refused). Feature names come from search summaries and the company site.
- I could not confirm any gamification, streaks, rewards or avatar system. Its absence from my sources is not proof it does not exist inside the app.
- YouTube channels were not checked. I have no subscriber counts for Infinity Learn's own channels.
- I could not open the "study material" pages, so the free content list (NCERT solutions, worksheets, formula sheets) is not confirmed by class.
- I could not find out what Infinity Meta and Infinity Meta Teacher do.
- The current number of offline centres and partner schools is unknown. The 2023 plans (40 centres, 400 to 500 schools) were targets, and I found no report on whether they were met. The "10,000+ schools" figure of 2025 is a company claim that may count schools where any paid learner studies.
- One search summary said an Infinity Learn app "was removed from Google Play Aug 17, 2024". The Play listing for `com.infinitylearn.learn` did resolve in November 2026, so the claim may refer to a different, older app. Unresolved.

## 4. Which companies did it acquire or invest in, when, and are those products still running?

### Takeaway
Three acquisitions, all within eight months of 2021 and 2022: Teacherr, Don't Memorise and WizKlub. WizKlub is the only one with visible signs of life in 2025 and 2026. I found no reported acquisition after May 2022.

### Cited Findings
- (Independent, opened) Inc42 lists exactly three acquisitions: WizKlub (edtech, Bengaluru, founded 2018, USD 9.31 million raised before, founder Amit Bansal and two others); Don't Memorise (edtech, Mumbai, founded 2013, founder Ganesh Pai); Teacherr (Hyderabad, founded 2019, founder Harsha Teja and two others). No investments are listed. — [Inc42 acquisitions page](https://inc42.com/company/infinity-learn/acquisitions/)
- (Company claim, opened) The FAQ names the same three: "Teacher, WizKlub (May 2022), and Don't Memorize". — [Infinity Learn FAQ](https://infinitylearn.com/surge/faqs)

**Teacherr (October 2021; older than 2024)**
- (Independent press, search summary only) Hyderabad-based community platform for teachers. Bought for an undisclosed amount. — [YourStory, Oct 2021](https://yourstory.com/2021/10/infinity-learn-acquires-edtech-startup-teacherr)

**Don't Memorise (2022; older than 2024)**
- (Independent press, search summary only) Concept-based content platform. Short curriculum-aligned videos, mostly maths and science, grades 6 to 10, in English, Hindi, Marathi and Bengali. Price undisclosed. At the time it claimed 2.6 million YouTube subscribers and over 250 million views. — [EdTechReview](https://edtechreview.in/?p=44748); [Venture Intelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/ma/edtech-startup-infinity-learn-acquires-k-10-content-platform-don%27t-memorise)
- **[Conflict]** Date. EdTechReview dates the news to 8 March 2022. An ANI release carried by ZEE5 is dated September 2022. Wikipedia says 2022, citing BusinessLine. — [EdTechReview](https://edtechreview.in/?p=44748); [ZEE5/ANI](https://zee5.com/articles/?p=5215763); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Chaitanya_Educational_Institutions)

**WizKlub (May 2022; older than 2024)**
- (Independent press, search summary only) Bengaluru startup teaching thinking skills (HOTS) and "SmartTech" (coding, robotics) to ages 6 to 14. About 100 staff were to join under a new vertical called "Infinity Futurz". WizKlub was to keep running as a separate unit. — [YourStory, May 2022](https://yourstory.com/2022/05/edtech-startup-infinity-learn-acquires-wizklub-10-million); [Education21](https://education21.in/infinity-learn-acquires-wizklub-for-10-million-launches-a-new-vertical-infinity-futurz/)

Wikipedia also says a 75% stake. ## 5. What does it cost, and what is the business model?

### Takeaway
It sells long, prepaid course packages, not monthly subscriptions. Full JEE or NEET live courses run from roughly Rs 60,000 for one year to about Rs 1.3 to 1.65 lakh for two years, with cheaper crash courses, "microcourses" and test series below that, and a free tier used to pull students in.

### Cited Findings
- (Company claim, opened, Nov 2026) NEET Grade 11 and 12 Live Full Course, DC Pandey batch 2026-28, two years: fee after discount Rs 1,64,420. No list price was shown. — [NEET class 11 course page](https://infinitylearn.com/neet-class-11-online-course)
- (Company pages, search summary only, 2026) Class 11 NEET course: Rs 59,999 for one year; Rs 1,29,999 for the two-year class 11 and 12 option. — [NEET class 11 course page](https://infinitylearn.com/neet-class-11-online-course)
- (Company pages, search summary only, 2026) Class 12 NEET one-year course: Rs 74,999, including live classes, books, test series, mock exams and 24×7 doubt solving. — [NEET class 12 course page](https://infinitylearn.com/neet-class-12-online-course)
- (Company pages, search summary only, 2026) "Atreya" NEET repeater course, valid to May 2027: Rs 98,140. — [NEET repeater page](https://infinitylearn.com/neet-class-13-online-course); [Infinity Learn Q&A on repeater fees](https://infinitylearn.com/question-answer/what-are-the-fees-for-neet-repeaters-batch-2026)
- **[Conflict]** The class 11 page showed me Rs 1,64,420 for two years. The search summary of the same site gave Rs 1,29,999 for a two-year class 11 and 12 option. These may be different batches (a named-teacher batch against a standard one), or the price changed. Not resolved.
- (Third party, search summary only, listing about 18 months old) Collegedunia lists JEE online programmes for class 11, class 12 and droppers at roughly Rs 28,499 to Rs 90,949, and JEE test series from about Rs 8,099. — [Collegedunia](https://collegedunia.com/institute/8885-sri-chaitanya-infinity-learn-hyderabad/courses-fees)

Positioned as dearer than Physics Wallah and cheaper than BYJU'S. — [FirmSuggest review](https://www.firmsuggest.com/blog/infinity-learn-cutting-through-the-hype-in-indias-edtech-rush/)
- **[Conflict]** The reviewer's 2025 prices (Rs 35,000 to 40,000 a year) are about half the 2026 prices on the company site (Rs 59,999 to 74,999 a year). Either prices rose, or the reviewer quoted recorded or discounted versions. Not resolved.

- (Company claim, 10 Sept 2025, opened) AINA is open to non-paying users too. — [Tribune India advertorial](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/infinity-learn-unveils-aina-indias-first-voice-first-ai-powered-mentor-to-revolutionize-exam-preparation)

### Inferences
- The model is "free content and a scholarship test at the top, a phone sales team in the middle, a prepaid one- or two-year course at the bottom". The sales phone number on every page and the "talk to experts" prompt support this.
- A large share of "paid learners" probably comes through schools, where the school or parent pays a far lower fee per child than the consumer course prices. Simple arithmetic supports this: about Rs 200 crore of revenue (FY24) spread over 750,000 to 1,000,000 paid learners is only about Rs 2,000 to Rs 2,700 per learner per year. That is far below Rs 60,000. This is my inference, not a reported fact.
- Parents are the buyers and exam results are the promise. Pricing is set against coaching institutes, not against children's learning apps.
