# Character test 3: the ordinary Mender, for the crowd

**Date:** 24 November 2026. **For:** Marc, to paste into the same Gemini chat. **Nothing in the game was changed.**

**Marc's ask** [Source: Marc, 24 November]: "Give me this. 'The ordinary Mender for the crowd: shall I write those prompts?'"

**The idea** [Proposal]: one ordinary Mender, drawn once by Gemini, then turned into each Mender of the crowd in code: another copper, a hat, an apron, something to carry. Named characters (Elder Thimble, Pim) keep their own pictures.

**The crowd in the big world today** [Built]: a gardener, a fisher, "a Mender with big ears" (the listener), "an old Mender" (she tells of her gran and the old stones), a reed cutter, the orchard keeper, and the Mender at the first lesson.

**Learned from tests 1 and 2:**
- Open, waving hands get a fifth digit. So these prompts ask for hands that hold, point or rest.
- The green background cuts away cleanly. Kept.
- Gemini keeps a character when the prompt names what must stay the same. Kept.

**Left for later:** the listener's "big ears". Menders have no ears; perhaps bigger handles. That is a story question first.

## Prompt 9 (the ordinary Mender, and the character sheet)

```
NEW CHARACTER, SAME WORLD. Keep exactly the same drawing style, outline and kind of body as Elder Thimble and Pim in the pictures above. This is an ordinary grown-up Mender: not anyone special, just one of the village folk. I will use this one picture for many different Menders, so keep him plain.

HIS BODY: like every Mender, a copper pot with the same two curved pot handles, the same separate arms below the handles with small round hands (three stubby fingers and a thumb on every hand, always), a dark brown band round his middle, and short brown feet.
- the same height as Elder Thimble, a little slimmer
- a middle copper colour (about #C9793A): darker than Pim, lighter than Elder Thimble; smooth, with no dents, no scratches and no patterns, so that the colour can be changed later
- a plain lid that FITS him well, sitting straight, with the same small round gold knob on top; no dent
- NO beard, NO moustache, NO hair; thin drawn eyebrows only
- big round white eyes with dark navy pupils, and a friendly, calm mouth
- nothing on him: no hat, no apron, no tools, no badges

DRAWING STYLE: exactly the same as before: a clean, flat 2D cartoon, a bold dark navy outline (about #0E1626) of even thickness around every shape, flat colours with one darker shade and one small white shine on the upper left, light from the upper left.

AS BEFORE, FOR EVERY PICTURE:
- a plain, flat, bright green background (#00FF00), the same green everywhere: no floor, no shadow, no frame
- no green anywhere on the characters or on what they hold
- no words, no letters, no numbers, no labels and no signature anywhere
- whole bodies, with space all round
- hands holding, pointing or resting; if a hand is open, it has exactly three fingers and a thumb

PICTURE 9: THE ORDINARY MENDER'S CHARACTER SHEET.
One wide picture (16:9). Show him four times in a row, standing, all the same size, with even gaps:
1. from the front
2. turned three-quarters to his right
3. from the side
4. from the back (no face)
```

## Prompt 10 (his four feelings, the same four as Elder Thimble's)

```
PICTURE 10: THE ORDINARY MENDER'S FOUR FEELINGS.
The same ordinary Mender as in picture 9: the same middle copper pot, the same plain lid that fits, the same eyes, handles, arms and three-fingered hands, the same colours, outline and style. Do not change his shape, size or colours.

One wide picture (16:9), plain flat bright green background (#00FF00), no shadow, no words. Show him four times in a row, all the same size, whole body, standing the same way (turned three-quarters to his right):
1. HAPPY: a warm smile, one hand giving a thumbs-up (three fingers curled, the thumb up)
2. WORRIED: eyebrows raised in the middle, a small frown, both hands held together in front of his band
3. THINKING: eyes looking up and to one side, one finger tapping his chin
4. SURPRISED AND PLEASED: eyes wide, mouth open in a small round "oh", both hands pressed to his cheeks
Only his face and hands change.
```

## Prompt 11 (the things Menders wear and carry, as separate pieces)

```
PICTURE 11: THINGS MENDERS WEAR AND CARRY.
Separate objects only, no characters. Draw each one on its own, with plenty of green space between them, in the same flat cartoon style with the same bold dark navy outline. Each is sized to fit the ordinary Mender in picture 9 (a hat fits his lid; an apron fits the front of his pot).

One wide picture (16:9), plain flat bright green background (#00FF00), no shadows, no words, letters or numbers. Two rows:
Row 1:
- a wide straw sun hat, seen from the front, with a hole in its top for a lid's knob
- a cloth apron with one pocket, seen from the front, with a neck strap and waist ties
- a woollen shawl, folded in a triangle, seen from the front
- a small round watering can
Row 2:
- a simple fishing rod made of a stick, with a line and a hook
- a bundle of long reeds, tied in the middle with string
- a round wicker basket full of small round red fruit
- a small wooden toolbox with a handle on top
Nothing touches anything else. No green on any object.
```
