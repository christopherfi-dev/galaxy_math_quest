## 1. What each player needs

- **A ship of five parts:** Engine Core, Navigation, Shield Generator, Weapons System and Power Module.
  - Each part has HP (health) and AP (attack).
  - A ship of five Standard parts has 100 HP and 55 AP. That is worked out from your tables.
- **A Main Shield of 100 HP.** [20 November: "Shields 100 hp."]
  - **[Open]** Does it still grow to 140, 220 and 360 at Shield Generator Levels 3, 6 and 10?
- **A deck,** built from the player's own cards. It has no limit on size or kind. Spy and Policeman cards may go in.
- **A hand of 5 cards.**
  - **[Open]** Does it grow with the Engine Core level: 6 at Level 3, 7 at Level 6, 8 at Level 10?

## 2. Setting up

- Each player draws a full hand.
- Both shields start whole.

## 3. A round

[20 November: "They go same time."]

1. Both players put down one card.
2. Both cards take effect.
3. Both ships attack. A ship's attack is the AP of all its parts added up, plus any bonus from the card it played.
4. Played cards go to the bottom of their player's deck, so nobody runs out.
5. Each player draws back up to their hand size.

**A card that cannot be used is still played.** One example is an attack card after Weapons is lost. Nothing happens, and it goes to the bottom of the deck.

**[Open] Same time, or one leads?** Your Spy and Policeman rules (section 6) need one player to lead and the other to answer. Choices:
- (a) Both always put down cards together, and the Spy and Policeman rules change.
- (b) Both put down together, except when someone plays a Spy. Then the other may answer with a Policeman.
- (c) One player leads and the other answers, taking turns to lead (your 15 November words).

## 4. Damage

- **The shield first.** Attacks hit the Main Shield first. It blocks everything except Spy cards.
- **Nothing carries over.** If an attack is bigger than what is left of the shield, the shield breaks and soaks up the whole attack that round.
- **Then the parts.** Once the shield is gone, the defender decides how to split each attack across their parts. [20 November: "Defender chopped."]
  - Example: an 80-point attack can be 16 on each of five parts, so all five survive.
  - Or the whole 80 can go onto one part with 5 HP left. That part is lost, and the other 75 points are lost with it.

## 5. A part at 0 HP

A lost part adds 0 AP. Its penalty, from your board:

| Part | If HP = 0 |
|---|---|
| Weapons System | cannot attack (no AP attack, and no attack cards) |
| Engine Core | lose 1 card in hand |
| Shield Generator | cannot defend |
| Navigation | lose 1 card in hand |
| Power Module | cannot heal HP |

- **[Open]** "Lose 1 card in hand": one card thrown away at that moment, or a hand one smaller for the rest of the battle? Two parts say it, so it can happen twice.
- **[Open]** "Cannot defend": defence cards only, or does the Main Shield stop working too?
- **Revival:** some cards bring a lost part back. It gets its AP back, and its penalty ends.

## 6. Spy and Policeman

- **A Spy is sabotage.** It gets past the shield. Its owner chooses which parts it hits.
- **A Spy works even after its owner has lost Weapons.**
- **A Policeman has one job:** to cancel a Spy. It uses up its player's card for that round.
- **If no Policeman answers,** the Spy acts first, before anything else in the round.
- **[Open]** When a Spy may be played depends on section 3's open choice.

## 7. The end of a battle

- **A win:** a ship is beaten when all five of its parts are at 0. [20 November: "All parts zero."]
- **Nobody wins:** this happens as soon as neither player can win any more. For example, both ships have lost Weapons, and neither player has a Spy or a Weapons-revival card left to play.

## 8. Two kinds of battle

- **Fun battle:** nothing at stake.
- **Challenge battle:** something is at stake.
  - **[Open]** What is it? Your two ideas so far: each player puts up an epic card and the winner takes the loser's; or the winner takes one of the loser's cards, drawn at random.
  - A battle nobody wins moves no card.
