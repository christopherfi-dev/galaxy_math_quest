# The bag: will it get too big?

**Date:** 2026-11-20
**For:** Marc
**You asked,** with a photo of your board: "The bag will have too much stuff soon. This is me only jumping around. But if I do every mission on every planet, will this be way too big soon?"
**Marks:** [Source] is from the game as built. [Proposal] is mine.

Nothing here is built.

---

## 1. The short answer

- **Not today.** The most a child can ever carry is 10 things. Ten fits on every iPad and on your board.
- **But you have found a real fault in how the bag works.** Nothing leaves it when you leave a place. And it shows things that do nothing where you stand.
- **It breaks at 11 to 14 things** on an iPad mini held sideways, depending on the room. One more place built like the station or Voltara would get there.

## 4. The choices [Proposal]

| | Choice | What a child sees | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Leave it** | as now | nothing. It holds until 11 to 14 things. |
| B | **The bag shows only what belongs here** | on the mission ship, an empty bag. On the station, the station's things. The rest wait in "My ship". | an evening. A child can no longer try a wrench on another planet. |
| C | **One bag, folded** | things from this place first. Then one button: "+ 5 more". A tap opens the rest. | an evening. Nothing is lost. The row never grows past two lines. |
| D | **A rule for new places** | no change now | nothing to build. Each new place must say which things are used up, which are left behind, and which few are keepsakes. |

**My pick, if you want one:** C, and D as a rule from now on.

**Why not B:** you said big quests can spiral back. A thing from one planet that opens something on another is that idea in a child's hand. B rules it out. C keeps it.

2. **The Mission button.** It shows Voltara's mission, wherever you are. On the "mission ship" that is confusing. [Proposal] Call it "Voltara mission", or show it only on Voltara.
3. **"Sound: off" sits alone on its own line** under Hint. It wastes a row. [Proposal] Move it under More.

## 6. What could prove this wrong

- **Ten is today's count.** The card battle, new planets or a class project could each add things. The limit is the row, not the number ten.
- **I measured in a test browser,** not on your board or a real iPad mini. Real screens lose a little height to the browser's own bars.
- **A folded bag hides things.** A child may forget what they carry. That is a cost of C.
