## 1. The review

The story thread asked for an independent review of section 4 before the finds are built. One reviewer, with no stake in either thread, read `CLAUDE.md`, the peoples note (sections 0 to 4, and 8), the empty places note, and the page as it is today ("big world 12": Elder Thimble; the old stones under the water until Pim's secret; the two Year Machine lines). It ran `checks/bigworld_empty_places.js`. It edited nothing.

**Verdict: "Yes, with these fixes."** The four places and the four tellers match the page:
- **D:** the north-west copse, just south of the old wall. Teller: Elder Thimble, in the village, the same bank.
- **E:** x 50 to 53, y 59 to 67, where the old stones land. **The path runs down x = 52, so the find must sit beside the path, not on it.** Teller: the old Mender by the big tree, **on the other bank**.
- **G:** the beach below the orchard. Teller: the orchard keeper, the same bank.
- **H:** x 50 to 54, y 76 to 79, south of the bridge's landing, by the reeds. Teller: the guard, the same bank.

I checked three of its claims myself, and they hold:
- The page's own check faults a find that "leads nowhere and is not marked scenery".
- "Pebbles" already means the grey test pebbles at every machine ("Pebbles in:").
- The three peoples' names (Twinseers, Furrows, Eights) are only in the page's data. No line a child reads says them.

### Blockers

1. **What a find gives was undecided.** Every find on the page gives a card. **Decided by Marc on 24 November: a card for each of the four.** It comes after the teller's story is read.
2. **D invites the wrong act.** A child sees a window frame and a fallen shutter among Menders who mend. They will try "mend" or "fix" (which mean "use" on this page) and "take". The page's other answers say "you leave it". The custom that allows taking it ("a shutter that falls is brought home") is said by nobody. **Fix (words):** the frame's answer to use, mend and take says the custom, and points to the village.

### Major

1. **E's teller is on the other bank,** 30 to 40 steps back over the bridge or the stones, and nothing at E points to her. Fix: a pointer line at E, or a teller in the Reach.
2. **The names arrive cold.** "Twinseers", "Furrow" and "Eights" are new to a child. No line ties the Twinseers to the lake. G never names the S.S. Infinity, the ship whose call brought the child here. Fix: one clause each.
3. **"Pebbles" clashes** with the grey test pebbles. And H's story ends at the hill lift, whose sign says "IT RISES ON THE RIGHT COUNT": pebbles, a count and the lift together read like a lock. Fix: another word for the Furrows' tokens, and drop the lift line.
4. **G reads as the hedge's knock.** "Eight of us, as always", then "we leave food by the hedge", then the hedge's "KNOCK THE WAY THE WOODS COUNT": a child will try "knock 8". Fix: cut the hedge from the keeper's line, or the Woods design must allow for it.
5. **A hole a child can push:** "I was there the day it burnt." The bridge went up the same day. How did the elder get home? Keep the decision; add the answer to section 5.
6. **The elder's order.** The elder answers in a fixed order (welcome, pouch, Rule Stone). Shown the shutter at the wrong moment, he would give the pouch speech. **The build's answer is in section 2.**
7. **The painting is only words.** The painted board fills the picture; the shutter should too. Drawing it fixes a first look of the Year Machine before the Lake is designed. **Section 2.**
8. **Mechanics.** "Show" exists: it is "give", as with the shard and the coin. A yes or no box exists (the fresh pouch). "Tell about…" is new: no talk on the page offers a choice. **Section 2.**
9. **Reading.** The elder's card has 5 sentences and 135 characters (the page's check allows 120 for a spoken line). "Gold month" is not on the page. "The suns' word" is never explained. "The Year Machine took their word" can mean "believed them". "This whole bank is loads" can mean "lots". G says "the sea has not spoiled it", then the keeper says the wind brought it.

### Minor

- "Frame" will clash with the Year Machine's black frame. Typing a word walks to the nearest thing of that name.
- Children will call the paper a "note", but "note" is the bottle's word.
- At G, "look stone" gets no answer; giving the paper the word "stone" would pull children from the post.
- "Stand" is not a verb on the page.
- The Rillfolk live in houses on legs, so "stone for our walls" is odd. No "river road" is on the map.
- Grans: the old Mender's, the elder's, the guard's, and the Gran of Shells.
- In section 3b, "(H)" sits beside "the lift's top", but H is the riverbank.
- "Tonight" is still in G.
- Line 17 still says "Elder Rivet". (The page says Elder Thimble since big world 11.)
- What starts the dent line is not said. The elder's "door" is not a thing in the village: its "door" is the way out.
- **The page's own fault, mine to fix:** Pim says "The big kids cross the river... on the old stones", and the harbour keeper says "Nobody has done that for years."

## 2. How I will build it [Proposal: the big world thread's picks]

1. **Showing is giving.** "give shutter to elder", or tapping give, the shutter, then the elder. No new verb.
2. **"Leave it with me?"** is a yes or no box, like the fresh pouch.
3. **No "Tell about…" choice.** For the finds too big to carry (E and H): after the child has looked at the find, **the teller's next talk tells its story.** Looking was the child's own act, so the game does not decide a child noticed (Marc's rule 9). No new kind of talk.
4. **The teller's order.** A child who **gives** the shutter has chosen it: its story comes first. A plain **talk** gives the teller's job first (the pouch, the Rule Stone); the find's story comes on the next talk.
5. **The shutter's painting fills the picture,** as the painted board does: a big machine by a lake, ladders, tiny copper Menders, one with a dented lid. It is a painting, so it fixes the Year Machine's look only loosely. **Marc may want to see it first.**
6. **A card for each find,** after its story [Source: Marc, 24 November].
7. **E's find sits beside the path,** not on its squares.
8. **The page's own contradiction** (Pim and the harbour keeper) is fixed in the same build.
