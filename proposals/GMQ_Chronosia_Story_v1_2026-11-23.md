# Chronosia as one story

**Date:** 23 November 2026. **For:** Marc. **From:** the Chronosia story thread.
**Stage:** a design note. Nothing here is built. I did not touch the game.
**Tags:** [Source] = from your pages or your words. [Proposal] = my suggestion. [Conflict] = your sources disagree. [Built] = in the game today (preview, version 90).

## 0. In short

- **Chronosia has good parts and no spine.** The six quests, the 19 finds, the case and the three trails were built at different times. Each has its own little reason. Nothing says why any of it is happening.
- **Your own material already holds the spine.** Zara's profile says: "too much time travel is ripping holes in the universe." [Source] That one sentence can explain the quakes, the clocks, the crack, the portal, the cougar and the cave.
- **I found 31 contradictions and loose threads.** They are in section 7, each with a fix. Five are real faults a child could catch.
- **Of 30 tasks: 12 keep, 10 rework, 5 merge into others, 3 become scenery.** No finished quest is lost. Section 4.
- **Your lantern chain of today is section 5.** The cave is black. Nothing can be done there without the lantern.
- **Eleven decisions are yours.** Section 11.

### The story in one breath

1. Long ago a Clockmaker built Chronosia a time portal, and the Council of Timekeepers has used it ever since for its missions.
2. Every trip tears the sky a little, and after too many trips the tear set off Time Quakes that jammed every clock.
3. Chronosia calls for help, and the cadet answers: start the great clock, hold the seventh quake to the very minute, and plan the Council's missions so that no trip is wasted.
4. Off the main road, the cadet finds out who still slips through the portal at night, wakes the brass cougar that was built to guard it, and carries a lantern from another planet into the Clockmaker's black cave.
5. In the workshop under the hill the cadet learns that the Clockmaker warned them all, and Chronosia gives the ship its new Engine Core.

## 1. The story on one page

### What happened on Chronosia

1. **Time runs at different speeds in different places.** A river that rushes and crawls. A mountain that grows younger as you climb. A sky in stripes. [Source: your Chronosia page]
2. **A Clockmaker built the great machines:** the great clock with its eight small clocks, the Time Stabilizer, and the time portal. [Proposal. The Clockmaker is the main thread's character, version 87.]
3. **The Council of Timekeepers uses the portal for its missions:** Atlantis, the dinosaurs, the black hole, and the rest of your booklet. [Source: the eight missions. Built: the chart house map shows "every place the portal reaches".]
4. **Every trip tears the sky a little.** [Source: Zara's profile.] A small tear mends by itself, slowly. Too many trips, and the tear grows faster than it mends. [Proposal]
5. **The Clockmaker saw the first thin crack, years ago.** The Clockmaker built a brass cougar to guard the portal, so that only missions on the timetable could pass. The Council said no. Their missions mattered too much to them. [Proposal]
6. **So the Clockmaker gave up on the Council, and trusted the future.** The cougar was left on its rock, not wound. Its key was set adrift on the river, where the Council would not look. Notes were left for whoever came later. Then the Clockmaker went through the portal, one way. [Proposal]
7. **The trips went on, a few more each year.** [Proposal]
8. **Today the tear grew too big.** Six Time Quakes came this afternoon, each longer than the last. They jammed the clocks. The Watcher fears the seventh will break Chronosia apart. [Source: your Tasks 1 and 2, and your words of 19 November]
9. **Chronosia called for help.** [Source: "a mysterious call from the newly discovered world of Chronosia"]

### Why the cadet comes

- Chronosia called. [Source]
- The ship needs an advanced Engine Core, and Chronosia has one. "The Chronosians insist we help them first." [Source]
- **Today the game says neither.** A child lands and reads: "Time is broken here. Walk around. Find who needs help." That breaks your rule 5: a child must arrive knowing what happened and why they are here.

### What the cadet does

**The main road** (your four tasks of 2023, in your order):
1. **The Clock Tower.** Set the eight jammed clocks true. The great clock runs. Chronosia has one true time again.
2. **The Quake Station.** The Stabilizer keeps time from the great clock. Now it can be set. Work out the seventh quake to the minute, and hold it.
3. **The Council Hall.** The ground is safe, but the sky is still torn. From now on no trip may be wasted. Choose one leader for each mission. Then find the mission each member cannot finish.

**Off the main road** (nobody sends you):
- **The case:** someone still uses the portal at night.
- **The guard:** the tortoise's eggs lead to the pond, the pond to the Clockmaker's note, the note to the key, the key to the cougar.
- **The black cave:** a lantern from Voltara, made with sand from Xylophora, lights the way to the Clockmaker's workshop.
- **The Backward Mountain:** Sara's time capsule.

### How it ends, and what is different afterwards

| What the cadet did | What a child can see afterwards |
|---|---|
| Started the great clock | The tower ticks. The tiny clocks in the hollow tree tick together. |
| Held the seventh quake | The needle draws a flat line. The digger sits outside its burrow. The landing words no longer say "time is broken". |
| Planned the Council's missions | The Council gives the Engine Core, and the name Timekeeper Cadet. |
| Closed the case | The portal is locked at night, or one member owes you a secret. |
| Woke the guard | The rock is empty. The cougar sits by the portal. The crack fades. |
| Carried the lantern in | The cave is lit. The workshop is open. The stone door in the hills opens from inside. |

**The crack is the scoreboard of the story.** It fades only when night trips really stop: the Council locks the portal, or the guard takes its post. If the cadet keeps the secret and never wakes the guard, the crack stays. That is a choice that matters.
