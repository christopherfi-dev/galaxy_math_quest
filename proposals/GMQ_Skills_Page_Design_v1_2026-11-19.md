## 4. Your question 4: say something, or only show what happened?

**Both are possible. My pick is: say something, in one plain word, and keep the evidence one tap away.**

**Why not counts only.** A class of 20 and 25 standards is 500 boxes of numbers. You would have to judge every one in your head. That is the marking you wanted to get rid of.

**Why not more than one word.** A machine sees answers. It does not see thinking. So the word must be modest and must say what it is based on.

### The quick column: five words [Proposal]

It looks only at **first tries**, and only at the **last 10** of them for that standard.

| Word | Rule |
|---|---|
| Not seen | no first tries yet |
| Just started | 1 to 4 first tries. Too few to say. |
| Not yet | 5 or more, and half or fewer are right |
| Getting there | between the two |
| Secure | 5 or more, 8 in 10 right, **and on at least 2 different days** |

- **First tries only:** a right answer after a hint is not the same thing.
- **The last 10 only:** so learning shows. A child who was wrong last week and right this week reads as right.
- **Two different days:** so one good session cannot say "secure". Remembering it tomorrow is the point.
- The numbers 5, 8 in 10 and 2 days are my guesses. They are easy to change.

### The quest column: no word, just the moments [Proposal]

A quest uses a skill once or twice. That is too little for a word. So this column lists what happened: "crossed the river, first try" or "the energy plan, three tries".

### What it cannot do

- It cannot say a child **understands**. It can say they answer quick questions right, and whether they used the skill when a quest needed it.
- For 15 of the 25 standards the game has nothing yet. Those rows will say so, until questions are written.
- **Children never see these words.** They see cards and credits.

## 7. What could prove this wrong

| Claim | Test |
|---|---|
| One word is more use than counts | After your class plays, open a few pages. If you tap through to the evidence every time, the word is not earning its place. |
| The rule for "secure" is fair | Find a child you know is secure on rounding, and one who is not. See what the page says about each. |
| First tries are the right thing to count | Watch a child guess wildly at wisps. If they guess, first tries will read "not yet" when the truth is "not trying". |
| Two columns help | See whether any child is "secure" on quick rounding but needed help at the river. That gap is what the second column is for. |
