## 4. Three things I learned the hard way

1. **Names.** "Sky-walker" was in the page ten times before a reviewer heard the film in it. An earlier people's name was one letter from a famous game's character. Say every name aloud.
2. **"The net mender" and "the Menders"** were two peoples with one word. Children would have walked to the wrong one.
3. **A story reason must hold when a child pushes on it.** "Why can't I just take some jars off the sunk raft?" If the story has no answer, the maths on top of it falls over.
