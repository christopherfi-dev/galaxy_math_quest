## 2. What is built

### The map, from above

- 20 by 13 squares. A river cuts it in two. One bridge.
- The cadet walks by tapping a square, or by typing: `go north`, `go to tower`.
- **Mist covers what you have not walked near.** The map fills in as you explore. [Source: your Hollow House has a map that fills in.]
- Places show a gold "!" while someone there needs help, and a tick when all is done.

| Place | How you find it | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| Landing field | you start here | your ship. Type `fly` to leave. |
| Clock Tower | cross the bridge, follow the path | the Keeper, a board of eight clocks, the tower door |
| Quake Station | north along the path | the Watcher, the quake needle, the log book |
| Council Hall | east along the long path | Sara, Tim, Alex, Zara, the book, the round table, the wall timetable |
| **Tortoise Grove** | **hidden.** A gap in the trees north of the long path. A tortoise wanders near it. | a Temporal Tortoise, an old tree, a slow pond |
| Cougar Rock | south of the river, east of the ship | a Clockwork Cougar |
| A ring of stones | far south-east | something buried. `dig`. |
| A TimeTweak tree | far north-east | fruit to taste |

### Six quests, in any order

| # | Quest | Where | The maths | Yours or mine | Answers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fix the clocks | Clock Tower | minutes past 59 make a new hour | [Source] your Task 1 | 7 "fine or broken", 4 times to fix |
| 2 | The Time Quake log | Quake Station | how long between two times; a pattern | [Source] your Task 2 | 10 |
| 3 | Who goes where? | Council Hall | reading for detail, **with the booklet** | [Source] your Task 4, first half | 4 pairs of page numbers |

- Each quest earns a card. All six earn a gold card and the name "Timekeeper Cadet".
- A cadet can leave a quest half way. Their place is kept.
- A wrong answer gives a hint. Some hints come close to the answer, so that nobody is stuck.
- The sundial under the stones gives one more card.

## 4. Choices I made that are yours to change

1. **24-hour time.** Your clocks and your timetable use it (14:75, 25:00, 14:45). Your strand plan puts 24-hour time in the Advanced quest. [Conflict] I kept your prints as they are, so the screen matches the paper. For clock 3 both 15:15 and 3:15 are right.
2. **Clock 8.** One of your prints says 0:00 and the other says 00:90. [Conflict] I used 00:90. It gives five broken clocks and three fine ones.
3. **Alex and "risky".** Your sheets never say which mission is risky. [Conflict] In your drafting chat you wrote that "we might know that the dinosaur one is too risky". So my question points at the asteroid, and the answer is page 7.
4. **Tim.** Your video says each member will miss one mission. But both of Tim's are in the same dimension, so he misses none. [Conflict] I made that the question: "Type 0 if he can go to both."
5. **Left out of your Task 3:** "the longest event" (two missions tie at 90 minutes), "the date with most events" (three dates tie), and the gap of 21 hours 15 minutes across midnight.
6. **Left out of your Task 1:** the Restoration Report with 33 blanks. It is writing, not time.
7. **The reward.** Your page says both "an advanced Engine Core" and "an upgraded Weapons System". [Conflict] The game gives a gold card and names neither.
8. **The Keeper and the Watcher** are mine. So are the map, the grove, the rock, the sundial and the tree.
9. **The pictures in the book** are small emoji. Your photos stay in your printed booklet.

## 6. What could prove option B wrong

| Claim | Test |
|---|---|
| Walking the map feels like exploring | Watch whether your daughter walks into the mist by herself, or only follows the path. |
| The hidden grove is found | See whether she finds it without the hint. If nobody finds it, it is too well hidden. |
| A choice of where to go helps | Ask her why she went to a place first. "It was nearest" is fine. "I didn't know where to go" is not. |
| Two ways to move are not confusing | From above she taps a square. From the side she taps a thing. Watch the first time she steps into a place. |
| The booklet is better on paper | Give her the printed booklet for quest 3. See whether she flips pages or asks for the screen. |
| Six quests is more than five minutes | Time her. My guess is 60 to 90 minutes. It is only a guess. |
| The "!" marks do not turn it into a to-do list | See whether she ever goes somewhere with no mark on it. |
