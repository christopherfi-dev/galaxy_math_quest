## Shield Calibration

**Your mission:** make the shield strong on every side.

1. Take the shield mat.
2. Take the number of power tokens your screen shows.
3. The shield has 5 sections.
4. Share the tokens equally over the 5 sections.
5. Show your Commander. Say how many tokens are on each section.

**Maths word: share equally.** Every section gets the same number.

Your Commander gives you a code. Type it into the game.

2. Paint five kinds of stars.

4. Show your Commander. Your Commander draws a path on your map.
5. Count the stars on the path.

## Target Practice

**Your mission:** test your aim, and keep count.

1. Stand behind the line.
2. Throw at the wall of numbers. The numbers are 1 to 9.
3. Write down each number you hit.
4. Keep a running total. Add each new number to it.
5. Stop when your total is an even number from 32 to 40. A total of 32 counts. A total of 40 counts.

**Maths word: even.** An even number can be shared into 2 equal groups. It ends in 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8.

Your Commander gives you a code. Type it into the game.

## Power Allocation

**Your mission:** build the power for four ship parts. Work with a partner.

1. Take base-ten blocks. You have only 2 thousand-blocks.
2. Build these four numbers:
   - Engine: 1,237
   - Navigation: 1,193
   - Shield: 1,097
   - Weapons: 933
3. You do not have enough thousand-blocks. Swap a thousand for 10 hundreds where you must.
4. Show your Commander.

**Maths word: place value.** 10 hundreds make 1 thousand.

Your Commander gives you a code. Type it into the game.

## Mission Voltara: the Council's choice

**Your mission:** decide what Voltara does about the Number Nomads.

1. Build your energy plan with base-ten blocks. You have 8 hundreds, 70 tens and 150 ones.
2. The Number Nomads need 240 energy each night.
3. Decide. Will you share with them?
4. If yes, what will you switch off?
5. Tell your Commander your choice. Say why, in 2 or 3 sentences.

After this, Nomad Hollow is on your plan table. Light it, and the Nomads are your neighbours. Leave it dark, and their town goes dark.

Your Commander gives you the Commander's code. Type it into the game.
