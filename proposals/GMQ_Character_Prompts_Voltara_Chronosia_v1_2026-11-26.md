## 1. Rules for these pictures (read once)

**Nothing may give an answer away** [checked against every task]:
- **No readable clock anywhere.** The Cougar's chest clock is read in its task. The Tower's board shows wrong times the child must fix. So every clock face is blank: no hands and no numbers.
- **Nothing a child could count:**
  - the Nomads' tiny houses (the answer is 30);
  - the Tortoise's eggs;
  - the digger's seeds;
  - the councillors' crystals or the city's tower (1,650).
- **No writing on any log, map or chart:** the Watcher's log has the quake times.
- **Nobody holds the night traveller's clue.** It changes for each child; one is "a tiny clay clock".

**Background: plain white, not green** [Proposal]:
- Voltara's councillors and Chronosia's Timekeepers include green, pink and blue people. Green or magenta would cut holes in them.
- Pure white cut cleanly for Elder Thimble.

**Hands: small mitten hands** [Proposal]:
- The game's drawings of these people have no arms. Its words give them hands: "One hand holds a rope", "taps her log", "folds both arms".
- Mitten hands (a thumb and a round mitten, no separate fingers) cannot get a fifth finger.
- Your other choice: no arms, as now. Their faces and antennae would show their feelings.
