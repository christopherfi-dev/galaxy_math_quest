## 3. Ten minutes to see it

1. Open the preview link. Tap **More**, then **Teacher**, then **Go to Stardust**.
2. Walk north, out of the airlock, along the corridor. Find the Control Room, top left.
3. Tap the Mission Control screen. Read what it asks.
4. Tap the thermometer in that room. Read it. Write it down.
5. Tap the wall chart. Last trip's chart is there first. Then start a new one. Tap the big chart to set a bar.
6. Tap the Central Computer. Two graphs. Which month went wrong?
7. Walk to the Engine Room. Look at the glass tank. Then look at a steel one.

The four quests take longer than ten minutes. That is on purpose.

## 5. What I am not sure of

- **No child has played any of this.** Every test was a script.
- **Reading the thermometer may be the hard part,** not the graph. Each small line is 1 degree. Only the tens have numbers.
- **"Breaks its plate" is my phrase.** It may not be clear.
- **The locker may be guess-and-fix,** not planning. "Too little" and "too much" let a child creep up on the answer.
- **Four quests may be one too many,** after your own verdict that Stardust was too long.
- **"See it first" on a screen is a picture and three sentences.** On your slides it is a talk. One tap skips it.
- **I changed your numbers and units in five places.** Litres for gallons. Bar graphs for line graphs. 10 or 20 points for your 30. Six months for nine. One extra month that burned a lot and went far. Each is in the guide, section 3, with the reason.
