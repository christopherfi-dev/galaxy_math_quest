**What this says:**
- The cadet's size was never the main cause. Before, it was about one square, like the hero in your photo, only a little taller on the screen.
- The causes were the mist (6 squares), the walk that stopped at the mist, and nothing to aim for.
- Now the cadet is about the hero's height on the screen, and you see about as many squares as in your photo.
- **One difference remains** [Source: the main thread's measurement]: in that game a whole town fits in one square. In Algora one square holds one tree. So each square of Algora shows less of the world. Step 3 below is for that, if it matters.

## 3. What changed

You said "we can try this and see what happens," so step 1 is built. Each part can be undone on its own.

1. **No black mist on the walking screen.**
   - The Map still fills in as you walk. It now fills in with every square that has been on your screen.
   - A tap anywhere you can see walks there.
2. **16 squares across on an iPad, held either way.**
   - About 51 pixels a square: about 1 cm under a finger.
   - The rows follow the picture's shape: 14.6 sideways, 13.9 upright. So it is 16 × 15, as in your photo. The main thread's "16 × 12" would need a wider picture.
   - Phones are not changed: 36-pixel squares, 10.8 × 11.
3. **Paths.**
   - Already there: the ship to the village; the village to the bridge and on into the Reach; to each machine; the old track to the stepping stones; the lane to the hedge.
   - New: to the orchard keeper, to the fisher, and to the old Mender.
   - Paths are now worn earth, a darker brown. Before, they were the same pale colour as the beach, so the beach looked like a wide road.
4. **Landmarks.** Every person in the Meadow now stands at something you can pick out from across the screen:

| Person | Where they stand | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Elder Rivet | Mender Village, with its "!" when he has news [the game calls him "he"] | nothing |
| the gardener | the first bend of the path from the ship, in sight of the ship | nothing |
| the fisher | the pond | a path to the fisher |
| the reed cutter | the reed bed by the old stones | nothing |
| the orchard keeper | the side of the orchard ("Four rows of six trees. I count them every day.") | a path to the keeper |
| the Mender with big ears | **the broken bridge**, listening across the river ("The Rillfolk sing at night, across the river.") | moved from open grass (square 38,72) to the bridge (44,73) |
| the old Mender | **a big old tree** in the middle of the meadow | the tree is new, with a path |

In the Reach, every person already stood on a road, at a town, the harbour or the gate.

5. **The Map's words** now say "land you have not seen yet" (not "walked"), and "Brown dot: the village", because paths are brown too.

## 4. What I chose. Each is yours to change [Proposal]

1. **What counts as seen:** every square that has been on the screen. The other way was 6 squares round the cadet, as before. But then the Map would stay dark where the child clearly saw land.
2. **The big old tree.** It is new, and it is not in the story. It has no words: tapping it walks you next to it. A word like "tree" would make the cadet walk across the meadow whenever a child typed "look tree". The story thread may want to name it, or move it.
3. **Moving the Mender with big ears** to the bridge. The listener's own lines are about hearing the Rillfolk across the river.
4. **The path colour.**
5. **The cadet is drawn as before** (1.3 squares tall, as in the game), so it shrank with the squares.

## 5. Not done, on purpose

- **Step 3, one screen at a time:** only if children still get lost.
- **Signposts at each fork** ("ORCHARD", "POND"): not built. Kept for later, if children reach a fork and do not know which way.
- **People on the Map:** not built.
- **Hidden things.** The rubble, the hollow log and the bottle can now be seen from across the screen, not only from 6 squares away. They are still off the paths. Watch whether this makes them too easy.
- **An older fault, not mine to change today:** the old Mender's word button says "old".

## 6. Step 2: what to watch when two or three children play [Source: the main thread's plan]

- How long from landing to finding Elder Rivet.
- Taps that lead nowhere.
- Whether they leave the path to explore.
- New: whether they find the hidden things too easily now.

**What could prove step 1 wrong:**
- Children still wander and do not know where to go.
- At 51 pixels, children's taps land on the wrong square.
- Children never leave the paths, because the paths are too strong.
