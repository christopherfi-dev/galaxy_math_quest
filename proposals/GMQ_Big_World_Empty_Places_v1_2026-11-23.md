# Algora: places a child can walk to and find nothing

**For:** Marc, and the story thread. **From:** the big world thread. **Date:** 23 November 2026, night.
**Picture:** `proposals/GMQ_Big_World_Empty_Places_v1_2026-11-23.png` (pink: 9 or more steps from anything to meet or find; letters as below).

## Why this matters now

- With no mist, a child sees much more of the world, and will walk to what looks interesting.
- Marc: "I just also wanted them to experience the world and they just walk around and explore it."
- The story thread (section 16e): the culture is "found, not told".
- So a place a child can walk to, with nothing in it, is a promise the world does not keep.

## The four places a child can walk into [measured from the page, with every built gate open]

| | Where | How big | Ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | The north-west corner of the Meadow: a copse by the old wall, west of the Wall Machine | 32 squares, up to 15 steps from anything | grass, trees, tall grass |
| E | The far bank where the old stones land, north of the Reach's lookout | 30 squares, up to 16 steps | grass and a path |
| G | The south beach below the orchard | 17 squares, up to 12 steps | sand |
| H | The far bank just south of the bridge's landing | 12 squares, up to 13 steps | grass |

## Land a child can see but never reach

A, B, C and F (the Reach's south-west, its north-east by the hill lift, its north behind the reeds, and its east by Reed Town) are fenced by reeds or water. They are 20 to 149 squares each.
- A child will see them now, and may tap them. The walk stops at the reeds.
- **Watch for this** when children play (testing sheet, row 19).

## What could go there [Proposal: the story thread writes it; Marc decides]

- One found thing in each of D, E, G and H: something of a people's past, as the story thread's section 16e says. No maths needed.
- It should be off the path, so finding it is a reward for leaving the path.
- The big world thread then builds it: a thing with a look, and a line on the map.

How it was measured: walking steps from the nearest thing, through squares a child can walk on, in the Meadow and the Reach. `node checks/bigworld_empty_places.js` prints it again. It is a measure, not a check: nothing passes or fails.
