## 4. Three things only you can settle

1. **The unit for measuring.** Your drafts use centimetres and metres. A centimetre ruler is marked in tenths. The standard asks for halves and quarters. I remember the Common Core wording as "halves and fourths of an inch". I have not checked how your AERO document words it. Choices: inches, or a centimetre ruler read to the half, or a made-up Measuronian unit marked in halves and quarters.
2. **A real ruler, or a ruler in the picture.** iPads are different sizes, so the game cannot check a real ruler held to the screen. Either the ruler and the thing are both in the picture, or the child measures on paper and you give a code, as in your real-life station tasks.
3. **Which shapes plan.** The construction site with area, the three levels with the chart, or the monument. Or parts of each.
