## 3. Ten minutes to see it

1. Open the preview link. Tap **More**, then **Teacher**, then **Go to Xylophora**.
2. Walk east to the camp. Talk to the Site Chief.
3. Walk north. Find the giant's skin. Work out how many squares. Set the number on the survey post.
4. Go back to the Chief. Read the order board.
5. Tap **Build**, then **Hospital**. Tap two corners on the building ground. Read what the game says back.
6. For Chronosia: **More**, **Teacher**, **Go to Chronosia**. Walk to the Clock Tower. Use the board of clocks.

The four quests take longer than ten minutes. That is on purpose.
