## 2. The short version

- Voltara is now **nine places you walk between**, with things to find, people to talk to and a story that turns.
- The story is yours: the energy crisis, the Energy Council, the 21 city places, the 1,650 energy. [Source]
- **Every piece of maths opens something.** You round to cross a river. You regroup crystals to open a count. You add to learn what a street needs. You subtract to find what is missing.
- **Each one shows the idea first,** then asks. That was your point about understanding before doing.
- The city lights up by **the student's own plan**.
- **The plan has effects.** Every place uses energy and makes people happy. Some make people unhappy if they stay dark. Two places make energy for tomorrow, and one of them makes smoke.
- **A plan lasts one day.** Next morning a report says how the people are and what the tower holds. Then you plan again.
- **There are two goals and four endings.** Flying away shows a summary of your effect on Voltara. You can come back and change it.
- Then there is a **twist**, as in your Atlantis project. The student's own morning sum does not match the tower: energy is vanishing.
- The last step is **done in real life**, with blocks and a spoken reason. You give a code. It earns the **gold card**.

- A place can help one number and hurt another. The Factory gives power and makes smoke. The Amusement Park gives joy and no power.
- The goal is enough energy and enough happy people.

### The two goals

1. **People:** 600 more happy people than unhappy people.
2. **Power:** 1,650 or more in the tower next morning. That is what you found when you landed.

### How the tower fills each morning

| Part | Energy |
|---|---|
| The tower makes by itself | 1,050 |
| The Solar Power Plant, if lit | + 600 |
| The Factory, if lit | + 500 |
| The Nomads mend a panel, if their town is lit | + 100 |
| What you saved | + your spare |
| Taken in the night, until the Council decides | − 240 |
| The tower is full at | 2,000 |

**Your question: "We don't turn on the solar plant. What happens?"**
- The tower makes only 1,050 by itself. With the solar plant it makes 1,650.
- Spend everything with no power place lit: next morning the tower holds **810**.
- After the Council's choice, starting from a tower of 1,650, **no clean plan can meet both goals without the solar plant.** The code checked every plan.

### The four endings

| | Tower 1,650 or more | Tower lower |
|---|---|---|
| **People happy** | **Voltara shines.** | **Happy, but short of power.** "The people are happy. But the tower holds less power than they are used to." |
| **People not happy** | **Power, but not happy.** "Voltara has enough power. But the people are not happy." | **Voltara is worse off.** |

- The ending comes from the last day you played.
- The summary also counts the days when both goals were met.
- Voltara stays as you leave it. Come back, plan more days, and the ending changes.

### What the student sees

- **The plan table:** each place shows its numbers. Tapping one says what it does in words. Running totals show happy people, unhappy people, and what the tower will get tomorrow.
- **The morning report:** who was happy, each harm with its number and its story, then the sums.
- **The world:** rubbish in the streets, smoke in the sky, a dark or lit Nomad town.
- **The Mission box, the Council and the wall map** all say which ending Voltara is in now.
- **Flying away:** a table of then and now, for the tower, the people, the sky and the Nomads.

### Who does the adding

- The game keeps the running totals while the student plans.
- The student does the sums that matter each morning:
  - How many more were happy than unhappy?
  - How much should the tower hold?
  - How much is missing? (until the Council's choice)

### What the code found (all in `checks/voltara_quest_check.py`)

**It is a real choice.**
- On day 1, 2,025,758 plans fit. Only **186** meet both goals. 57 are clean. 129 use the Factory.
- No plan makes the people happy with the Hospital, a Neighborhood or the Supermarket dark. Not even with a full tower.

**Day 1 usually ends "happy, but short of power".**
- A child who lights every "!" place, the solar plant and the best fun gets 750 happy and 0 unhappy.
- But they do not know about the missing 240 yet. The tower holds 1,440. That is the twist doing its work.

**Before the Nomads are found, the clean way is very narrow.**
- The same child, keeping 240 back, gets 590. Ten short.
- **The Factory fixes it today:** 670 happy, 50 unhappy, tower 1,940. Both goals met.
- **But the Factory cannot do it for ever.** Run every day, it holds both goals for 4 days. Then the smoke wins.

**After the Council's choice it opens up.**
- 1,076 clean plans meet both goals and can be repeated every day. 425 light the Nomads. 651 leave them dark.
- So sharing or not sharing can both end well. The debate stays yours and the students'.
- A careful child who picks fun places at random succeeds 86% of the time now, against 19% before.

**Thinking pays.**
- Of 5,000 students tapping at random for 8 days: 170 shine, 72 are happy but short, 4,560 have power but unhappy people, 198 are worse off.

## 9. How it was tested

- `checks/voltara_quest_test.js` plays the whole quest at iPad size and phone size: 9 of 9 steps, 7 of 7 secrets, the gold card, and flying away.
  - It works each answer out from the rules, not from the page.
  - It checks that you cannot cross, open the tower, plan, or pass the tunnel too early.
  - **It plays five days and reaches all four endings.**
    - Day 1, a careless plan: 710 happy, 160 unhappy, tower 950. Worse off. Rubbish in the streets.
    - Day 2, the Factory as a quick fix: tower 1,320, smoke 50. Still worse off.
    - Day 3, both power places: the tower fills to 2,000 and 160 is lost. Smoke 100. Power, but not happy.
    - Day 4, the clean way with the Nomads lit: 790 happy, 80 unhappy, tower 1,890. Voltara shines.
    - Day 5, everything for the people and nothing for tomorrow: tower 1,170. Happy, but short of power.
  - It works every answer out from the rules, written out a second time in the test.
  - It checks the ending summary, staying, flying, and coming back to find Voltara as it was left.
  - It checks both Nomad endings: the dark town and the thankful elder.
- `checks/voltara_quest_check.py` checks every number. All pass.
  - The river rule was run on 10,000 seeds: 40,000 stones, each with one right answer.
  - It tried all 2,097,152 plans for day 1, and all 4,194,304 for after the choice.
  - It searched every way of playing day after day, to find how long the Factory can last.
  - It works out a day in two separate ways and checks they agree on 2,000 random plans.
  - It ran 5,000 random students for 8 days each. Every morning sum had one whole-number answer. The tower stayed between 810 and 2,000.
- A game saved under sketch version 19 or 20 opens under version 21 and carries on. Its record of days starts clean. Tested on five old saves.
- **A bug an earlier test found:** walking from a street back to the plaza froze the game. It is fixed, and one bad frame can no longer stop the game.

**Not tested:** real children, a real iPad, and how long it takes. My guess is three or four lessons, plus the block lesson. That is a guess. I also have not tested whether children can hold three numbers for each place in mind.
