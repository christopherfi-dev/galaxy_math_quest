## Prompt 5 (Pim: who he is, and his character sheet)

```
NEW CHARACTER, SAME WORLD. Keep exactly the same drawing style, outline, colours and kind of body as Elder Thimble in the pictures above. This is another Mender, called Pim.

WHO HE IS: Pim is a young Mender child who lives in the village, next to Elder Thimble. He is cheerful, curious and a little cheeky. He lost his favourite toy, a red spinner, by the pond. He knows a secret about the old stepping stones in the river.

HIS BODY: like every Mender, Pim is a copper pot with the same two curved pot handles, the same separate arms below the handles with small round hands (three stubby fingers and a thumb on every hand, always), a dark brown band round his middle, and short brown feet. But he is different from Elder Thimble in these ways:
- he is SMALLER and rounder: about two thirds of Elder Thimble's height
- his copper is lighter and brighter, a warm light orange-copper (about #E09A55), smooth and shiny, with no dents and no scratches
- NO beard, NO moustache and NO hair eyebrows: a smooth copper face
- big round white eyes with dark navy pupils, a little bigger for his size than Elder Thimble's, and a wide, happy mouth
- HIS LID IS TOO BIG FOR HIM: it is clearly wider than his pot, tipped a little to one side, and slipping down over his forehead to the tops of his eyes, like a hat that is too big. It has the same small round gold knob on top as Elder Thimble's lid. His lid has no dent.

DRAWING STYLE: exactly the same as Elder Thimble's pictures: a clean, flat 2D cartoon, a bold dark navy outline (about #0E1626) of even thickness around every shape, flat colours with one darker shade and one small white shine on the upper left, light from the upper left. Not 3D, not a photo, not a painting, not anime.

FOR EVERY PICTURE FROM NOW ON:
- a plain, flat, bright green background (#00FF00), the same green everywhere: no floor, no ground line, no shadow, no frame, no border
- no green anywhere on the characters or on anything they hold
- no words, no letters, no numbers, no labels and no signature anywhere in the picture
- show every character's whole body, with space all round

PICTURE 5: PIM'S CHARACTER SHEET.
One wide picture (16:9). Show Pim four times in a row, standing, all the same size, with even gaps between them:
1. from the front
2. turned three-quarters to his right
3. from the side
4. from the back (the knob, the back of the pot, the handles; no face)
His too-big lid must slip and tip the same way in every view.
```
