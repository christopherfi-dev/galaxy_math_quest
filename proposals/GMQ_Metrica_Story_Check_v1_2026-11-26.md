# Xylophora and Lengthar: the story test, task by task

**Date:** 26 November 2026. **For:** Marc, and the main thread. **From:** the story thread.
**Stage:** a check, with fixes in words. Nothing here is built. I changed no file of the main thread's.
**Version 2,** after one review ("not yet": two blockers). Section 5a says what changed. Version 2 is not re-read.

**Tags:** [Built] is in the game today (version 100). [Source] is your words or pages. [Proposal] is mine.

**The test** (the master plan, section 5b, and your rule of 25 November):
1. **Need:** someone in the story needs this worked out, for a reason the child is told first.
2. **Meaning:** the answer changes the world because of what it means. No maths as a password, and no code, count or pattern as a thing.
3. **Consequence:** a wrong answer plays out, and shows why.
4. **Picture:** what the child works on fills the picture.

**No numbers are changed.** Every fix below is words, or what the world does after an answer.

## 0. In short

- **Xylophora mostly passes.** Its story is strong: Earth is sending families, and four buildings must stand before the rains [Built]. Walls leave a gap or waste skin; roofs stay dry, or the leaves run out; Homes fit, or the skin runs short. Three small fixes, in section 1.
- **Lengthar fails test 3 everywhere.** A wrong answer "gets only a red message in the box. Nothing changes in the world." Two tasks also have no reason told first.
- **The fix for Lengthar, in version 2** [Proposal]: in each task, the child's number is used to make or do something, and a wrong number shows its fault in what was made. Your Mistakes note's rule: "A failure that does not show its cause is just 'wrong' with extra steps", and "The consequence teaches; it does not punish."
- **The Chaos Guardian stays your last task** [Source: your drafts, "a creature feeding on imprecision"]. Whether it should also feed on the other tasks' mistakes is your decision 1. Version 1 had it grow on every wrong answer; the review showed that only says "wrong", with extra steps.
- **The measuring tape** is given and never used. It is your open decision 15 in the master plan.
- **Project Second Home** is your own frame [Source: your home page: "They began Project Second Home"]. You also asked, on 20 November, for something nearer than a galaxy-wide plot: a child arriving and knowing where they are. So one line at most, and only as a Proposal (decision 3).
- **Your decisions:** section 4. Three.
