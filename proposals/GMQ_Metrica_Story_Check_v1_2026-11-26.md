# Xylophora and Lengthar: the story test, task by task

**Date:** 26 November 2026. **For:** Marc, and the main thread. **From:** the story thread.
**Stage:** a check, with fixes in words. Nothing here is built. I changed no file of the main thread's.
**Reviewed:** see section 5.

**Tags:** [Built] is in the game today (version 100). [Source] is your words or pages. [Proposal] is mine.

**The test** (the master plan, section 5b, and your rule of 25 November):
1. **Need:** someone in the story needs this worked out, for a reason the child is told first.
2. **Meaning:** the answer changes the world because of what it means. No maths as a password, and no code, count or pattern as a thing.
3. **Consequence:** a wrong answer plays out, and shows why.
4. **Picture:** what the child works on fills the picture.

**No numbers are changed.** Every fix below is words, or what the world does after an answer.

## 0. In short

- **Xylophora mostly passes.** Its story is strong: Earth is sending families, and four buildings must stand before the rains [Built]. Walls leave a gap or waste skin, roofs stay dry or leaves run out, homes fit or skin runs short. Four small fixes, in section 1.
- **Lengthar fails test 3 everywhere.** A wrong answer "gets only a red message in the box. Nothing changes in the world." It also has two tasks with no reason told first. Its own ideas, which are yours, carry the fixes: the Chaos Guardian "feeds on careless measuring" [Source: your drafts], so a careless answer should feed it. Section 2.
- **One link is loose across both:** Lengthar's measuring tape is given and never used. The plan says the lantern uses it; the built lantern weighs in grams instead. That is the main thread's to settle.
- **Xylophora is already Project Second Home:** Earth's families are settling a new world. One line could say so, and it ties to the small worlds' star seeds.
- **Your decisions:** section 4. Three.
