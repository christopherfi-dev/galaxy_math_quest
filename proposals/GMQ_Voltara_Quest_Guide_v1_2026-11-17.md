## 2. The short version

- Voltara is now **nine places you walk between**, with things to find, people to talk to and a story that turns.
- The story is yours: the energy crisis, the Energy Council, the 21 city places, the 1,650 energy. [Source]
- **Every piece of maths opens something.** You round to cross a river. You regroup crystals to open a count. You add to learn what a street needs. You subtract to find what is missing.
- **Each one shows the idea first,** then asks. That was your point about understanding before doing.
- The city lights up by **the student's own plan**, and stays that way.
- Then there is a **twist**, as in your Atlantis project. The lights are on, but energy is vanishing.
- The last step is **done in real life**, with blocks and a spoken reason. You give a code. It earns the **gold card**.

## 8. How it was tested

- `checks/voltara_quest_test.js` plays the whole quest at iPad size and phone size: 9 of 9 steps, 7 of 7 secrets, the gold card, and flying away.
  - It works each answer out from the words on the screen.
  - It checks that you cannot cross, open the tower, plan, or pass the tunnel too early.
  - It checks the log uses the student's own plan total.
- `checks/voltara_quest_check.py` checks every number. All pass.
  - The river rule was run on 10,000 seeds: 40,000 stones, each with one right answer.
  - It counted the full plans: 15,019. None lights everything.
- **A bug the test found:** walking from a street back to the plaza froze the game. It is fixed, and one bad frame can no longer stop the game.

**Not tested:** real children, a real iPad, and how long it takes. My guess is three or four lessons, plus the block lesson. That is a guess.

## 9. What could prove this wrong

| Claim | Test |
|---|---|
| It feels like a place, not a quiz | Ask five students what they did on Voltara. See whether they start with the fish and the hidden town, or with the sums. |
| The maths feels needed, not added | Ask why they had to round. A good answer is "to get across". |
| Going back for the glow stone is fun, not a chore | Watch faces on the walk back. It is six screens. |
| The twist lands | See whether anyone guesses before the hollow. |
| Adding eight numbers on a board is fair | Watch Home Street. It is the longest sum in the quest. |

## 10. Decisions for you

1. **Which needs list?** The class version (2,220, so 570 short), or your planning list (1,940, so 290 short)?
2. **Is the walk back for the glow stone too long?** I can put a second stone nearer, or keep it as the price of not exploring.
3. **Should every student have the same city numbers?** They do now, so a class can argue about one plan. The river numbers differ for each student.
4. **Cards:** each of the seven maths steps gives a random card, and the last step gives gold. Too many?
5. **What should the Nomads change in the world,** once the Council decides? Nothing changes yet after the gold card.
