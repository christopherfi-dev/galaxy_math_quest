## 1. Your rule [Source: Marc, 22 and 23 November]

1. A story problem gives a table. The child reads it and makes a decision.
2. The world does what the child decided, right or wrong. Nobody says "that is not right".
3. The child sees what follows.
4. Then a new problem of the same size.
5. Stepping down to an easier one is not the child's choice. **The maths skills engine decides.**
6. A child who is right again and again gets harder problems, **and is never told.** "I'm still able to help solve these real-life problems."
7. Algora is the trial. Build it to this rule.

## 4. One family, as the trial

Your own example: "how much stone do we need in the 12th month if this growing pattern continues".
The Reach's story already says: "The high water is coming." [built] Nobody has asked how high.

### The story

Each town has a **tide post**: a tall post with marks. Every morning the keeper paints where the water stood. The water climbs the same number of marks each day. The town must build its wall before the high water, on day 12. Stone is dear: every row is carried from the Meadow.

"Where will the water stand on day 12? Paint the line. We build the wall to your line."

### What the child looks at

- **The picture is the tide post,** and the town behind it. Four painted lines: day 1 to day 4, each with its mark number.
- **The child's own table,** in the notebook: day, and mark. The child writes the rows they want. The game fills in nothing.
- Nothing shows day 12.

### The decision, and what follows

The child paints a line on the post (types a mark). The builders build to it. Then the days pass on the screen, quickly: the water climbs, one day at a time, to day 12.

| The child's line | What follows |
|---|---|
| At the water's mark | The water stops at the top of the wall. The town is dry |
| Too low | The water comes over. It floods as many shelves of the rice store as the line was short. The town bails |
| Too high | The wall stands above the water by the rows that were not needed. That stone was for the next job, and that job now starts short |

- Nobody says "wrong". The child sees the water and the wall.
- **What follows is the size of the mistake.** The most likely one: counting 12 jumps when there are 11. The wall is then exactly one day's climb too high.
- The water's last mark can be read off the post afterwards. **That is why the next problem is a new one** [Source: your answer 1].

### The three sizes

| Size | The table | The ask | An example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Smaller** | starts at the step, climbs by 2, 5 or 10 | a near day: 6 to 8 | 5, 10, 15, 20. Day 7? **35** |
| **The size** | starts anywhere from 2 to 9, climbs by 3, 4 or 6 | day 10, 11 or 12 | 5, 8, 11, 14. Day 12? **38** |
| **Harder** | the same | **backwards:** "the rice store floor is at mark 41. On which day does the water reach it?" Or day 24 | 5, 8, 11, 14. Mark 41? **Day 13.** Day 24? **74** |

Worked again: 5 + 11 × 3 = 38. 5 + 12 × 3 = 41, so mark 41 is day 13. 5 + 23 × 3 = 74. 5 × 7 = 35.

## 7. What could prove this wrong

- **Writing all 12 rows always works.** Then "the 12th day" teaches going on, not jumping. Day 24 in the harder size is where a jump is needed. Is that enough?
- **"Too high" may not feel like a mistake.** A careful child may build high on purpose. That is good sense in real life. The story says stone is dear, but a child may not care.
- **The step down may feel like a punishment** when the child did not choose it. In your engine a child expects a tutor. In a world they may not.
- **Three right in a row, after a step down, is a lot of tide posts.** Up to 9 problems by your engine's rule before it moves on.
- **Watching 12 days pass** must be quick, or a class loses minutes.
- **The off-by-one mistake gives a wall that is too high.** So the most common mistake has the mildest result.
- **Nobody has played it.**

## 9. The review: 6 blockers. Not built.

The reviewer worked every example again. **The four worked examples are right.** It also listed every case the number rule allows: there are only 72 problems of full size.

### The blockers

| | Blocker | A case |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **"Day 12" reads two ways.** The keeper paints each morning, and the water climbs during the day too | 5, 8, 11, 14. The morning line on day 12 is 38. The water at the end of day 12 is 41. I called 41 "the common mistake". A careful reader gets it honestly, and is then stepped down |
| B2 | **My "smaller" problem rewards the wrong method.** It starts at the step, so day × step is always right | 5, 10, 15, 20: day 7 is 7 × 5. A child stepped down for exactly that thinking gets it right, then goes back to a size where it is wrong in 63 of 72 cases |
| B3 | **When the start equals the step, two mistakes cancel** | 3, 6, 9, 12: day 12 is 36 by the right way, and by day × step. 9 of the 72 cases |
| B4 | **"Too high" shows a dry town, and the engine counts it wrong.** The child sees success and is then stepped down | A child who types 99 every time keeps every town dry |
| B5 | **The towns run out.** One problem, one new one, then three in a row: two of the three towns are already used | By your engine's rule, the worst case in this one family is **14 problems, 12 of them full size: 45 to 60 minutes**, ending in "move on" |
| B6 | **The harder size breaks the story.** High water is day 12, so there is no day 24, and the water never reaches mark 41 | |

### The major faults that matter most

- **It is "what comes next" with a story on top, not a decision from a table.** There is one row of four numbers. Nothing is weighed or chosen. **You said "how much stone do we need in the 12th month". I changed the quantity to a water line.** With stone, too little and too much both hurt in a natural way.
- **I rebuilt your engine as fixed code, with no AI, and did not list that as your decision.** Your engine leans on judgement: classify the error, find the skill underneath. Fixed code with one "smaller" problem is a lesser thing.
- **My advice bent your answer.** You said "your planet is the trial". I made one new problem the trial and left the seven built tasks saying "wrong".
- **Your own words pull two ways** [Conflict]: on 16 November the engine was "a bit too much", and mini quests are 3 to 5 problems. On 23 November stepping down "is in the maths skills engine". I took the whole of the engine without asking how much of it you mean.
