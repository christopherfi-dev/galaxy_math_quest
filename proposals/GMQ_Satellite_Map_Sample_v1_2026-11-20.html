Chronosia from orbit

This map is drawn by code when the page opens. No picture file is used. It follows the game's own map of Chronosia square by square: the river, the bridge, the mountains, the forests and the path are all where the cadet walks today.

The ground shifts colour in wide bands. On your page, each colour of Chronosia's sky is a different time zone. Seen from above, the time zones tint the land. The river runs fast in the west, with white water, and slow in the east.
