## 1. The lucky draw

[Source: Marc, 2026-11-20] "They can choose the middle version of whatever engine parts they are getting, or they can choose to enter the lucky draw." Lucky draw 10%, the middle one 50%, the one below and the one above 20% each. Cards spin like a tornado, one floats down and flips, colour bursts out. "LUCKY DRAW" fills the screen. Then the part, with its attack points and health points.

**What a child sees**

1. They finish the third task of a part. The game says the part is fitted: "Now choose which Engine Core."
2. A choice opens:
   - **Take the Standard Reactor** for sure.
   - **LUCKY DRAW!**
   - "Not yet." The choice then waits in My ship.
3. The draw bag shows **10 tokens: 5 green, 2 red, 2 light blue, 1 gold**, with the part each colour gives and its HP and AP. That is your odds, shown as tokens a Grade 3 child can count.
4. **The show:**
   - Four cards spin like little tornadoes, backs only. The result never shows early.
   - One floats down and flips.
   - Its colour bursts out like a sparkler: red, green, light blue, or a gold rainbow.
   - Gold: "LUCKY DRAW!" glows across the screen, with three chimes and more sparks.
   - Then a card with the part's picture, its name, your words for it, its health points and its attack points. "Fit it" ends the show.
5. My ship lists each part's version and numbers. The teacher's log says who drew and what came out.

**Things to know**

- **The draw is decided before the cards spin, and saved.** Closing the game mid-show cannot change it.
- **"Take the Standard" also gets a short show:** one green card flips. No spin.
- **With "reduce motion" on,** the card is shown already turned, with no spin and no sparks.
- **The show covers the picture for a few seconds,** like the reading cards. That bends your rule that nothing covers the picture. I think a celebration earns it. Your call.
- **Children with saved games** whose parts are already fitted see "Choose your Engine Core" in My ship. They get their draw too.

**The versions, from your 2023 pages** [Source]. Which ordinary version is red and which is light blue is mine [Proposal]. Red is the more lopsided one: one of its numbers is 0, or close to it.

| Part | Red (20%) | Green, the sure one (50%) | Light blue (20%) | Gold, lucky (10%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engine Core | Neutron Reactor 25 HP 0 AP | Standard Reactor 10 / 10 | Quantum Reactor 20 / 5 | Stellar Reactor 30 / 10 |
| Navigation | Astrolabe Navigator 25 / 0 | Standard Navigator 15 / 5 | Celestial Cartographer 15 / 10 | Cosmic Navigator 20 / 15 |
| Shield | Quantum Deflector 35 / 0 | Standard Shield 20 / 0 | Neutron Wall 30 / 5 | Nova Shield 40 / 10 |
| Weapons | Pulsar Phaser 5 / 25 | Standard Arsenal 10 / 15 | Proton Launcher 15 / 15 | Supernova Arsenal 15 / 30 |
| Power | Proton Generator 30 / 0 | Standard Battery 15 / 10 | Quantum Dynamo 25 / 5 | Galaxy Battery 30 / 15 |

**[Conflict] The gamble has no downside in these numbers.** In every part, the Standard version has the **smallest HP + AP** of the four. Engine Core: Standard 20, red 25, light blue 25, gold 40. So a red draw still adds up to more than the sure choice. Two ways to fix it, both yours:
- Lower the red versions so they add up to less than the Standard.
- Keep the numbers, and let red's cost be that it is lopsided: a Neutron Reactor has no attack at all. Whether that hurts depends on your battle rules.

Each version is one line in the game (`PVER`), so new numbers take minutes.

**Maths a child can do here** [Proposal]: "Out of 10 draws, how many give a part better than the Standard?" Or: "The sure part adds to 20. What do the four add to on average?" (27.5, so the draw wins on average.)

## 3. The mission ship's thermometers

[Source: Marc, 2026-11-20] "It said write down the temperature... before I even [knew] why I'm supposed to do that."

- Before Mission Control asks, a thermometer shows itself and its sign, and says: "Nobody has asked you about it yet."
- After, it says why: "Mission Control wants to know how warm each room is."

**Something I found that you did not ask about** [Conflict to check]: about half the rooms that are in range sit **exactly on a limit**, such as 24 on a sign that says "Keep between 18 and 24". A child can read "between" two ways: does 24 count? The game says it does. Options: change the sign to "From 18 to 24", or keep in-range rooms off the limits. Not changed.
