## 1. How to run them

1. **Open the Gemini chat** where you made the Docking Bay.
2. **Before each room's dark picture,** attach your dark Docking Bay picture (picture 1) and paste the box. The picture keeps the style; the words say what is new.
3. **Before each magenta sheet,** attach your sheet of six things on magenta (picture 5).
4. **The lit copy** comes straight after its room. Attach nothing; paste the "Lit" line under the room's box. It redraws the picture just made.
5. **Look at each picture** with the check under its box (and section 7). If it fails, write: "Try again. Keep everything, but ..." and say what to change. Two tries are enough; if it still fails, move on and tell the main thread.
6. **Save it** with its code as the name, for example `R11-dark.png`, `R11-lit.png`, `CV3.png`.
7. **If Gemini starts to drift** (a different style, a different camera), start a new chat. Attach the dark Docking Bay and write: "Match this drawing style, camera and lighting exactly in every picture I ask for." Then go on.

## 2. The style paragraph (it ends every room box)

This is the same in every room. It is already inside each box below. It is here so you can see what it asks for.

> Match the attached Docking Bay picture exactly: the same hand-drawn cartoon style with clean dark outlines and soft painted shading, the same worn, friendly blue-grey metal, and the same camera: seen from the front and a little above, like a stage with its front wall taken away. The back wall runs straight across and meets the floor about 40% of the way down the picture; the two side walls slant in; the floor runs to the bottom edge. Dim amber emergency lamps in oval cages, with soft warm cones of light; everything can still be seen. Wide 16:9 picture. Keep the middle of the floor open, with tall furniture against the walls and a few bare patches of floor. The bottom eighth of the picture is plain floor. Every screen, board, chart, sign, dial and nameplate is blank. No people, no animals, no small loose objects. No text, letters or numbers anywhere.

**Why each part is there** (from building the test page):
- **"40% of the way down":** the cadet's size is worked out from that line. Your Docking Bay is 38%, your Greenhouse 42%.
- **"Tall furniture against the walls":** the game cannot draw the cadet behind painted furniture.
- **"No small loose objects":** everything a child can take is painted on magenta, so the game can move it.
- **"Blank":** each child's numbers differ, so the game draws every number. A painted screen is also far too small to read on an iPad (the Docking Bay's wall screen is about 12 pixels wide).
- **"The bottom eighth plain":** the game's bar covers it.

**A way out to the south:** a picture never shows its near wall. So the boxes ask for "a walkway with yellow-and-black edges running from the middle of the floor straight down off the bottom edge".
