## 3. Two meanings of "off-line"

### 3.1 Does it need the internet?

Less than you might think.

- The sketch is **one file**. It makes every problem, draws every picture and marks every answer on the iPad itself.
- It saves on that iPad. It does not talk to a server.
- So all of today's game would run with no internet once the page is open.

**What will need your server, when built:**

| Part | Why it needs a server |
|---|---|
| The AI that answers unexpected typing | The model runs on your VPS |
| AI-written stories | Same. The story bank is the fallback without it. |
| A student's save on any iPad | Today the save lives on one iPad only |
| Your class view, and traces other students left | Needs one shared store |

**A check that could prove this wrong:** open the sketch, turn off Wi-Fi, and play. Only the two lettering styles should change.

### 3.2 Does it happen away from the screen?

Almost none of it, so far.

- Every minute of what I built is a child looking at an iPad.
- The one off-screen moment is a single sentence on Voltara: "In class you build this with blocks."
- Your own account says the 1.4 grade-level gain came from a version with paper, blocks, cards and teams. [Source: your Edutopia draft]
- You also said the major quests should be things students design in real life. [Source: Marc, 2026-11-16]

So the build is leaning the wrong way on this.
