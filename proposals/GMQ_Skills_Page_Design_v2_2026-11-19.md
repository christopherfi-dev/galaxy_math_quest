## 4. One plain word for quick answers [Proposal]

You asked which is best and which is possible. Both are possible. My pick is one plain word, with the evidence one tap away.

It looks only at **first tries**, and only at the **last 10** of them for that step.

| Word | Rule |
|---|---|
| Not seen | no first tries yet |
| Just started | 1 to 4. Too few to say. |
| Not yet | 5 or more, and half or fewer right |
| Getting there | between the two |
| Secure | 5 or more, 8 in 10 right, on at least 2 different days |

- The quest part gets no word. A quest uses a skill once or twice. It lists what happened.
- Children never see these words. They see cards and credits.
- The numbers 5, 8 in 10 and 2 days are my guesses. They are easy to change.

## 5. How the game pushes a child on [Proposal]

This is new, and it comes from your goal.

- **When a child is Secure on a step, the quick questions for that strand move up to the next step.** A wisp that asked "round 347 to the nearest 10" starts asking "round 3,470 to the nearest 1,000".
- A child who is not secure stays where they are. Nobody is pushed before they are ready. That is your "if necessary".
- Your space hazards already work this way: pass a level, meet the next.
- So the record does two jobs. It **shows** you where each child is. It **tells the game** what to ask next.

**Cost:** each step needs its own question maker, checked on thousands of numbers as before. Most are not written yet. Section 7 says which exist.

## 9. What could prove this wrong

| Claim | Test |
|---|---|
| Moving up on "secure" is fair | A child can be quick at rounding and still not use it in a quest. Watch for a child who is moved up and then stuck. If it happens, moving up should also need one quest use. |
| One word is more use than counts | After your class plays, open a few pages. If you tap through to the evidence every time, the word is not earning its place. |
| The rule for "secure" is right | Find a child you know is secure on rounding, and one who is not. See what the page says about each. |
| First tries are the right thing to count | Watch for wild guessing at wisps. Guessing reads as "not yet" when the truth is "not trying". |
| A strand is the right size for a row | If you need to see 3.NBT.1 apart from 3.NBT.2, a strand is too coarse. |
