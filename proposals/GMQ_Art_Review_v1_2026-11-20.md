## 2. Place by place

### KIS Station
- **Style:** a room seen from above. Dark metal floor plates, thin outlines, small yellow signs.
- **Good:** the ship is big and is clearly the main thing. Crates, doors and arrows are easy to read. It looks like a space station and nothing else.
- **Weak:** it is very dark. The wall blocks repeat. Nothing here says "a mess", though the words do.

### Planet Voltara
- **Style:** a painted view from the side, in layers: rainbow sky, ringed planet, dark city, river, glowing trees, dark grass in front.
- **Good:** this is the best picture in the game. It has depth and light. The dark city under a rainbow tells the story without words.
- **Weak:** it is busy. The "your ship" label sits on a tree. The cadet stands on top of the ship.

### Planet Chronosia
- **Style:** a board of squares seen from above. Each place also has a flat side view.
- **Good:** the labels and the yellow "!" marks are clear. The clocks on the board can be read.
- **Weak:**
  - On landing, about four fifths of the picture is dark blue mist. That is the first thing a child sees.
  - The grass is a checkerboard. The trees are one stamp, set in rows like a fence. The mountains are one triangle, repeated.
  - The buildings are the size of an icon. The Clock Tower is as tall as two squares.
  - The striped sky, the planet's one special thing, is a thin strip at the top.
  - There is an empty green band under the map.
  - The Clock Tower view is flat colour: bands of sky, one zigzag of mountain, plain boxes, and a lot of empty green.

### Planet Xylophora
- **Style:** the same board of squares from above. The arrival screens are bigger, simpler pictures.
- **Good:** the squares belong here, because the squares are the maths. The arrival screens are the best new art: the planet as a patchwork from space, and the sleeping giant.
- **Weak:**
  - On the ground it looks like Chronosia: green squares, a sand path, blue squares, tree stamps round the edge.
  - The patchwork promised from space (pink, gold, orange) is gone when you land. The ground is green.
  - The Site Chief is a small figure the same shape as the cadet.
  - I could not tell what the yellow square with a cross is.

### The mission ship (Operation Stardust)
- **Style:** a floor plan from above. Each room is one flat colour.
- **Good:** room colours tell the rooms apart. The orange doors are clear.
- **Weak:** this is the cheapest-looking place.
  - On arrival more than half the picture is black.
  - Each room is a big empty rectangle with two or three tiny things in it.
  - The walls are one grey block, repeated.
  - Your rocket is parked inside a room, the same size as you.

### Space travel
- **Style:** a big planet with a glow, seen from space.
- **Good:** Voltara in rainbow stripes with clouds is clear and good to look at. A child knows what to tap.
- **Weak:** the other planets in the sky are plain two-colour discs.

### Small worlds (Tatian, Ixlumi)
- **Style:** a side view from a kit: a sky, two moons, one row of far shapes, flat ground, seven things in a line.
- **Good:** very clear for tapping. The sky and ground colours differ from world to world.
- **Weak:** the seven things always stand in one row with even gaps, like stickers on a shelf. The moons are two-colour discs. The worlds differ by colour, not by shape. After three, a child will see the kit.

## 3. Do the planets look like their own places?

**No. The game has three looks, and they do not match each other.**

| Look | Places | How it reads |
|---|---|---|
| A painted view from the side | Voltara. The small worlds are a thinner version. | A place you are standing in |
| A board of squares from above | Chronosia, Xylophora, the mission ship | A game board |
| A detailed room from above | The station | A place, but dark |

- Voltara looks like itself.
- Chronosia and Xylophora look like each other.
- The mission ship looks like neither a ship nor a planet.

## 4. Three ways to draw the planets better

All three are **[Proposal]**. Each has a sample of Chronosia in the samples file. All three stay still, and all are drawn in code.

### Choice 1. Dress the squares
- **What:** the same map and the same walking. New paint only: evening light, long shadows, a river that shows the striped sky, buildings that stand up, trees that are not all alike.
- **Cost:** the smallest. About one working session for each planet. No test changes, because nothing moves.
- **Risk:** it is still a board. Two green planets still look like cousins unless each gets its own ground and light.

### Choice 2. A big view when you arrive
- **What:** one painted view from the ground for each planet, like Voltara. A child sees it on arrival and again at each place. The walking map stays.
- **Cost:** middle. One big picture for each planet, then a smaller one for each place. This is the same picture your arrival screens need (job A).
- **Risk:** the map between the views is still the plain board. The jump between the two looks will show.

### Choice 3. A painted map with no squares showing
- **What:** the map becomes one picture. The river bends, the path winds, trees grow in clumps. The squares stay underneath for walking.
- **Cost:** the largest. Each map is placed by hand. The walking and tapping tests must be checked again. Xylophora cannot use it: there the squares are the maths.
- **Risk:** a child may not know where they can walk.
- **One thing in the sample is mine, not yours:** a ring of twelve standing stones, like a clock face. It can come out.

**Choices 1 and 2 can be done together. Choice 3 replaces choice 1.**

## 5. What could prove this wrong

- **The children may like the board.** It looks like games they know. Ask them which place they liked the look of, before any repainting.
- **Small fingers.** Bigger buildings cover more squares. Test the smallest iPad: can a child still tap the square beside the Clock Tower?
- **Speed.** More shapes take longer to draw. The background can be painted once and kept, but this must be timed on an old iPad, not guessed.
- **The mist.** A better map still starts four fifths hidden. If mist is the real trouble on arrival, paint will not fix it.
- **My eye is one eye.** I judged from pictures on a server. You see it on the board in your room.
