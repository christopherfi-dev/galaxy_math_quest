## 2. The plan [Proposal]

- **The four in the harbour each get their own pictures.** They stand side by side in one scene. The same body four times, with different hats, would look like copies. (For the Menders, one ordinary Mender became the whole crowd. That works on the map, where they stand far apart.)
- **The guard, the three speakers and the child share two pictures,** calm and happy. Each stands alone on the map, so two faces are enough there.
- **Seven pictures in all,** in two rounds. Round 1 is the harbour (5 pictures). Round 2 is the map (2 pictures).
- **The same Gemini chat as the Menders,** so the style stays the same.

**Changed from the Mender prompts, and why:**
1. **Fins, not hands.** The game says "a fin at each side". A fin has no fingers, so the finger fault from the Menders (four fingers in a wave) cannot happen. A fin can curl round a thing to hold it, like a mitten.
2. **"Not see-through."** A body like glass or water would let the green show through, and the cut-out would fail. It also keeps them away from the water people of a well-known film.
3. **"No beak, no feathers."** A blue body with flipper arms and orange feet can drift towards a famous cartoon penguin. Every character must be original.
4. **"No turquoise or teal."** Blue cuts away cleanly from the green background. Turquoise is close to green, and would leave holes.
5. **The game's blues, made a little bluer.** Two of the game's colours (the baker's and the net weaver's) are close to teal. The prompts ask for a clear blue near them.

```
NEW PEOPLE, SAME WORLD. Keep exactly the same drawing style as the Menders in the pictures above: the same clean, flat 2D cartoon, the same bold dark navy outline of even thickness (about #0E1626), the same flat colours with one darker shade and one small white shine on the upper left, light from the upper left. THE RILLFOLK are the river people of the same planet. They live in wooden houses on legs over the water, and they sail rafts. They are calm, kind, busy folk who love to sing.

EVERY RILLFOLK HAS THIS BODY:
- the shape of a drop of water: a rounded point at the very top, widening to a full, round bottom. About one and a half times as tall as it is wide. There is no separate head and no neck: the face is on the upper front of the drop.

- arms: one fin on each side, like a fish's side fin, in a darker blue than the body, joined to the body a little below the middle. A fin has NO fingers, NO hand and NO thumb, ever. A fin can bend and curl round a thing to hold it, like a mitten.

THE HARBOUR KEEPER: she runs the harbour. She is the busy, kind boss of all the rafts. She is a little taller than most Rillfolk.
- body: a strong sea blue (about #2F7FC0), with a darker blue in shadow (about #1F5E94)
- belly patch: pale blue (about #A9CFEA); fins: dark blue (about #24639A)
```

## 5. What could prove this wrong

- **A blue drop with fins and orange feet may still look like a famous character.** Look at Round 1 before Round 2, and ask one other adult: "Does this remind you of anyone?"
- **Gemini may give the fins fingers anyway,** the way it gave the elder a fifth finger in a wave.
- **The harbour may look crowded.** Four pictured folk, the oven, the net and the loading board in one scene. I will check it at iPad sideways, upright and phone size, and say if anyone must stand elsewhere.
- **Seven pictures in one long chat may drift** (the cap's colour, the sash's side). Each picture will be checked against picture 12.
- **The page grows** by perhaps 250 to 350 KB (my estimate: the Menders' pictures are about 13 KB a frame, and these are 27 frames, the map's smaller). I will measure it on a phone.
