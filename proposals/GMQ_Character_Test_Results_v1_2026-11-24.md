## 1. The verdict

**Gemini kept him the same across all four pictures.** Marc was right; my guess of "5 to 15 tries" for a good picture was too pessimistic, at least for one character in one chat.

## 2. What is right

Checked against the prompts and against the game's own words ("An old Mender with a white beard. His lid has a dent in it." [Built]):
- the copper pot body, the dark band, the two handles, the brown feet
- the lid with its gold knob, and a dent on the lid (pictures 1 to 3)
- the white beard and bushy eyebrows, the big round eyes
- the colours and the drawing: flat, a dark outline, a shine on the upper left
- plain white backgrounds; **no words, letters or numbers anywhere**, not even on the board
- picture 2: all four feelings as asked
- picture 3: the board shows a box with a funnel, a lever and a chute, as asked
- picture 4: two suns (one big and yellow, one small and white); three round huts with cream walls, round doors and copper domes; the middle and right left open. The huts even have gold knobs on their domes, as the game's own drawn huts do.

## 3. Faults, found by zooming in

1. **He has handles AND separate arms.** I asked for the handles to be his arms, as the game's drawn Menders have it. Gemini added arms below the handles. **Your call:** keep the arms (they hold things better), or ask again.
2. **His fingers change.** Three fingers and a thumb in picture 1; four fingers and a thumb when he waves in picture 2.
3. **Picture 3's pouch is doubled:** an open pouch in his hand, and a tied sack hanging below it. **My fault:** I asked for a pouch "tied at the top" with beads "peeking out". Those do not fit together.
4. **Picture 4:**
   - he is too big: a hut is about 1.4 times his height, not 3 times
   - the dent moved to the other side of his lid
5. **The dent looks like a shiny oval.** At game size it almost disappears.

## 5. What it means [Proposal]

- **One character, in one chat: it works.** The pictures are good enough for the game, with a thicker outline added in code.
- **The real question is the rest of the cast.** If Elder Thimble is painted, the other Menders (Pim, the gardener, the fisher and more) stand beside him drawn in code, and look plainer. Choices:
  - **Named characters only** are painted (the elder, the harbour keeper, the Keeper on Chronosia). The crowd stays drawn. Cheapest; the named ones stand out, which may even help.
  - **Every person** is painted. One look everywhere, but many more pictures.
- **Gemini's scene (picture 4)** is softer and paler than the game's scenes. I would not use Gemini scenes as backgrounds until the style is matched, or the game would have two looks.
