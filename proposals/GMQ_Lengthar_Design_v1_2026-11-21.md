## The standards it carries

- **3.MD.4:** measure lengths to the nearest half and quarter inch, and show them on a line plot.
- **3.NF.3:** on a ruler, 2/4 inch and 1/2 inch are the same mark.
- **3.MD.2** (grams and kilograms) waits for Planet Massar.
- **[Check first]** the AERO wording of 3.MD.4. The Common Core says "halves and fourths of an inch", which is why the ruler is in inches. The AERO document was kept out of this folder; if its wording differs, the unit changes before the build.

## The story

The anomaly has made Lengthar's things the wrong length. The Measuronians cannot fix them, because their own rulers were scrambled too: every mark is still there, but the numbers fell off. The cadet brings a working ruler (in the picture) and the Unit Alignment Matrix is fed true lengths again, one place at a time. The Chaos Guardian feeds on careless measuring; it weakens each time a length is exact.

**Nothing gives the answer away:** the ruler has marks, not a running read-out. Lengths are chosen so that no object ends exactly halfway between two quarter marks.

## What has to be true before it is built, and checked on thousands of cadets

- Every object's length is a whole number of quarter inches, from 1 to 6 inches, and is drawn at that length on every screen.
- The ruler scales with the screen, so "inches" are picture inches. A real ruler held to an iPad will not match, and the game says so (decision 37: "a ruler in the picture").
- No two readings: no length sits between two marks.
- The line plot's question always has one answer.
