# Gemini prompts for Stardust (Tally Station): the list for Marc

**Date:** 28 November 2026. **From:** the main thread. **For:** Marc, to run in Google Gemini.
The painted rooms thread's prompt book (`proposals/GMQ_Stardust_Prompts_v1_2026-11-28.md`, when it lands) replaces this list. Everything here is **[Proposal]**.

## How to run them

- **Use the same Gemini chat as your tests.** It remembers the style. If you must start a new chat, first attach your Docking Bay picture (the dark one) and write: "Match this drawing style, view angle and lighting exactly."
- **One prompt at a time.** Copy a grey box, paste it, wait for the picture, save it.
- **Name each picture by its code** (for example `R03-dark`), or say the code when you send it.
- **Send them to the main thread** by attaching them in its chat, a few at a time. It files them.
- **Check each picture for:** the ways out in the right places; open floor to walk on; no words or numbers; a room that looks different from the others.
- **Ways out to the south:** a picture never shows its near wall, so a way out to the south is the floor running off the bottom edge of the picture. Every prompt below says so where it is needed.
- **Boards, screens, charts, signs and gauges stay blank.** The game draws each child's own numbers and words on them.

**Batch 1 (tonight or tomorrow, about 10 minutes):** C1, C2, C3, R15 (the Hangar) and R11 (the Entry Hall), each with its lit copy (L).
**Batch 2 (when you have time, about 40 minutes):** the other rooms, each with its lit copy, then T1 and C4.
Your Docking Bay (dark and lit) already fits the map. Keep it.

## Characters and things (on magenta, to cut out)

**C1. Cadets to choose from (front views).**
```
In the same drawing style, on a plain flat bright magenta background, draw eight different child space cadets standing in a row, seen from the front, spread out and not touching: girls and boys, with different skin colours and different hair, all in the same white and orange suit with the helmet on and the face visible through the glass. All the same height. No shadows on the background. No text, letters or numbers.
```

**C2. The cleaning robot, awake, from four sides.**
```
In the same drawing style, on the same plain flat bright magenta background, draw the small round cleaning robot from before, now awake: its screen shows two happy open eyes, and a small light on its antenna is on. Draw it four times, spread out and not touching: from the front, from the back, from the left side and from the right side. Exactly the same robot each time. No shadows on the background. No text, letters or numbers.
```

**C3. Small things to find and carry.**
```
In the same drawing style, on the same plain flat bright magenta background, draw these eight things separately, spread out and not touching: a green watering can; a small paper packet of seeds with a picture of an apple and no words; a cloth bag of dried peas; a metal mug; a padlock; a clipboard with blank paper; a small box of spare machine parts; a folded blanket. No shadows on the background. No text, letters or numbers.
```

**C4. The Supply Chief (only if you keep her, Stardust decision 3).**
```
In the same drawing style, on the same plain flat bright magenta background, draw one grown-up woman, the station's Supply Chief: friendly, a little tired, in a blue station jumpsuit with many pockets, a tool belt and a clipboard. Draw her five times, spread out and not touching: standing from the front, from the back, from the left side, from the right side, and sitting cross-legged on a nest of blankets. Exactly the same person each time. No shadows on the background. No text, letters or numbers.
```

**T1. The view of each room on the map (optional).** Skip this one; the game can make the map from the room pictures.
